# Inputs

## Request or proposal context

* Request ID and title.
* Description of the proposed initiative, decision, change, procurement, technology, research activity, data use, policy, or operational action.
* Requesting individual, team, and organizational unit.
* Executive sponsor.
* Business or operational owner.
* Purpose and expected outcome.
* Scope and exclusions.
* Current phase.
* Desired implementation or decision date.
* Urgency and consequence of delay.
* Source document and submission date.

## Request classification

* Request type:

  * Strategic initiative.
  * Technology acquisition or implementation.
  * AI or automated-system use.
  * Data access or data-sharing request.
  * Research activity.
  * Clinical or operational change.
  * Policy or procedure change.
  * Vendor or procurement request.
  * Contract or partnership.
  * Financial or investment decision.
  * Workforce or organizational change.
  * Public or external communication.
  * Security, privacy, legal, or regulatory exception.
* Whether the request is new, a renewal, an expansion, a pilot, or a material change.
* Whether the request affects production systems.
* Whether the request involves institutional, personal, confidential, clinical, research, financial, personnel, or regulated data.
* Whether an existing approval pathway or precedent applies.

## Business and strategic information

* Strategic objective supported.
* Business need or problem addressed.
* Expected value and benefits.
* Intended users or beneficiaries.
* Success measures.
* Alternatives considered.
* Cost of delay or inaction.
* Executive or board commitments.
* Funding and resource requirements.
* Relevant initiative charter or business case.

## Technology and architecture information

When applicable:

* Proposed platform, system, application, model, or service.
* Hosting and deployment environment.
* Production, test, development, or demonstration status.
* Architecture and integration design.
* Identity and access model.
* Data flows.
* Storage and retention.
* External connections.
* APIs and dependencies.
* Vendor or third-party components.
* Operational support model.
* Business-continuity and retirement plans.
* Required architecture or technology review.

## Data information

* Data sources.
* Data owners and stewards.
* Data classifications.
* Personal, clinical, research, financial, workforce, student, proprietary, or confidential information involved.
* Purpose and legal or institutional basis for use.
* Minimum data required.
* Access model.
* Data-sharing parties.
* Data location.
* Retention and deletion requirements.
* Records-management requirements.
* Data-quality requirements.
* De-identification or synthetic-data use.
* Prohibited or out-of-scope data.
* Required privacy, security, research, or data-governance review.

## Risk and assurance information

* Strategic, operational, financial, technology, cybersecurity, privacy, legal, regulatory, clinical, research, workforce, ethical, accessibility, reputational, or continuity risks.
* Risk owner.
* Inherent and residual risk.
* Existing safeguards.
* Proposed mitigation.
* Testing and evaluation plan.
* Monitoring and logging.
* Human-review requirements.
* Incident and escalation process.
* Required specialized assessment.

## Vendor, contract, and procurement information

When applicable:

* Vendor and proposed service.
* Procurement method.
* Contract status.
* Funding source.
* Estimated cost and term.
* Data-processing role.
* Subcontractors.
* Hosting location.
* Security and privacy documentation.
* Insurance and indemnification requirements.
* Accessibility documentation.
* Service levels.
* Exit and data-return provisions.
* Required procurement, legal, finance, risk, or vendor-management review.

## Governance and policy information

* Applicable institutional policies.
* Laws, regulations, standards, contracts, or grant requirements.
* Governance committees or decision bodies.
* Delegation-of-authority rules.
* Approval thresholds.
* Required consultations.
* Required notifications.
* Required public, board, labor, community, or stakeholder processes.
* Existing exceptions.
* Relevant precedent decisions.
* Effective dates and policy versions.

## Approval-history information

* Prior submissions.
* Reviews completed.
* Reviewer comments.
* Conditions imposed.
* Decisions made.
* Approval, rejection, deferral, or withdrawal status.
* Decision-maker.
* Decision date.
* Expiration or renewal date.
* Changes since approval.
* Outstanding conditions.
* Supporting evidence.

## Timeline and dependency information

* Required decision date.
* Proposed launch or implementation date.
* Review lead times.
* Sequence requirements.
* Dependencies among approvals.
* Materials required before each review.
* Meetings and governance calendars.
* Procurement or contracting lead time.
* Remediation or resubmission time.
* Consequence of missing a review window.

## Data-quality inputs

* Source owner.
* Last validation date.
* Missing documents.
* Conflicting pathway information.
* Policies of uncertain applicability.
* Outdated approval records.
* Unverified decision authority.
* Confidence level.
* Items requiring human or specialized confirmation.
