# Outputs

## Approval-pathway summary

* Request title and classification.
* Requested outcome.
* Current phase.
* Recommended approval pathway.
* Required reviews and approvals.
* Current pathway status.
* Next required action.
* Owner.
* Target date.
* Material blockers.
* Confidence and limitations.

## Approval pathway map

Show the recommended sequence:

Request intake
→ Classification
→ Required consultation
→ Specialized reviews
→ Remediation or conditions
→ Authorized decision
→ Implementation authorization
→ Ongoing monitoring
→ Renewal, material-change review, or retirement

For each stage:

* Purpose.
* Responsible owner.
* Reviewer or decision body.
* Required inputs.
* Entry criteria.
* Exit criteria.
* Expected lead time.
* Dependencies.
* Current status.
* Required evidence.
* Escalation path.

## Required reviews

Identify whether the request may require:

* Executive sponsor review.
* Business-owner approval.
* Architecture review.
* Information-security review.
* Privacy review.
* Data-governance or data-owner approval.
* Clinical review.
* Research or human-subjects review.
* Legal review.
* Regulatory or compliance review.
* Procurement and contracting review.
* Finance or budget approval.
* Accessibility review.
* Records-management review.
* Policy review.
* Workforce, labor, or HR review.
* Communications review.
* Ethics, responsible-AI, or model-governance review.
* Board or other formal governance review.

For each review:

* Reason it appears applicable.
* Authoritative source.
* Reviewer or decision body.
* Required materials.
* Timing.
* Dependencies.
* Current status.
* Confidence.
* Need for human confirmation.

## Approval matrix

For every required decision:

* Approval or review name.
* Decision authority.
* Responsible preparer.
* Required consultees.
* Required informed parties.
* Decision criteria.
* Evidence required.
* Target date.
* Current status.
* Conditions.
* Expiration or renewal requirement.
* Source supporting the assignment.

## Submission checklist

* Business case or charter.
* Scope and requirements.
* Architecture and data-flow documentation.
* Data classification and ownership.
* Security and privacy assessment.
* Risk assessment.
* Testing and evaluation plan.
* Human-review and escalation plan.
* Procurement and contract materials.
* Financial approval.
* Accessibility documentation.
* Operational support and continuity plan.
* Records-retention plan.
* Communication and change plan.
* Required specialized documentation.
* Missing or incomplete materials.

## Sequence and timeline

* Reviews that may occur in parallel.
* Reviews that must occur sequentially.
* Earliest feasible decision date.
* Critical-path review.
* Governance meeting dates.
* Required submission dates.
* Expected remediation periods.
* Approval expiration or renewal date.
* Consequence of delay.

## Conditions and obligations

* Conditions required before implementation.
* Conditions required before production use.
* Required safeguards.
* Usage restrictions.
* Required monitoring and reporting.
* Human-review requirements.
* Incident and escalation obligations.
* Renewal requirements.
* Material changes that trigger re-review.
* Required evidence of compliance.

## Approval record

* Review completed.
* Decision.
* Decision-maker.
* Decision date.
* Supporting record.
* Conditions.
* Owner responsible for each condition.
* Due date.
* Expiration date.
* Changes since the decision.
* Current compliance status.

## Gaps and conflicts

* Missing decision authority.
* Conflicting policies or pathways.
* Duplicate reviews.
* Required review omitted from the proposed plan.
* Review requested without a documented basis.
* Missing documents.
* Unresolved conditions.
* Approval represented as broader than documented.
* Material changes that may invalidate a previous approval.

## Recommended next actions

For each action:

* Action.
* Owner.
* Reviewer or decision-maker.
* Required evidence.
* Target date.
* Dependency.
* Escalation trigger.

## Executive attention

* Approval or authority conflict.
* Material risk requiring acceptance.
* Unresolved cross-organizational issue.
* Resource or funding decision.
* Exception request.
* Decision required to maintain the timeline.
* Required decision-maker and deadline.

## Confidence and source report

* Policies and authoritative records reviewed.
* Policy versions and effective dates.
* Assumptions.
* Unconfirmed pathway elements.
* Stale or conflicting sources.
* Questions requiring legal, policy, governance, or specialized interpretation.
