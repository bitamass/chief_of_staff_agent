- Conflicting dates.
- Events recorded out of sequence.
- Decisions dated after dependent actions began.
- Approvals missing before implementation.
- Milestones marked complete before criteria were met.
- Forecast dates earlier than required predecessor dates.
- Due dates without adequate lead time.
- Duplicate events.
- Missing sources.
- Time-zone inconsistencies.
