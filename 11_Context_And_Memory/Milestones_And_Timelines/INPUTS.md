# Inputs

## Timeline scope

- Organization, portfolio, program, initiative, project, decision, policy, or operational area.
- Timeline purpose.
- Intended audience.
- Start and end dates.
- Reporting cutoff date.
- Level of detail required.
- Executive sponsor.
- Accountable owner.
- Source documents.
- Items explicitly out of scope.

## Timeline events

For each event:

- Event ID.
- Event title.
- Event date or date range.
- Event type:
  - Meeting.
  - Decision.
  - Approval.
  - Commitment.
  - Action.
  - Deliverable.
  - Milestone.
  - Launch or implementation.
  - Policy or regulatory change.
  - Organizational change.
  - Risk or issue.
  - Escalation.
  - Funding or procurement.
  - Vendor or partner event.
  - External development.
- Description.
- Initiative or objective affected.
- Owner.
- Participants.
- Source.
- Confidence level.
- Whether the date is actual, planned, forecast, estimated, or unknown.

## Milestone definition

For each milestone:

- Milestone ID and title.
- Description.
- Initiative or workstream.
- Milestone type.
- Owner.
- Accountable approver.
- Baseline date.
- Approved revised date.
- Current forecast date.
- Actual completion date.
- Status.
- Critical-path designation.
- Completion criteria.
- Acceptance criteria.
- Required evidence.
- Source and last update.

## Historical meeting information

- Meeting ID and title.
- Date and time.
- Purpose.
- Participants and roles.
- Agenda.
- Discussion summary.
- Decisions made.
- Actions assigned.
- Commitments.
- Risks or issues raised.
- Follow-up meeting.
- Source notes, minutes, transcript, or recording.
- Approval status of the record.

## Decision information

- Decision ID.
- Decision statement.
- Decision date.
- Decision-maker or body.
- Options considered.
- Rationale.
- Conditions.
- Effective date.
- Initiative or milestone affected.
- Actions created.
- Superseded decision.
- Required review or expiration date.
- Source and approval status.

## Action and commitment information

- Action or commitment ID.
- Description.
- Source event.
- Owner.
- Assignee.
- Date assigned.
- Original due date.
- Current due date.
- Completion date.
- Status.
- Outcome.
- Related milestone.
- Related decision.
- Evidence of completion.

## Deliverable information

- Deliverable ID and name.
- Initiative.
- Owner and approver.
- Planned date.
- Forecast date.
- Actual date.
- Status.
- Acceptance status.
- Related milestone.
- Dependencies.
- Source and evidence.

## Dependency and sequencing information

- Predecessor event, milestone, or deliverable.
- Successor event, milestone, or deliverable.
- Required sequence.
- Lead or lag time.
- Dependency owner.
- Required input.
- Required-by date.
- Current forecast.
- Acceptance status.
- Effect of delay.
- Workaround.

## Risk and issue timeline information

- Risk or issue ID.
- Date identified.
- Owner.
- Status changes.
- Rating or severity changes.
- Mitigation or resolution events.
- Escalation dates.
- Conversion of risk into issue.
- Closure date.
- Related milestones and decisions.
- Source.

## Strategy and organizational context

- Strategic priorities.
- Initiative charters.
- Organizational changes.
- Leadership changes.
- Policy or governance changes.
- Funding changes.
- Relevant external events.
- Date each change became effective.
- Initiatives, decisions, or milestones affected.

## Future planning information

- Upcoming meetings.
- Planned decisions.
- Required approvals.
- Expected milestones.
- Deliverables due.
- Actions and commitments due.
- Governance reviews.
- External deadlines.
- Risk triggers.
- Contingency dates.
- Planned communications.
- Forecast confidence.

## Calendar and date information

- Working-day calendar.
- Holidays and closure periods.
- Fiscal-year periods.
- Academic, clinical, regulatory, or governance calendars.
- Board and committee schedules.
- Reporting cycles.
- Time zones.
- Date-format requirements.
- Rules for approximate or unknown dates.

## Version and change information

- Original date or plan.
- Revised date.
- Reason for revision.
- Person authorizing the change.
- Date changed.
- Previous status.
- Current status.
- Related decision.
- Effect on downstream events.
- Source and version.

## Data-quality inputs

- Missing dates.
- Conflicting dates.
- Events without sources.
- Planned dates represented as actual.
- Completed milestones without evidence.
- Duplicate events.
- Events in the wrong sequence.
- Time-zone inconsistencies.
- Stale forecasts.
- Confidence level.
- Items requiring human validation.
