# Inputs

## Assumption context

- Initiative, program, project, decision, analysis, plan, forecast, or recommendation.
- Purpose of the work.
- Strategic objective or expected outcome.
- Scope and exclusions.
- Executive sponsor.
- Accountable owner.
- Current phase.
- Reporting period and cutoff date.
- Decision or milestone affected.
- Source documents and previous analyses.

## Assumption record

For each assumption:

- Assumption ID.
- Clear assumption statement.
- Initiative, decision, deliverable, forecast, or objective affected.
- Assumption category:
  - Strategic.
  - Business.
  - Financial.
  - Operational.
  - Clinical or research.
  - Technical or architecture.
  - Data.
  - Security or privacy.
  - Legal, regulatory, or policy.
  - Workforce or resource.
  - Vendor or partner.
  - Schedule.
  - Stakeholder.
  - Adoption or change management.
  - Market or external environment.
- Person or role who introduced the assumption.
- Assumption owner.
- Date recorded.
- Source and supporting context.
- Current status.
- Last review date.

## Basis and evidence

For each assumption:

- Evidence supporting the assumption.
- Source of the evidence.
- Source owner.
- Publication or reporting date.
- Whether the evidence is primary or secondary.
- Historical information.
- Comparable examples.
- Expert judgment.
- Stakeholder input.
- Data-quality limitations.
- Conflicting evidence.
- Confidence level.
- Reason the assumption is necessary.

## Validation information

- Validation method.
- Evidence required for confirmation.
- Person or function responsible for validation.
- Validation deadline.
- Relevant milestone or decision date.
- Required review or approval.
- Current validation status.
- Result of validation.
- Date validated.
- Conditions that would invalidate the assumption.
- Next review date.

## Impact information

- Decisions relying on the assumption.
- Deliverables relying on the assumption.
- Milestones relying on the assumption.
- Financial forecasts relying on the assumption.
- Resource plans relying on the assumption.
- Risks and mitigations relying on the assumption.
- Stakeholders affected.
- Potential impact if the assumption is incorrect.
- Time available to respond.
- Reversibility.
- Alternative or contingency approach.

## Dependency and relationship information

- Related assumptions.
- Assumptions that depend on this assumption.
- Risks connected to the assumption.
- Issues that may invalidate it.
- Decisions supported by it.
- Initiatives or workstreams affected.
- Upstream evidence required.
- Downstream consequences.
- Duplicate, overlapping, or conflicting assumptions.

## Risk information

- Probability that the assumption may prove incorrect.
- Impact if incorrect.
- Exposure created by relying on it.
- Early-warning indicators.
- Trigger thresholds.
- Risk owner.
- Mitigation.
- Contingency plan.
- Escalation status.
- Residual exposure.
- Risk-acceptance authority.

## Change history

- Original wording.
- Revised wording.
- Reason for revision.
- Date changed.
- Person authorizing the change.
- Changes in evidence.
- Changes in confidence.
- Changes in affected decisions or plans.
- Previous validation results.
- Previous escalation or contingency action.

## Governance and sensitivity inputs

- Applicable policies, standards, contracts, or regulatory requirements.
- Required legal, clinical, research, security, privacy, finance, procurement, architecture, or governance review.
- Confidentiality classification.
- Information-sharing restrictions.
- Required approval for continued reliance.
- Retention and records-management requirements.

## Data-quality inputs

- Missing owners.
- Missing validation dates.
- Unsupported assumptions.
- Stale assumptions.
- Conflicting assumptions.
- Assumptions presented as facts.
- Assumptions no longer relevant.
- Low-confidence evidence.
- Items requiring specialized or human validation.
