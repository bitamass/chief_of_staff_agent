# Outputs

## Assumption register

For each assumption:

- Assumption ID.
- Assumption statement.
- Category.
- Initiative, decision, deliverable, or forecast affected.
- Assumption owner.
- Date recorded.
- Current status.
- Supporting evidence.
- Confidence level.
- Validation method.
- Validation owner.
- Validation deadline.
- Impact if incorrect.
- Related risks and dependencies.
- Next review date.
- Source references.

## Assumption classification

Each assumption should be classified as:

- Proposed.
- Unvalidated.
- Partially supported.
- Supported.
- Validated.
- Challenged.
- Invalidated.
- Replaced.
- Expired.
- No longer relevant.
- Insufficient evidence.

The output must explain the evidence supporting the classification.

## Critical-assumption summary

Identify assumptions that:

- Support a major executive decision.
- Affect strategic outcomes.
- Affect a critical milestone.
- Affect a material financial forecast.
- Affect security, privacy, clinical, legal, regulatory, or operational obligations.
- Are relied upon by multiple initiatives.
- Have high impact if incorrect.
- Have low confidence.
- Must be validated soon.
- Lack a responsible owner.

For each critical assumption:

- Why it matters.
- Current evidence.
- Confidence.
- Validation action.
- Owner.
- Required date.
- Contingency.
- Escalation requirement.

## Evidence and confidence assessment

For each material assumption:

- Evidence supporting it.
- Evidence contradicting it.
- Source quality.
- Source recency.
- Degree of corroboration.
- Known limitations.
- Confidence rating.
- Explanation of the confidence rating.
- Information required to improve confidence.

## Validation plan

For each unvalidated or challenged assumption:

- Validation question.
- Validation method.
- Evidence required.
- Responsible owner.
- Required reviewer.
- Target date.
- Cost or effort.
- Dependency.
- Decision or milestone affected.
- Expected outcome.
- Escalation trigger.

## Assumption-to-decision traceability

Show the relationship:

Assumption  
→ Supporting evidence  
→ Decision or recommendation  
→ Initiative or deliverable  
→ Expected outcome  
→ Risk if incorrect

For each relationship:

- Source.
- Owner.
- Confidence.
- Materiality.
- Validation status.
- Recommended action.

## Assumption-risk conversion

Identify assumptions that should be converted into risks because:

- The assumption has significant uncertainty.
- The impact of being wrong is material.
- Validation cannot occur before a required decision.
- Early-warning indicators can be monitored.
- Mitigation or contingency planning is necessary.

For each recommended conversion:

- Assumption.
- Proposed risk statement.
- Probability.
- Impact.
- Risk owner.
- Mitigation.
- Trigger.
- Review date.

## Conflicts and inconsistencies

Identify:

- Assumptions that contradict one another.
- Different teams using different assumptions.
- Assumptions inconsistent with approved strategy or policy.
- Assumptions inconsistent with current evidence.
- Forecasts based on different timeframes or definitions.
- Duplicate assumptions.
- Facts incorrectly labeled as assumptions.
- Assumptions incorrectly presented as facts.

## Change and trend report

- New assumptions.
- Validated assumptions.
- Invalidated assumptions.
- Changed confidence.
- Revised wording.
- Assumptions approaching expiration.
- Assumptions overdue for review.
- Decisions affected by assumption changes.
- Contingencies activated.

## Executive attention

- Assumptions requiring acceptance before a decision.
- Critical assumptions with weak evidence.
- Conflicting assumptions affecting multiple initiatives.
- Assumptions requiring specialized review.
- Decisions that should be delayed pending validation.
- Decisions that must proceed with explicit conditions.
- Contingencies requiring approval.
- Required decision-maker and deadline.

## Data-quality summary

- Unsupported assumptions.
- Missing owners or validation dates.
- Stale evidence.
- Conflicting sources.
- Low-confidence conclusions.
- Assumptions no longer connected to active work.
- Items requiring human confirmation.
