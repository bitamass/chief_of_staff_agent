# Inputs

## Organizational scope

- Organization, institution, division, department, business unit, program, portfolio, or operational area.
- Purpose of the organizational-structure analysis.
- Intended audience.
- Effective date.
- Reporting cutoff date.
- Executive sponsor.
- Accountable organizational owner.
- Geographic, functional, or legal scope.
- Items explicitly out of scope.
- Authoritative source documents.

## Organizational units

For each organizational unit:

- Unit ID and name.
- Unit type:
  - Institution.
  - Division.
  - Department.
  - Office.
  - Program.
  - Portfolio.
  - Service line.
  - Center.
  - Committee or governance body.
  - Shared service.
  - Subsidiary or affiliated organization.
- Parent unit.
- Subordinate units.
- Unit leader.
- Leader’s title.
- Mission or purpose.
- Primary responsibilities.
- Geographic or functional scope.
- Effective date.
- Status: active, planned, transitional, consolidated, or retired.
- Authoritative source.

## Positions and roles

For each relevant position or role:

- Position or role ID.
- Position title.
- Incumbent, when appropriate and authorized.
- Organizational unit.
- Reports-to role.
- Direct reports.
- Functional or matrix relationships.
- Acting, interim, delegated, or permanent status.
- Effective and expiration dates.
- Primary responsibilities.
- Decision authority.
- Budget authority.
- Operational authority.
- Required consultations.
- Delegated authority.
- Backup or succession role.
- Source of authority.

## Leadership structure

- Executive leadership roles.
- Senior leadership team.
- Business, operational, clinical, research, technical, financial, administrative, or governance leaders.
- Reporting relationships.
- Shared or joint leadership arrangements.
- Interim and acting appointments.
- Leadership committees.
- Decision responsibilities.
- Escalation responsibilities.
- Effective dates and authoritative sources.

## Governance bodies

For each board, council, committee, steering group, advisory body, or working group:

- Name.
- Purpose.
- Sponsoring authority.
- Chair.
- Membership.
- Charter.
- Decision, approval, recommendation, advisory, or oversight role.
- Scope.
- Quorum and voting requirements.
- Meeting cadence.
- Reporting destination.
- Escalation authority.
- Recordkeeping requirements.
- Effective date and charter version.

## Reporting relationships

For each relationship:

- Source role or unit.
- Recipient role or unit.
- Relationship type:
  - Direct managerial.
  - Functional.
  - Matrix.
  - Programmatic.
  - Governance.
  - Financial.
  - Operational.
  - Advisory.
  - Oversight.
  - Contractual.
  - Informational.
- Responsibilities covered.
- Decision or escalation authority.
- Reporting cadence.
- Effective date.
- Authoritative source.

## Decision rights

For each material decision category:

- Decision type.
- Responsible preparer.
- Required consultees.
- Recommending authority.
- Reviewing authority.
- Approving authority.
- Implementation authority.
- Risk-acceptance authority.
- Financial threshold.
- Delegation conditions.
- Escalation path.
- Source policy, charter, or delegation document.
- Effective date and version.

## Functional responsibilities

For each unit or role:

- Core responsibilities.
- Services provided.
- Stakeholders served.
- Systems or processes owned.
- Data owned or stewarded.
- Policies owned.
- Budget or resources managed.
- Risks owned.
- Initiatives sponsored or delivered.
- External obligations.
- Shared responsibilities.
- Areas of overlap with other units.

## Program and initiative relationships

- Initiative or program.
- Executive sponsor.
- Accountable owner.
- Program manager.
- Participating units.
- Shared services.
- Governance body.
- Resource providers.
- Decision and approval authorities.
- Operational owner after implementation.
- Temporary organizational arrangements.
- End date or transition plan.

## Shared-service relationships

- Shared-service provider.
- Receiving units.
- Service provided.
- Service owner.
- Intake process.
- Priority-setting process.
- Funding model.
- Service-level expectation.
- Escalation route.
- Capacity limitations.
- Decision authority.
- Source agreement or policy.

## External and affiliated relationships

When relevant:

- Parent or affiliated organization.
- Legal relationship.
- Partnership.
- Vendor or contractor.
- Regulatory or accrediting body.
- Funder or sponsor.
- Governing authority.
- Responsibilities.
- Reporting obligations.
- Decision rights.
- Contract or agreement.
- Effective and expiration dates.

## Organizational change information

- Current-state structure.
- Approved future-state structure.
- Change decision.
- Effective date.
- Units created, consolidated, transferred, renamed, or retired.
- Roles affected.
- Responsibilities transferred.
- Interim reporting relationships.
- Transitional leadership.
- Open organizational-design decisions.
- Communication and implementation plan.
- Source approval.

## Workforce and capacity context

When authorized and relevant:

- Role counts.
- Vacancies.
- Critical capabilities.
- Shared or constrained resources.
- Contractor or vendor reliance.
- Key-person dependencies.
- Succession or backup coverage.
- Organizational capacity concerns.
- Skills or responsibilities without adequate coverage.

Do not include unnecessary personal or protected information.

## Historical context

- Previous organizational structures.
- Prior reorganizations.
- Dates of change.
- Responsibilities transferred.
- Units renamed or retired.
- Historical reporting relationships.
- Decisions that established the current structure.
- Superseded documents.
- Lessons or unresolved issues.

## Data-quality inputs

- Authoritative source.
- Document owner.
- Last validation date.
- Conflicting organization charts.
- Missing units or roles.
- Stale incumbent information.
- Unclear reporting relationships.
- Roles without documented authority.
- Expired delegations.
- Acting assignments without expiration dates.
- Informal structures represented as formal.
- Confidence level.
- Items requiring HR, governance, legal, policy, or leadership validation.
