# Organizational Structure

## Purpose
Perform **organizational structure** as a distinct executive workflow—not as a generic summary.

## Skill logic
1. Map roles, reporting relationships, authorities, and required sequence.
2. Validate the effective date and source of each relationship.
3. Identify gaps, overlaps, ambiguity, or missing approvals.
4. Produce the structure or pathway with items requiring confirmation.

## Operating rules
- Use INPUTS.md as the evidence contract.
- Produce every item in OUTPUTS.md.
- Cite material findings and explain every score.
- Separate facts, assumptions, analysis, and recommendations.
- Require human review before distribution, approval, reassignment, or action.
