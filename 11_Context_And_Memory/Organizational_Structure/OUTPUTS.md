# Outputs

## Executive organizational summary

- Organization and scope.
- Top-level leadership.
- Major organizational units.
- Primary responsibilities.
- Important governance bodies.
- Key reporting and accountability relationships.
- Material organizational changes.
- Responsibility gaps or overlaps.
- Decision or clarification required.
- Effective date and source summary.

## Organizational hierarchy

For each organizational unit:

- Unit name.
- Parent unit.
- Subordinate units.
- Leader.
- Purpose.
- Primary responsibilities.
- Decision authority.
- Status.
- Effective date.
- Authoritative source.
- Confidence and validation status.

## Role and leadership directory

For each material role:

- Position title.
- Organizational unit.
- Reports-to role.
- Direct reports.
- Functional or matrix relationships.
- Appointment status.
- Effective and expiration dates.
- Primary responsibilities.
- Decision and budget authority.
- Delegations.
- Backup or interim coverage.
- Source.

## Organizational tree

Show:

Organization  
→ Division or major unit

→ Subordinate roles or teams

- Formal hierarchy.

## Gaps and overlaps

Identify:

- Responsibilities with no clear owner.
- Multiple units claiming the same responsibility.
- Decision authority inconsistent with responsibility.
- Units performing duplicate work.
- Governance bodies with overlapping mandates.
- Informal structures operating without documentation.
- Critical capabilities without sufficient coverage.
- Conflicting reporting relationships.
- Organizational changes not reflected in records.
- Expired or unclear authority.

## Recommended clarifications

- Recommended clarification.
- Decision-maker.
- Required date.
- Interim approach.
- Required documentation or communication.
