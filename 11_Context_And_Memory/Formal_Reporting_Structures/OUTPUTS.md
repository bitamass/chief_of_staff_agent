## Reporting gaps and conflicts

Identify:

- Roles with unclear reporting lines.
- Multiple authorities with conflicting responsibilities.
- Decisions without approval authority.
- Reporting obligations without owners.
- Governance bodies without current charters.
- Informal practices inconsistent with formal documentation.
- Expired or unclear delegations.
- Reports produced without a defined recipient or decision purpose.
- Duplicate reporting.
- Missing escalation routes.
- Organizational changes not reflected in reporting records.
