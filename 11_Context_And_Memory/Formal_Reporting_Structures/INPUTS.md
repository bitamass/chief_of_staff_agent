# Inputs

## Organizational context

- Organization, division, department, program, portfolio, initiative, or operational area.
- Organizational mission and strategic priorities.
- Executive sponsor.
- Accountable business or operational owner.
- Governance model.
- Current organizational structure.
- Reporting period.
- Source documents and effective dates.
- Scope and items explicitly out of scope.

## Organizational hierarchy

For each organizational unit:

- Unit name.
- Parent unit.
- Leader.
- Leader’s title.
- Reporting manager or governing authority.
- Subordinate units.
- Primary responsibilities.
- Decision authority.
- Budget authority.
- Operational authority.
- Geographic or functional scope.
- Effective date.
- Source and approval status.

## Position and role information

For each relevant role:

- Role or position title.
- Incumbent, when appropriate and authorized.
- Organizational unit.
- Reports-to role.
- Direct reports.
- Dotted-line or matrix-reporting relationships.
- Acting, interim, delegated, or permanent status.
- Formal responsibilities.
- Decision and approval authority.
- Required consultations.
- Required reporting obligations.
- Delegation or backup.
- Effective and expiration dates.
- Source of authority.

## Governance bodies

For each board, committee, council, steering group, review body, or working group:

- Name.
- Purpose.
- Charter.
- Chair and membership.
- Sponsoring authority.
- Decision, recommendation, advisory, or oversight role.
- Scope.
- Quorum or voting requirements.
- Meeting cadence.
- Reporting destination.
- Escalation authority.
- Records and documentation requirements.
- Effective date.
- Charter version and source.

## Reporting relationships

For each relationship:

- Reporting source.
- Reporting recipient.
- Relationship type:
  - Direct managerial.
  - Functional.
  - Matrix.
  - Programmatic.
  - Financial.
  - Governance.
  - Regulatory.
  - Advisory.
  - Oversight.
  - Contractual.
  - Operational.
  - Informational.
- Information or decisions reported.
- Reporting frequency.
- Required format.
- Decision or action expected from the recipient.
- Escalation path.
- Source and effective date.

## Decision and approval authority

- Decision category.
- Responsible preparer.
- Recommending role.
- Reviewing role.
- Approving authority.
- Implementation authority.
- Risk-acceptance authority.
- Financial threshold.
- Delegation conditions.
- Required consultation.
- Escalation route.
- Source policy, charter, contract, or delegation document.
- Effective date and version.

## Formal reporting obligations

- Required report or submission.
- Reporting owner.
- Recipient.
- Purpose.
- Scope and required content.
- Frequency.
- Submission deadline.
- Review or approval requirement.
- Source system.
- Retention requirement.
- Confidentiality classification.
- Applicable policy, regulation, contract, grant, or governance requirement.
- Escalation for missed reporting.

## Program and initiative reporting

- Initiative or program name.
- Executive sponsor.
- Accountable owner.
- Program manager.
- Portfolio owner.
- Governance body.
- Reporting cadence.
- Status-report recipient.
- Decision forum.
- Risk and issue escalation route.
- Financial reporting route.
- Milestone and deliverable reporting.
- Change-control authority.
- Closure and benefit-realization reporting.

## Matrix and cross-functional relationships

- Primary manager.
- Functional manager.
- Program or initiative lead.
- Shared-service owner.
- Assigned responsibilities.
- Priority-setting authority.
- Resource-allocation authority.
- Performance-management authority.
- Conflict-resolution route.
- Escalation path.
- Effective dates.
- Source documentation.

## External reporting relationships

When applicable:

- External governing body.
- Regulator.
- Funder.
- Partner.
- Vendor.
- Contracting entity.
- Accrediting organization.
- Required submission.
- Reporting owner.
- Frequency.
- Deadline.
- Approval requirement.
- Confidentiality and records requirements.
- Consequence of missed reporting.

## Historical and change information

- Previous organizational structure.
- Previous reporting relationships.
- Approved restructuring decision.
- Effective date.
- Transitional reporting arrangement.
- Interim authority.
- Responsibilities transferred.
- Reporting relationships ended.
- Pending organizational changes.
- Change owner.
- Communication and records of approval.

## Data-quality inputs

- Authoritative source.
- Document owner.
- Last validation date.
- Conflicting organizational charts.
- Unclear reporting relationships.
- Roles without documented authority.
- Expired delegations.
- Acting assignments without end dates.
- Informal relationships presented as formal.
- Missing governance charters.
- Stale position information.
- Confidence level.
- Items requiring HR, governance, legal, policy, or leadership confirmation.
