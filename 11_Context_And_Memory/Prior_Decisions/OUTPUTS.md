# Outputs

## Prior-decision register

For each decision:

- Decision ID and title.
- Decision statement.
- Decision date.
- Effective date.
- Decision-maker or body.
- Decision status.
- Scope.
- Rationale.
- Conditions.
- Implementation owner.
- Current applicability.
- Source.
- Confidence and validation status.

## Decision summary

- What was decided.
- Who decided it.
- When it was decided.
- Why it was decided.
- Options considered.
- Material trade-offs.
- Conditions and restrictions.
- Actions created.
- Expected outcome.
- Current implementation status.
- Current validity.

## Decision chronology

Show:

Original decision  
→ Implementation action  
→ Review or outcome  
→ Modification  
→ Superseding decision  
→ Current controlling position

For every stage:

- Date.
- Decision or event.
- Decision-maker.
- Effect.
- Source.
- Current relevance.

## Decision-precedent analysis

For each potentially relevant prior decision:

- Prior decision.
- Current decision.
- Similarities.
- Material differences.
- Applicable principles.
- Conditions limiting applicability.
- Changes in strategy, policy, technology, data, law, risk, or stakeholders.
- Recommendation:
  - Directly applicable.
  - Applicable with modification.
  - Informative but not controlling.
  - No longer applicable.
  - Insufficient evidence.
- Rationale and confidence.

- Decision-maker.

- Implementation authority.
- Whether authority was valid on the decision date.
- Missing or conflicting authority evidence.

## Current decision briefing

- Current decision required.
- Relevant prior decisions.
- What can be reused.
- What must be reconsidered.
- Changed context.
- Outstanding conditions or commitments.
- Risks of inconsistent treatment.
- Questions leadership should resolve.
- Recommended next step.

## Decision gaps and conflicts

- Decision discussed but never formally made.
- Multiple inconsistent decisions.
- Missing decision authority.
- Conditional decision treated as final.
- Expired decision still being applied.
- Superseding decision not reflected in operations.
- Decision without implementation ownership.
- Decision without outcome review.
- Missing source or approved record.

## Executive attention

- Decision requiring clarification.
- Conflict between prior and current direction.
- Expired or superseded authority.
- Unfulfilled conditions.
- Precedent with significant changed context.
- Required governance or specialized review.
- Decision-maker and required date.

## Source and confidence report

- Authoritative sources.
- Draft or unofficial records.
- Missing documentation.
- Conflicting minutes or decisions.
- Stale applicability assessment.
- Assumptions.
- Confidence level.
- Items requiring human validation.
