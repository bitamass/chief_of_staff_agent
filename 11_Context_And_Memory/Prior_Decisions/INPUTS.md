# Inputs

## Decision context

- Organization, portfolio, program, initiative, project, policy, or operational area.
- Purpose of retrieving or analyzing prior decisions.
- Current executive question or decision.
- Reporting cutoff date.
- Time period in scope.
- Executive sponsor.
- Accountable decision owner.
- Relevant strategic objectives.
- Source repositories and access limitations.
- Items explicitly out of scope.

## Decision record

For each prior decision:

- Decision ID.
- Decision title.
- Complete decision statement.
- Decision date.
- Effective date.
- Expiration or review date.
- Decision status:
  - Proposed.
  - Approved.
  - Approved with conditions.
  - Rejected.
  - Deferred.
  - Withdrawn.
  - Superseded.
  - Expired.
  - Reversed.
  - Under review.
- Decision-maker or decision body.
- Decision owner.
- Initiative, program, policy, or operation affected.
- Source document.
- Approval or verification status.

## Decision authority

- Authorized decision-maker.
- Source of authority.
- Delegation or approval threshold.
- Quorum or voting requirement.
- Required consultees.
- Recommending authority.
- Reviewing authority.
- Implementation authority.
- Risk-acceptance authority.
- Governance or escalation body.
- Effective dates of the authority.

## Decision rationale

- Problem or opportunity addressed.
- Strategic objective supported.
- Options considered.
- Evaluation criteria.
- Evidence reviewed.
- Recommendation presented.
- Rationale for the selected option.
- Trade-offs.
- Risks considered.
- Stakeholder considerations.
- Financial or resource implications.
- Assumptions.
- Dissenting or alternative views, when formally recorded.
- Confidence and limitations.

## Conditions and constraints

- Conditions attached to approval.
- Usage or implementation restrictions.
- Required safeguards.
- Scope limitations.
- Funding or resource conditions.
- Required governance or assurance reviews.
- Required reporting and monitoring.
- Human-review requirements.
- Expiration or renewal conditions.
- Conditions triggering re-review.
- Required evidence of compliance.

## Implementation information

- Implementation owner.
- Required actions.
- Action owners.
- Due dates.
- Deliverables.
- Milestones.
- Dependencies.
- Funding or resources.
- Communication requirements.
- Operational owner.
- Implementation status.
- Evidence of implementation.
- Residual actions.

## Outcome and effectiveness information

- Expected outcome.
- Success measures.
- Actual outcome.
- Benefits realized.
- Risks or issues created.
- Unintended consequences.
- Stakeholder response.
- Implementation effectiveness.
- Lessons learned.
- Date of outcome review.
- Evidence and confidence.

## Relationship to current decision

- Current decision or question.
- Similarity to the prior decision.
- Differences in scope, context, data, technology, policy, stakeholders, or risk.
- Whether the precedent remains applicable.
- Material changes since the prior decision.
- Conditions that limit use as precedent.
- Elements that may inform the current decision.
- Elements that should not be reused.

## Supersession and change history

- Prior decision superseded.
- Subsequent decision.
- Relationship between decisions.
- Reason for change.
- Date changed.
- Decision-maker.
- Effective date.
- Responsibilities or conditions changed.
- Current controlling decision.
- Source and approval status.

## Meeting and governance context

- Meeting or governance body.
- Date.
- Agenda item.
- Participants.
- Discussion summary.
- Motion or resolution.
- Vote, when formally recorded.
- Decision and conditions.
- Follow-up actions.
- Approved minutes or record.
- Confidentiality and records classification.

## Related records

- Related decisions.
- Related initiatives.
- Related policies.
- Related risks and issues.
- Related assumptions.
- Related actions and commitments.
- Related approvals.
- Related milestones and deliverables.
- Related stakeholder communications.
- Related contracts or agreements.

## Source and records information

- Authoritative source.
- Source owner.
- Document title.
- Document version.
- Publication or approval date.
- Records classification.
- Retention requirement.
- Access restriction.
- Whether the record is draft, approved, or final.
- Direct citation or repository location.
- Last validation date.

## Data-quality inputs

- Missing decision statement.
- Missing decision authority.
- Draft discussion represented as a decision.
- Recommendation represented as approval.
- Conditional approval represented as unrestricted.
- Superseded decision represented as current.
- Conflicting decision records.
- Missing source.
- Unverified implementation.
- Stale outcome information.
- Confidence level.
- Items requiring governance, legal, policy, records, or leadership validation.
