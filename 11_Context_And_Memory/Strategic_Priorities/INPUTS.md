# Inputs

## Strategic framework

- Organizational mission.
- Vision.
- Values and guiding principles.
- Approved strategic plan.
- Planning period.
- Strategic themes.
- Strategic objectives.
- Executive and board priorities.
- Annual operating priorities.
- Current version and effective date.
- Strategy owner.
- Approval authority.
- Source documents.

## Strategic-priority record

For each priority:

- Priority ID and title.
- Priority statement.
- Description.
- Strategic theme.
- Intended outcome.
- Rationale.
- Executive sponsor.
- Accountable owner.
- Planning horizon.
- Start and end dates.
- Status.
- Approval date.
- Authorizing person or body.
- Source and version.

## Outcome and success measures

For each priority:

- Intended outcome.
- Baseline.
- Target.
- Metric or key result.
- Calculation method.
- Unit of measure.
- Data source.
- Data owner.
- Reporting frequency.
- Current value.
- Forecast.
- Deadline.
- Evidence of progress.
- Confidence and limitations.

## Priority hierarchy

- Enterprise priority.
- Division or departmental priority.
- Portfolio priority.
- Program or initiative priority.
- Supporting objective.
- Supporting key result.
- Supporting deliverable.
- Relationship to parent priority.
- Relative importance.
- Mandatory versus discretionary status.
- Time horizon.
- Source of authority.

## Initiative alignment

For each initiative:

- Initiative name.
- Sponsor and owner.
- Strategic priority supported.
- Expected contribution.
- Key results supported.
- Current status.
- Resource requirement.
- Expected benefit.
- Risks and dependencies.
- Evidence of contribution.
- Confidence in alignment.
- Potential overlap or conflict.

## Resource and investment information

For each priority:

- Approved budget.
- Actual and forecast investment.
- Staffing and capabilities.
- Technology, data, vendor, or infrastructure needs.
- Executive attention required.
- Shared-resource dependencies.
- Funding gaps.
- Capacity constraints.
- Opportunity cost.
- Resource owner.
- Confidence and assumptions.

## Executive and governance commitments

- Executive decision.
- Board resolution or direction.
- Regulatory or policy obligation.
- Contractual commitment.
- Public commitment.
- Funding or grant condition.
- Required outcome.
- Accountable owner.
- Required date.
- Source.
- Current status.
- Consequence of nonperformance.

## Stakeholder context

- Stakeholders affected.
- Stakeholder needs.
- Intended beneficiaries.
- Stakeholder influence.
- Current support or concern.
- Equity, accessibility, community, workforce, clinical, research, customer, student, or partner considerations.
- Required engagement.
- Evidence and limitations.

## Risk and dependency information

- Risks affecting the priority.
- Issues already affecting progress.
- Cross-priority dependencies.
- Resource conflicts.
- Technology or data dependencies.
- Vendor or procurement dependencies.
- Policy, governance, security, privacy, clinical, legal, regulatory, financial, labor, or operational constraints.
- Mitigation.
- Owner.
- Target date.
- Escalation status.

## External and environmental context

- Regulatory changes.
- Policy developments.
- Economic conditions.
- Technology developments.
- Market or industry trends.
- Stakeholder expectations.
- Workforce trends.
- Research developments.
- Vendor landscape.
- Environmental or sustainability considerations.

## Priority conflict information

- Priorities competing for the same resources.
- Conflicting outcomes.
- Overlapping initiatives.
- Contradictory metrics.
- Timing conflicts.
- Mandatory obligations.
- Dependency conflicts.
- Trade-offs.
- Decision authority.
- Required decision date.

## Change history

- Previous priority statement.
- Revised priority.
- Reason for change.
- Date changed.
- Approving authority.
- Initiatives affected.
- Resources affected.
- Measures changed.
- Commitments changed.
- Communication and implementation status.
- Superseded priority.

## Review and reporting information

- Review cadence.
- Next review date.
- Reporting owner.
- Governance forum.
- Progress report.
- Decision thresholds.
- Conditions requiring reprioritization.
- Conditions requiring strategy refresh.
- Required evidence for continued priority status.

## Data-quality inputs

- Missing approval.
- Unclear priority wording.
- Duplicate or overlapping priorities.
- Priorities without outcomes or measures.
- Priorities without accountable owners.
- Priorities without supporting initiatives.
- Stale strategy documents.
- Informal priorities presented as approved strategy.
- Conflicting leadership direction.
- Unsupported alignment claims.
- Confidence level.
- Items requiring leadership or governance validation.
