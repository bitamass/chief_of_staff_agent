# Inputs

## Report definition

- Report title and reporting period.
- Initiative, program, project, portfolio, or operational area being reported.
- Purpose of the report and decisions it is intended to support.
- Intended audience and required level of detail.
- Reporting cadence and submission deadline.
- Approved reporting template, status definitions, and formatting requirements.
- Previous status report for comparison.
- Executive sponsor, program owner, and report preparer.

## Scope and objectives

- Approved charter, scope, objectives, and expected outcomes.
- Strategic priorities or business goals supported.
- Major workstreams, deliverables, and milestones.
- Items explicitly out of scope.
- Success measures, key performance indicators, and acceptance criteria.
- Approved baseline schedule and budget, when relevant.
- Current lifecycle or implementation phase.

## Progress data

For each workstream, deliverable, or milestone:

- Owner.
- Planned start and completion dates.
- Current forecast dates.
- Current status.
- Percentage complete, when supported by evidence.
- Work completed during the reporting period.
- Expected work that was not completed.
- Work planned for the next reporting period.
- Evidence supporting completion.
- Changes from the prior report.
- Dependencies and handoffs.

## Decision and action data

- Decisions made during the reporting period.
- Decision date, decision-maker, and source.
- Decisions pending.
- Decision owner and required decision date.
- Open actions, owners, due dates, and status.
- Actions completed during the reporting period.
- Overdue actions and documented reasons for delay.
- Commitments made to leadership or stakeholders.
- Escalations and their current disposition.

## Risk and issue data

For each material risk or issue:

- Description.
- Category.
- Probability and impact.
- Severity and urgency.
- Owner.
- Mitigation or resolution plan.
- Target resolution date.
- Current trend: improving, stable, or worsening.
- Dependencies or outcomes affected.
- Escalation status.
- Evidence source.

## Resource and financial data

When applicable:

- Approved and current budget.
- Actual and forecast spending.
- Variance and explanation.
- Staffing plan and current staffing.
- Capacity or expertise gaps.
- Vendor or partner dependencies.
- Resource decisions required.
- Material procurement, contracting, or funding constraints.

## Stakeholder and governance data

- Key stakeholder updates.
- Governance reviews completed or upcoming.
- Required approvals and assurance activities.
- Stakeholder concerns, feedback, or unresolved questions.
- Communications completed or planned.
- Changes in stakeholder support or participation.
- Compliance, security, privacy, legal, clinical, or regulatory requirements.

## Data-quality inputs

- Source documents and systems.
- Date of the latest update.
- Data owner.
- Known gaps, conflicts, or unverified information.
- Confidence level.
- Items requiring human confirmation.
