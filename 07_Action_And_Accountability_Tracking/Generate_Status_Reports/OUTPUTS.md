# Outputs

## Executive summary

- Overall status for the reporting period.
- Most important accomplishments.
- Most significant changes since the previous report.
- Material risks, issues, or delays.
- Decisions or interventions required.
- Near-term priorities.
- Concise explanation of why the current status matters.

## Overall status assessment

- Overall status: on track, watch, at risk, or off track.
- Status of schedule, scope, budget, resources, risks, and outcomes.
- Evidence supporting each status.
- Trend from the previous reporting period.
- Status exceptions or disagreements requiring validation.

## Accomplishments

- Deliverables completed during the reporting period.
- Milestones achieved.
- Decisions completed.
- Actions closed.
- Measurable outcomes or value realized.
- Source evidence and completion criteria.
- Difference between completed, substantially completed, and still in progress.

## Workstream and deliverable status

For each material workstream or deliverable:

- Owner.
- Current status.
- Planned versus current forecast dates.
- Progress during the reporting period.
- Next planned activity.
- Dependencies.
- Risks or issues.
- Required leadership attention.
- Source and confidence level.

## Decisions and actions

- Decisions made and their implications.
- Decisions pending, responsible decision-maker, and required date.
- Open actions with owners and due dates.
- Overdue or blocked actions.
- Actions requiring escalation.
- Commitments that should be reviewed in the next reporting period.

## Risks and issues

- Highest-priority risks and issues.
- Severity, urgency, owner, and trend.
- Effect on scope, schedule, cost, operations, compliance, or outcomes.
- Mitigation or resolution status.
- Decision or assistance required.
- Target resolution date.
- Escalation status.

## Milestones and forward look

- Milestones completed.
- Milestones due in the next reporting period.
- Upcoming meetings, reviews, approvals, and decision points.
- Critical dependencies.
- Expected deliverables.
- Conditions that could alter the forecast.

## Resource and financial status

When applicable:

- Budget and forecast summary.
- Material variances and explanations.
- Staffing and capacity status.
- Resource gaps affecting delivery.
- Vendor or partner concerns.
- Funding or resource decisions required.

## Leadership attention

- Decisions required from leadership.
- Issues requiring intervention or escalation.
- Conflicting priorities requiring resolution.
- Resource requests.
- Governance or assurance matters requiring attention.
- Recommended actions, owners, and requested response dates.

## Data confidence and sources

- Source list.
- Reporting cutoff date.
- Missing or stale information.
- Conflicting status reports.
- Unverified claims or metrics.
- Assumptions used.
- Items requiring confirmation before distribution.
