# Generate Status Reports

## Purpose
Generate Status Reports helps make commitments, ownership, due dates, status, and escalation needs visible.

## Instructions
1. Confirm the executive question, owner, and time horizon.
2. Use only dated, attributable evidence.
3. Normalize dated evidence, distinguish facts from inference, identify exceptions, and produce the specified executive work product.
4. Produce the initiative health report with exception flags defined in OUTPUTS.md.
5. State gaps, assumptions, and items requiring human review.

## Boundary
Do not mark work complete without evidence or reassign accountability without approval.
