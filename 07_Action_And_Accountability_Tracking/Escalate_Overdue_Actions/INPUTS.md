# Inputs

## Required action data

- Action ID and action description.
- Initiative, program, meeting, decision, or deliverable connected to the action.
- Source meeting, decision, commitment, or document.
- Accountable owner and responsible contributor.
- Original assignment date.
- Original due date and any revised due dates.
- Current status.
- Date of the most recent status update.
- Percentage complete or remaining work, when available.
- Expected completion date.
- Evidence or acceptance criteria required to close the action.

## Delay and impact data

- Number of calendar and business days overdue.
- Reason for the delay, if documented.
- Dependencies or blockers preventing completion.
- Deliverables, decisions, milestones, or meetings affected by the delay.
- Operational, financial, clinical, technical, security, privacy, regulatory, legal, reputational, or stakeholder impact.
- Consequences of further delay.
- Whether the action is on a critical path.
- Whether the delay affects another owner’s work.
- Whether an upcoming executive decision depends on completion.
- Current impact and projected future impact.

## Ownership and follow-up history

- Action owner’s role, authority, and organizational unit.
- Executive sponsor or accountable leader.
- Previous reminders and follow-up dates.
- Responses received from the owner.
- Prior revised deadlines or recovery commitments.
- Previous escalation attempts and their outcomes.
- Support, resources, or decisions requested by the owner.
- Delegation or backup owner, when documented.

## Escalation policy inputs

- Organizational escalation policy or agreed escalation thresholds.
- Severity, urgency, or priority classifications.
- Grace periods and reminder cadence.
- Escalation levels and authorized recipients.
- Conditions requiring immediate escalation.
- Required communication and documentation standards.
- Confidentiality, personnel, legal, labor, or privacy restrictions.
- Situations in which escalation must be paused or handled through another process.

## Optional contextual inputs

- Action register and commitment tracker.
- Decision log and risk register.
- Initiative charter, roadmap, and milestone plan.
- RACI or responsibility matrix.
- Meeting notes and executive updates.
- Resource plans, dependency maps, and issue logs.
- Current calendar and upcoming decision forums.
