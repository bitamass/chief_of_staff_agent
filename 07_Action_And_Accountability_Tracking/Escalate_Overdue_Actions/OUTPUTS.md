# Outputs

## Overdue-action register

For each overdue action:

- Action ID and concise description.
- Initiative or deliverable affected.
- Owner and accountable leader.
- Original and current due dates.
- Days overdue.
- Current status and latest update.
- Documented reason for delay.
- Dependencies or blockers.
- Impact of continued delay.
- Source references.
- Data-quality or verification flags.

## Escalation priority

- Recommended escalation classification:
  - Monitor.
  - Reminder required.
  - Owner follow-up required.
  - Sponsor attention required.
  - Executive escalation required.
  - Immediate risk or governance escalation required.
- Severity and urgency.
- Explanation of the classification.
- Confidence level.
- Escalation deadline.
- Conditions that would increase or reduce the escalation level.

## Recommended escalation action

- Recommended recipient.
- Recommended sender.
- Purpose of the escalation.
- Specific decision, intervention, resource, or response needed.
- Required response date.
- Proposed recovery date.
- Recommended follow-up meeting or decision forum.
- Alternative action if the owner cannot complete the work.
- Conditions for closing or de-escalating the item.

## Draft escalation communication

A concise draft containing:

- The overdue commitment.
- Original and revised due dates.
- Current status.
- Documented blocker or cause.
- Business or operational impact.
- Previous follow-up history.
- Specific assistance, decision, or commitment requested.
- Required response date.
- Next escalation step if unresolved.

## Portfolio-level analysis

- Number and percentage of actions overdue.
- Overdue actions by owner, initiative, severity, and age.
- Repeated overdue commitments.
- Actions with multiple revised deadlines.
- Critical-path or decision-blocking actions.
- Concentration of overdue work by team or owner.
- Systemic themes such as unclear ownership, resource constraints, dependency failures, or unrealistic deadlines.
- Recommended management interventions.

## Executive attention summary

- Most consequential overdue actions.
- Decisions or milestones at risk.
- Owners or sponsors requiring immediate engagement.
- Escalations awaiting response.
- Actions that should be reassigned, rescheduled, or formally closed.
- Trends indicating a broader accountability or delivery problem.
