# Inputs

## Commitment record

For each commitment:

- Commitment ID.
- Clear description of what was promised.
- Initiative, program, decision, meeting, or stakeholder relationship.
- Source meeting, communication, decision, agreement, or document.
- Date the commitment was made.
- Person or role who made the commitment.
- Accountable owner.
- Responsible contributor or supporting team.
- Recipient or beneficiary of the commitment.
- Due date and relevant interim milestones.
- Expected outcome or deliverable.
- Completion or acceptance criteria.
- Priority and materiality.
- Current status.
- Date of the latest update.

## Progress evidence

- Work completed.
- Remaining work.
- Percentage complete, when supported by a documented method.
- Drafts, approvals, deliverables, or other evidence of progress.
- Current forecast completion date.
- Changes from the previous reporting period.
- Owner-provided status narrative.
- Evidence that completion criteria have been met.
- Acceptance or confirmation from the authorized recipient, when required.

## Dependency and blocker information

- Decisions required.
- Resources or funding required.
- Inputs required from other teams.
- Technology, data, procurement, vendor, governance, or approval dependencies.
- Known blockers.
- Owner of each dependency or blocker.
- Expected resolution date.
- Effect on the commitment’s due date or outcome.
- Escalation status.

## Follow-up history

- Previous reminders and follow-up dates.
- Owner responses.
- Revised deadlines and who approved them.
- Recovery plans and interim commitments.
- Previous escalations.
- Assistance requested.
- Decisions made concerning the commitment.
- Reasons for deferral, cancellation, replacement, or closure.

## Portfolio and planning context

- Initiative charter and objectives.
- Executive priorities.
- Decision log.
- Action tracker.
- Milestone plan.
- RACI or ownership documentation.
- Risk and issue register.
- Upcoming meetings and decision forums.
- Related commitments and dependencies.
- Reporting and escalation policies.

## Data-quality inputs

- Source owner.
- Last validation date.
- Confidence level.
- Missing or conflicting information.
- Duplicate or overlapping commitment records.
- Commitments recorded only in narrative notes.
- Items requiring owner confirmation.
