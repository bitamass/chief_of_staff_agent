# Outputs

## Commitment register

For every active commitment:

- Commitment ID.
- Commitment description.
- Source and date established.
- Initiative or relationship supported.
- Accountable owner.
- Recipient or beneficiary.
- Due date.
- Current status.
- Current forecast date.
- Completion criteria.
- Latest progress update.
- Dependencies and blockers.
- Next follow-up date.
- Source references.
- Confidence and validation status.

## Status classification

Each commitment should be classified as:

- Not started.
- In progress.
- On track.
- Watch.
- At risk.
- Overdue.
- Blocked.
- Pending acceptance.
- Completed.
- Deferred.
- Cancelled.
- Superseded.

The output must explain the basis for any Watch, At risk, Overdue, or Blocked classification.

## Near-term commitments

- Commitments due within the selected monitoring period.
- Owners and due dates.
- Remaining work.
- Required decisions or dependencies.
- Likelihood of timely completion.
- Recommended follow-up date.
- Leadership involvement required.

## Overdue and at-risk commitments

- Days overdue or time remaining.
- Current impact.
- Reason for delay or risk.
- Prior follow-up history.
- Recovery plan.
- Requested decision, resource, or intervention.
- Recommended escalation level.
- Proposed response and recovery dates.

## Completed commitments

- Completion date.
- Evidence of completion.
- Acceptance status.
- Outcome delivered.
- Any residual follow-up.
- Whether the commitment can be formally closed.

## Commitment changes

- New commitments created during the reporting period.
- Commitments completed.
- Due dates revised.
- Owners changed.
- Commitments deferred, cancelled, or superseded.
- Commitments whose scope or expected outcome changed.
- Changes requiring formal approval or recipient notification.

## Portfolio-level analysis

- Total active commitments.
- Commitments due soon.
- Commitments overdue.
- Commitments at risk or blocked.
- Commitments awaiting acceptance.
- Completion rate for the reporting period.
- Commitments by initiative, owner, stakeholder, and status.
- Repeatedly revised commitments.
- Concentration of commitments by owner or team.
- Common blockers and dependencies.
- Trends in reliability and closure.

## Executive attention summary

- Most consequential commitments approaching their due dates.
- Overdue commitments affecting major decisions or milestones.
- Conflicting commitments or priorities.
- Commitments lacking clear ownership or acceptance criteria.
- Required executive decisions or interventions.
- Commitments that should be renegotiated, reassigned, deferred, or formally closed.
