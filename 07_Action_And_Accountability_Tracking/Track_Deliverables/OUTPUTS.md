# Outputs

## Deliverable register

For each deliverable:

- Deliverable ID and name.
- Initiative or objective supported.
- Owner and accountable approver.
- Original due date.
- Current forecast date.
- Current status.
- Percentage complete, when evidence supports it.
- Next milestone.
- Acceptance status.
- Dependencies and blockers.
- Risks and issues.
- Source references.
- Confidence and validation status.

## Status classification

Each deliverable should be classified as:

- Not started.
- In progress.
- On track.
- Watch.
- At risk.
- Off track.
- Blocked.
- Draft complete.
- Submitted for review.
- Pending approval.
- Approved.
- Implemented.
- Completed.
- Deferred.
- Cancelled.
- Superseded.

The output must explain the evidence supporting any Watch, At risk, Off track, or Blocked classification.

## Schedule performance

- Baseline due date versus current forecast date.
- Number of days ahead or behind schedule.
- Milestones achieved and missed.
- Critical-path impacts.
- Approval or review delays.
- Schedule changes and documented authorization.
- Effect on dependent deliverables, meetings, or decisions.
- Recovery milestones and expected recovery date.

## Scope and acceptance status

- Required components completed.
- Components remaining.
- Scope changes.
- Acceptance criteria met or unmet.
- Required reviews completed or pending.
- Approvals obtained or outstanding.
- Evidence needed before closure.
- Gaps between delivery, approval, implementation, and operational adoption.

## Near-term deliverables

- Deliverables due within the selected monitoring period.
- Owners and due dates.
- Remaining work.
- Readiness for review or approval.
- Dependencies requiring attention.
- Likelihood of timely completion.
- Recommended follow-up.
- Leadership participation required.

## At-risk and blocked deliverables

- Description of the risk or blocker.
- Root cause or documented contributing factors.
- Current and projected impact.
- Owner.
- Mitigation or recovery plan.
- Decision, resource, or intervention required.
- Target resolution date.
- Recommended escalation level.
- Effect on initiative objectives and downstream work.

## Completed deliverables

- Completion date.
- Evidence of completion.
- Acceptance or approval status.
- Outcome delivered.
- Implementation or handoff status.
- Residual actions.
- Whether formal closure is appropriate.

## Portfolio-level analysis

- Total active deliverables.
- Deliverables due soon.
- Deliverables completed during the reporting period.
- Deliverables at risk, off track, or blocked.
- Deliverables awaiting review or approval.
- Schedule and acceptance trends.
- Deliverables by initiative, owner, status, and priority.
- Concentration of critical deliverables by owner or team.
- Common blockers and dependency patterns.
- Delivery reliability against baseline dates.

## Executive attention summary

- Most consequential deliverables at risk.
- Deliverables affecting major decisions or milestones.
- Required approvals.
- Scope, resource, or priority conflicts.
- Cross-team dependencies requiring intervention.
- Deliverables that should be rebaselined, rescoped, reassigned, deferred, or cancelled.
- Specific leadership decisions requested and required dates.
