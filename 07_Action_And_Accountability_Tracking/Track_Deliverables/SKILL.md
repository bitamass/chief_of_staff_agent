# Track Deliverables

## Purpose
Track Deliverables helps make commitments, ownership, due dates, status, and escalation needs visible.

## Instructions
1. Confirm the executive question, owner, and time horizon.
2. Use only dated, attributable evidence.
3. Trace objectives to outcomes and deliverables, assess health and dependencies, and flag intervention needs.
4. Produce the track deliverables executive work product defined in OUTPUTS.md.
5. State gaps, assumptions, and items requiring human review.

## Boundary
Do not mark work complete without evidence or reassign accountability without approval.
