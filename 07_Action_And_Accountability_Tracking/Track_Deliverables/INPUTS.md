# Inputs

## Deliverable definition

For each deliverable:

- Deliverable ID and name.
- Clear description of the expected output or outcome.
- Initiative, program, project, workstream, or strategic objective supported.
- Deliverable owner and accountable approver.
- Responsible contributors and supporting teams.
- Intended recipient, user, or beneficiary.
- Planned start date and original due date.
- Current forecast completion date.
- Priority and criticality.
- Current status.
- Date of the most recent status update.
- Source charter, plan, decision, meeting, or agreement.

## Scope and acceptance requirements

- Approved scope.
- Items explicitly out of scope.
- Required components.
- Functional, technical, operational, business, clinical, security, privacy, legal, financial, accessibility, or quality requirements.
- Completion criteria.
- Acceptance criteria.
- Required reviewers and approvers.
- Definition of ready, submitted, approved, implemented, and completed.
- Required evidence or documentation.
- Handoff, implementation, training, support, or operational-readiness requirements.

## Schedule and milestone data

- Baseline schedule.
- Interim milestones.
- Planned and actual milestone dates.
- Current forecast dates.
- Critical-path designation.
- Dependencies and predecessor activities.
- Successor activities or decisions depending on the deliverable.
- Review and approval windows.
- Expected implementation or release date.
- Variances from the baseline schedule.
- Approved schedule changes.

## Progress evidence

- Work completed during the reporting period.
- Remaining work.
- Percentage complete and calculation method, when applicable.
- Drafts, versions, prototypes, analyses, reports, approvals, or implementation evidence.
- Quality-review results.
- Owner-provided status.
- Changes since the previous update.
- Current recovery plan, if delayed.
- Evidence supporting claimed completion.

## Dependency and blocker data

- Upstream inputs required.
- Downstream work affected.
- Decision dependencies.
- Funding and resource dependencies.
- Technology, integration, data, procurement, vendor, staffing, governance, or approval dependencies.
- Known blockers.
- Owner of each dependency or blocker.
- Expected resolution date.
- Impact on schedule, scope, quality, or acceptance.
- Escalation status.

## Resource and financial data

When relevant:

- Assigned staffing and expertise.
- Resource capacity and availability.
- Approved and current budget.
- Actual and forecast cost.
- Vendor or partner responsibilities.
- Procurement and contracting status.
- Material financial or resource variances.
- Resource decisions required.

## Risk and issue data

- Deliverable risks and issues.
- Probability, impact, severity, and urgency.
- Risk or issue owner.
- Mitigation or resolution plan.
- Target date.
- Current trend.
- Effect on acceptance, release, implementation, or dependent work.
- Required leadership intervention.

## Data-quality inputs

- Source owner.
- Source documents or systems.
- Reporting cutoff date.
- Last validation date.
- Confidence level.
- Missing or conflicting information.
- Unverified status claims.
- Deliverables recorded under multiple names.
- Items requiring owner or approver confirmation.
