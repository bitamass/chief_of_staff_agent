# Evaluation — Track Deliverables

The result passes when:

- It answers the specific track deliverables question.
- Every material claim is traceable to dated evidence.
- The track deliverables executive work product has the required fields and accountable owners.
- Unknowns, conflicts, and assumptions are explicit.
- Recommendations are practical and clearly separated from facts.
- Do not mark work complete without evidence or reassign accountability without approval.
