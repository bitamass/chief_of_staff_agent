# Inputs

## OKR framework

- Reporting organization, portfolio, program, team, or leadership scope.
- OKR cycle and reporting period.
- Approved OKR methodology and definitions.
- Strategic planning period.
- Dashboard audience.
- Reporting cadence and data cutoff date.
- Status and confidence definitions.
- Scoring methodology.
- Approved thresholds.
- Previous OKR dashboard or checkpoint.
- Dashboard owner and accountable leadership owner.

## Strategic context

- Organizational mission and strategy.
- Strategic priorities and themes.
- Executive commitments.
- Annual and multiyear goals.
- Relevant board, leadership, or governance decisions.
- Resource and investment priorities.
- Material assumptions and constraints.
- Changes in organizational direction since the OKRs were approved.

## Objective data

For each objective:

- Objective ID and title.
- Concise qualitative description.
- Strategic priority supported.
- Executive sponsor.
- Accountable owner.
- Supporting teams.
- Start and end dates.
- Intended organizational outcome.
- Rationale.
- Scope and exclusions.
- Current status.
- Confidence level.
- Last update date.
- Dependencies.
- Related objectives.
- Relevant risks and issues.

## Key-result data

For each key result:

- Key-result ID and description.
- Parent objective.
- Accountable owner.
- Metric definition.
- Calculation formula.
- Unit of measure.
- Baseline.
- Target.
- Current value.
- Previous-period value.
- Forecast value.
- Reporting period.
- Data source.
- Data owner.
- Update frequency.
- Direction of favorable performance.
- Milestone or due date.
- Current score.
- Confidence level.
- Evidence.
- Known limitations.

## Initiative and workstream data

For each supporting initiative:

- Initiative name and objective.
- Owner and sponsor.
- OKR or key result supported.
- Expected contribution.
- Current phase and status.
- Key deliverables and milestones.
- Resource allocation.
- Risks and issues.
- Dependencies.
- Actions and decisions.
- Evidence that the initiative contributes to the key result.

## Progress and outcome evidence

- Outcomes achieved.
- Milestones completed.
- Benefits realized.
- Adoption or utilization.
- Quality or performance improvement.
- Financial or operational results.
- Stakeholder outcomes.
- Evidence supporting reported progress.
- Difference between completed activity and measurable outcome.
- Attribution limitations.

## Risk and dependency data

- Risks affecting each objective or key result.
- Issues already affecting performance.
- Cross-objective dependencies.
- Resource and capacity constraints.
- Technology, data, vendor, policy, governance, or approval dependencies.
- Mitigation or recovery plans.
- Decision owners and required dates.
- Impact on confidence and forecast.

## Alignment data

- Parent strategic goals.
- Related objectives.
- Shared key results.
- Conflicting targets.
- Duplicate or overlapping work.
- Unfunded objectives.
- Objectives without measurable key results.
- Key results without supporting initiatives.
- Initiatives not connected to any approved OKR.

## Data-quality inputs

- Source system.
- Data owner.
- Last refresh date.
- Data freshness.
- Completeness.
- Reconciliation status.
- Calculation method.
- Manual adjustments.
- Conflicting definitions.
- Missing baselines or targets.
- Unsupported confidence ratings.
- Items requiring human validation.
