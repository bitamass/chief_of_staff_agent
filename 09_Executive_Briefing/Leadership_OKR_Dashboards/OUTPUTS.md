# Outputs

## Leadership OKR summary

- Reporting cycle and cutoff date.
- Number of active objectives and key results.
- Objectives on track, at risk, and off track.
- Key results achieved or likely to be achieved.
- Most significant progress.
- Most important performance gap.
- Major change since the prior checkpoint.
- Critical dependency or risk.
- Leadership decisions required.
- Overall data-confidence statement.

## Objective dashboard

For each objective:

- Objective title.
- Strategic priority supported.
- Sponsor and owner.
- Overall status.
- Confidence of achievement.
- Key-result summary.
- Current versus expected progress.
- Trend from previous checkpoint.
- Supporting initiatives.
- Material risks, issues, and dependencies.
- Required leadership attention.
- Source and last update.

## Key-result dashboard

For each key result:

- Key-result description.
- Owner.
- Baseline.
- Target.
- Current value.
- Forecast.
- Percentage or degree of progress.
- Current score.
- Status.
- Confidence.
- Trend.
- Data source and freshness.
- Supporting initiatives.
- Next milestone.
- Risks or data limitations.

## Progress versus time analysis

- Percentage of the OKR cycle completed.
- Expected versus actual progress.
- Current trajectory.
- Forecast likelihood of achievement.
- Key results progressing slower than elapsed time.
- Front-loaded or back-loaded delivery assumptions.
- Explanation of material variance.

## Outcome and activity distinction

- Measurable outcomes achieved.
- Outputs delivered.
- Activities completed.
- Key results relying mainly on activity measures.
- Key results without sufficient outcome evidence.
- Initiatives producing deliverables without demonstrated contribution to an objective.

## Alignment view

- Objectives mapped to strategic priorities.
- Key results mapped to objectives.
- Initiatives mapped to key results.
- Objectives with insufficient supporting work.
- Initiatives without clear OKR alignment.
- Duplicate or overlapping objectives.
- Conflicting targets.
- Shared dependencies and resource competition.

## Risk and dependency view

- Objectives and key results at risk.
- Highest-impact blockers.
- Cross-organizational dependencies.
- Resource constraints.
- Pending decisions.
- Required governance or assurance activities.
- Recovery plans.
- Owners and target dates.

## Confidence view

For each objective and key result:

- Confidence: high, medium, or low, or the approved scale.
- Evidence supporting confidence.
- Key assumptions.
- Conditions required for success.
- Signals that would increase or reduce confidence.
- Difference between current progress and confidence of final achievement.

## Leadership attention panel

- Decisions required.
- Priority conflicts.
- Resource reallocations.
- Objectives requiring reset or clarification.
- Key results requiring revised measurement.
- Initiatives to accelerate, pause, consolidate, or stop.
- Risks requiring acceptance or escalation.
- Requested decision-maker and required date.

## Check-in narrative

- What changed.
- Why it changed.
- What was learned.
- What is expected next.
- What support is required.
- What will be reviewed at the next checkpoint.

## Data-quality and methodology report

- Missing baselines or targets.
- Stale data.
- Metrics with unclear definitions.
- Unsupported scoring.
- Manual adjustments.
- Conflicting owner reports.
- Outcome attribution limitations.
- Items requiring confirmation before executive use.
