# Inputs

## Document purpose

- One-pager title.
- Topic, initiative, decision, proposal, issue, capability, or opportunity.
- Executive question the document should answer.
- Intended audience.
- Desired outcome.
- Required decision, approval, alignment, funding, or action.
- Document owner.
- Executive sponsor.
- Required completion date.
- Reporting cutoff date.
- Approved template, branding, and accessibility requirements.
- Distribution and confidentiality classification.

## Executive context

- Organizational mission and priorities.
- Strategic objective supported.
- Relevant executive commitments.
- Why the topic requires attention now.
- Decision timeline.
- Upcoming meeting or governance review.
- Previous decisions.
- Current leadership position.
- Matters that remain unresolved.
- Scope and items explicitly out of scope.

## Problem or opportunity definition

- Current state.
- Desired future state.
- Problem, need, or opportunity.
- Affected users, teams, customers, patients, partners, or stakeholders.
- Magnitude and urgency.
- Evidence demonstrating the need.
- Root causes, when established.
- Consequences of inaction.
- Relevant external or internal developments.
- Assumptions and uncertainties.

## Proposed approach

- Proposed solution, recommendation, or course of action.
- Alternative options considered.
- Scope.
- Major components.
- Operating or delivery model.
- Implementation phases.
- Owner and responsible teams.
- Dependencies.
- Required technology, data, staffing, funding, vendor, or partnership support.
- Governance and approval requirements.
- Items deliberately deferred.

## Value and outcomes

- Expected strategic value.
- Operational benefits.
- Financial benefits or avoided costs.
- Quality, service, clinical, research, workforce, stakeholder, or customer outcomes.
- Success measures.
- Baseline and target.
- Expected timing of value.
- Evidence supporting benefit assumptions.
- Attribution limitations.

## Cost and resource information

When relevant:

- Estimated cost.
- Funding source.
- Staffing requirements.
- Expertise requirements.
- Technology or vendor costs.
- Implementation effort.
- Ongoing operating and support costs.
- Opportunity cost.
- Resource trade-offs.
- Confidence and assumptions behind estimates.

## Risks and constraints

- Strategic, operational, financial, schedule, technology, security, privacy, legal, regulatory, clinical, workforce, reputational, or adoption risks.
- Dependencies.
- Constraints.
- Mitigation approach.
- Residual risk.
- Risk owner.
- Required specialized review.
- Conditions that would alter the recommendation.

## Decision information

- Decision requested.
- Decision-maker.
- Decision deadline.
- Options.
- Recommendation.
- Trade-offs.
- Consequence of delay.
- Conditions or guardrails.
- Follow-up actions after the decision.
- Owner and timing.

## Supporting evidence

- Source documents.
- Approved facts.
- Relevant metrics.
- Meeting notes.
- Decision records.
- Initiative charters.
- Business cases.
- Research or benchmarks.
- Financial models.
- Risk reviews.
- Stakeholder feedback.
- Source owner, date, and confidence.
