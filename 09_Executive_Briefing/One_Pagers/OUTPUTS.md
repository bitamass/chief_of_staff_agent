# Outputs

## One-page executive document

### Title and decision statement

- Concise title.
- One-sentence statement of the matter.
- Decision, approval, or action requested.
- Requested decision date.

### Executive summary

- What the matter is.
- Why it matters.
- Why attention is required now.
- Recommended course of action.
- Expected outcome.

### Current state

- Problem or opportunity.
- Affected stakeholders.
- Relevant evidence.
- Material gap between current and desired state.
- Consequence of inaction.

### Proposed approach

- Recommended solution or action.
- Scope.
- Key components.
- Major phases or milestones.
- Ownership.
- Dependencies.
- Items out of scope.

### Value and expected outcomes

- Strategic contribution.
- Expected benefits.
- Success measures.
- Timing of value.
- Evidence and assumptions.

### Options and trade-offs

For each material option:

- Description.
- Benefits.
- Costs.
- Risks.
- Dependencies.
- Time to implement.
- Organizational implications.

### Risks and safeguards

- Most material risks.
- Mitigation.
- Residual exposure.
- Required governance or assurance review.
- Conditions or guardrails.

### Required resources

- Funding.
- Staffing.
- Technology.
- Vendor or partner support.
- Executive participation.
- Major operational commitments.

### Decision and next steps

- Decision required.
- Recommended decision.
- Decision-maker.
- Required date.
- Immediate next actions.
- Owners and deadlines.
- Next review point.

### Sources and confidence

- Material sources.
- Reporting cutoff date.
- Key assumptions.
- Information gaps.
- Facts requiring validation.
- Confidence in the recommendation.

## Optional appendix prompts

If the topic cannot responsibly fit on one page, provide references to:

- Detailed business case.
- Financial model.
- Risk register.
- Architecture or technical assessment.
- Implementation plan.
- Stakeholder analysis.
- Source documentation.
