# Inputs

## Intelligence request

- Decision, question, or executive priority the analysis should support.
- Industry, market, technology, service, or capability being assessed.
- Competitors, peer institutions, vendors, partners, or alternative approaches in scope.
- Geographic and organizational scope.
- Time horizon.
- Reporting cutoff date.
- Intended audience and required level of detail.
- Required deliverable format and deadline.
- Known assumptions and exclusions.
- Approved information sources and research boundaries.

## Internal strategic context

- Organizational mission, objectives, and priorities.
- Current products, services, programs, or capabilities.
- Existing strategic plans and investment priorities.
- Internal strengths and constraints.
- Current initiatives and roadmaps.
- Known performance measures and outcomes.
- Current technology and operating environment.
- Existing partnerships, vendors, and contracts.
- Approved business cases and prior decisions.
- Leadership’s decision criteria.

## Competitor or peer profiles

For each organization:

- Organization name and type.
- Market, service area, or institutional role.
- Relevant products, services, research, programs, or capabilities.
- Target customers, users, patients, partners, or stakeholders.
- Geographic reach.
- Organizational scale.
- Leadership and governance structure when relevant and publicly available.
- Business or operating model.
- Pricing, funding, or revenue model when publicly available.
- Technology platforms and partnerships.
- Publicly announced strategy and priorities.
- Recent launches, investments, acquisitions, or organizational changes.
- Known strengths, limitations, and differentiators.
- Source and publication date for each material fact.

## Market and industry inputs

- Market size and growth.
- Customer or stakeholder needs.
- Adoption trends.
- Technology trends.
- Regulatory and policy developments.
- Funding and investment patterns.
- Workforce and talent trends.
- Vendor and supply-chain developments.
- Standards and interoperability requirements.
- Barriers to entry or adoption.
- Emerging substitutes and alternative models.
- Relevant economic, social, environmental, or demographic developments.

## Performance and benchmark inputs

When available:

- Financial measures.
- Market share.
- Adoption or utilization.
- Customer or stakeholder satisfaction.
- Quality and performance outcomes.
- Operational efficiency.
- Research output.
- Workforce size and expertise.
- Technology maturity.
- Implementation speed.
- Partnership network.
- Public commitments and reported progress.
- Benchmark definitions and comparability limitations.

## Source inputs

Permitted sources may include:

- Official organizational websites.
- Annual reports and financial filings.
- Public regulatory filings.
- Government and regulatory publications.
- Peer-reviewed research.
- Public procurement and contract information.
- Official press releases and product documentation.
- Public presentations and conference materials.
- Reputable industry research.
- Credible news sources.
- Approved internal analyses.
- Licensed databases available to the organization.

For every source:

- Title.
- Publisher.
- Author, when available.
- Publication date.
- Access date.
- Direct link or document reference.
- Source type.
- Reliability assessment.
- Relevant excerpt or summarized fact.
- Any applicable licensing or use restrictions.

## Evidence-quality inputs

- Source recency.
- Source independence.
- Whether the information is primary or secondary.
- Whether the claim is confirmed by more than one source.
- Potential bias or commercial interest.
- Comparability of definitions and measurement periods.
- Missing or conflicting information.
- Confidence level.
- Facts requiring validation.
