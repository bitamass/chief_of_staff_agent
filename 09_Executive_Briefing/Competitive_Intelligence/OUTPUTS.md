# Outputs

## Executive intelligence brief

- Intelligence question.
- Reporting cutoff date.
- Scope and organizations assessed.
- Most decision-relevant findings.
- Significant changes or emerging developments.
- Implications for organizational strategy.
- Opportunities and threats.
- Recommended leadership questions.
- Decisions or follow-up analysis required.
- Confidence and material limitations.

## Competitor and peer profiles

For each organization:

- Strategic direction.
- Relevant offerings or capabilities.
- Target audiences or markets.
- Operating or business model.
- Technology and partnership approach.
- Publicly reported investments and priorities.
- Recent developments.
- Apparent strengths.
- Apparent limitations or gaps.
- Relevance to the organization’s decision.
- Source-linked supporting evidence.
- Confidence level.

## Comparative analysis

A comparison across approved dimensions such as:

- Strategic alignment.
- Capability maturity.
- Product or service breadth.
- Technology approach.
- Data and integration model.
- Governance and risk controls.
- Implementation maturity.
- Partnerships.
- Financial or resource commitment.
- Stakeholder adoption.
- Reported outcomes.
- Scalability.
- Differentiation.

Clearly identify:

- Directly comparable facts.
- Partially comparable information.
- Non-comparable measures.
- Missing evidence.
- Inferences.

## Strategic positioning

- Areas of relative strength.
- Areas of relative weakness.
- Capability gaps.
- Potential differentiators.
- Areas where competitors or peers appear ahead.
- Areas where competitors’ claims lack supporting evidence.
- Opportunities to collaborate rather than compete.
- Potential substitutes or disruptive approaches.
- Risks of inaction.
- Risks of imitation without organizational fit.

## Trend and signal analysis

- Emerging market or institutional trends.
- New technology or service models.
- Changes in investment or partnerships.
- Regulatory or policy developments.
- Changes in customer or stakeholder expectations.
- Early indicators of strategic movement.
- Signals that are isolated versus corroborated.
- Developments requiring continued monitoring.

## Opportunities and threats

For each material item:

- Description.
- Evidence.
- Time horizon.
- Potential organizational impact.
- Likelihood or confidence.
- Relevant strengths or vulnerabilities.
- Recommended monitoring or response.
- Responsible owner or function for follow-up.

## Scenario implications

When appropriate:

- Maintain current course.
- Accelerate an existing initiative.
- Build an internal capability.
- Buy or contract for a capability.
- Partner or collaborate.
- Conduct a limited pilot.
- Delay pending further evidence.
- Stop or avoid an investment.

For each scenario:

- Strategic rationale.
- Benefits.
- Risks.
- Dependencies.
- Required capabilities.
- Decision criteria.
- Evidence gaps.

## Recommended executive actions

- Decisions to consider.
- Questions requiring further validation.
- Areas requiring deeper market, technical, financial, clinical, legal, privacy, security, or regulatory review.
- Competitors, peers, vendors, or partners to monitor.
- Potential outreach or benchmarking opportunities.
- Recommended monitoring cadence.
- Trigger events that should prompt reassessment.

## Source and confidence register

- Complete source list.
- Publication and access dates.
- Primary versus secondary source.
- Reliability assessment.
- Facts supported by each source.
- Conflicting claims.
- Stale or missing information.
- Inferences clearly labeled.
- Confidence level for each major conclusion.
