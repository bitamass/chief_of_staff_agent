# Inputs

## Dashboard purpose

- Dashboard title.
- Executive audience.
- Decision, oversight responsibility, or management question the dashboard should support.
- Organizational, portfolio, program, initiative, operational, or strategic scope.
- Reporting period.
- Data cutoff date.
- Refresh frequency.
- Required level of detail.
- Approved dashboard template, brand, and accessibility standards.
- Dashboard owner and accountable business owner.
- Distribution and confidentiality requirements.

## Strategic context

- Organizational mission and strategic priorities.
- Executive goals and objectives.
- Key results or success measures.
- Initiative and portfolio structure.
- Approved performance targets.
- Material commitments.
- Critical milestones and decision dates.
- Risk appetite and tolerance.
- Current leadership priorities.
- Previous dashboard for comparison.

## Measure definitions

For each metric or key performance indicator:

- Metric name.
- Executive question it answers.
- Business definition.
- Calculation formula.
- Numerator and denominator, when applicable.
- Unit of measure.
- Data source.
- Data owner.
- Reporting frequency.
- Reporting period.
- Baseline.
- Target.
- Warning threshold.
- Critical threshold.
- Direction of favorable performance.
- Dimensions or filters.
- Known exclusions.
- Data latency.
- Data-quality expectations.
- Approved status logic.

## Current performance data

- Current metric value.
- Previous-period value.
- Baseline value.
- Target value.
- Forecast.
- Variance from target.
- Trend history.
- Relevant benchmark.
- Commentary from the accountable owner.
- Explanation for material variance.
- Corrective or improvement action.
- Source timestamp.
- Confidence and validation status.

## Initiative and portfolio data

For each initiative:

- Initiative name and objective.
- Executive sponsor and owner.
- Current phase.
- Overall status.
- Scope status.
- Schedule status.
- Financial status.
- Resource status.
- Outcome or benefit status.
- Key deliverables and milestones.
- Decisions made and pending.
- Open actions and commitments.
- Dependencies.
- Risks and issues.
- Next executive checkpoint.

## Financial and resource data

When relevant:

- Approved budget.
- Actual spending.
- Forecast spending.
- Variance.
- Benefits forecast.
- Benefits realized.
- Staffing plan.
- Current staffing.
- Capacity gaps.
- Vendor commitments.
- Procurement status.
- Material funding or resource decisions.

## Risk, issue, decision, and action data

- Highest-priority risks.
- Risks above tolerance.
- Critical and high-severity issues.
- New and worsening risks or issues.
- Pending executive decisions.
- Decision owners and required dates.
- Overdue actions.
- Actions due soon.
- Escalations.
- Mitigation and recovery status.

## Stakeholder and governance data

- Upcoming governance reviews.
- Required approvals.
- Stakeholder concerns or changes in support.
- Communication commitments.
- Audit, assurance, compliance, security, privacy, clinical, legal, policy, financial, or regulatory matters.
- Exceptions requiring approval.
- Required executive engagement.

## User and presentation requirements

- Primary device and screen size.
- Required export formats.
- Need for interactive filters or drill-downs.
- Accessibility requirements.
- Color and branding constraints.
- Required print or presentation layout.
- Date, number, currency, and percentage conventions.
- Information that must be hidden or restricted by audience.

## Data-quality and governance inputs

- Source system of record.
- Data steward.
- Data freshness.
- Completeness.
- Accuracy.
- Reconciliation status.
- Known gaps or anomalies.
- Conflicting definitions.
- Manual adjustments.
- Calculation owner.
- Approval status.
- Data-classification and access requirements.
