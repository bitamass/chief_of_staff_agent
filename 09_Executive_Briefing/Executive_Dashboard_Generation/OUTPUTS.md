# Outputs

## Dashboard specification

- Dashboard purpose and audience.
- Decisions the dashboard supports.
- Reporting scope and cutoff date.
- Refresh frequency.
- Required sections.
- Data sources and owners.
- Access and confidentiality requirements.
- Export and presentation requirements.
- Assumptions and limitations.

## Executive summary panel

- Overall organizational, portfolio, or initiative status.
- Most significant change since the previous period.
- Highest-priority accomplishment.
- Most consequential risk or issue.
- Most urgent pending decision.
- Near-term milestone.
- Specific leadership action required.
- Data-confidence or freshness indicator.

## Key performance indicators

For every displayed KPI:

- Metric label.
- Current value.
- Target.
- Variance.
- Previous-period value.
- Trend.
- Status.
- Reporting period.
- Data source.
- Last updated date.
- Definition or information tooltip.
- Owner.
- Commentary for material variance.

## Strategic-priority view

- Strategic objective.
- Relevant measures.
- Current performance.
- Initiative contributions.
- Progress toward key results.
- Material gaps.
- Risks affecting the objective.
- Recommended leadership attention.

## Initiative and portfolio view

For each material initiative:

- Objective.
- Sponsor and owner.
- Overall status.
- Scope, schedule, financial, resource, and outcome status.
- Key milestone.
- Current forecast.
- Decisions pending.
- Actions due.
- Highest risk or issue.
- Next executive checkpoint.
- Source and confidence.

## Financial and resource view

When relevant:

- Approved versus actual spending.
- Forecast variance.
- Benefits expected versus realized.
- Staffing and capacity.
- Resource bottlenecks.
- Vendor or procurement dependencies.
- Required funding or resource decisions.

## Risk and issue view

- Risks above tolerance.
- Critical issues.
- Worsening trends.
- Overdue mitigation or resolution actions.
- Owners and target dates.
- Required decisions or escalation.
- Link or drill-down to supporting detail.

## Decisions and actions view

- Decisions pending.
- Decision-maker.
- Required decision date.
- Consequence of delay.
- Overdue actions.
- Actions due soon.
- Owner.
- Escalation status.

## Milestone and forward-look view

- Milestones achieved.
- Milestones due next.
- Upcoming meetings and governance reviews.
- Required approvals.
- Critical dependencies.
- Expected changes before the next dashboard refresh.

## Exception and attention panel

Display only material exceptions such as:

- Performance beyond threshold.
- Risks above tolerance.
- Critical issues.
- Missed milestones.
- Material budget variance.
- Missing ownership.
- Stale or low-confidence data.
- Conflicting status assessments.
- Decisions requiring executive action.

## Drill-down and source view

For every material visual or metric:

- Definition.
- Calculation.
- Source.
- Owner.
- Reporting period.
- Last refresh.
- Underlying records.
- Notes and limitations.
- Relevant decisions, actions, risks, or initiatives.

## Data-quality panel

- Freshness.
- Completeness.
- Reconciliation status.
- Missing sources.
- Stale metrics.
- Conflicting definitions.
- Manual adjustments.
- Low-confidence conclusions.
- Items requiring owner validation.
