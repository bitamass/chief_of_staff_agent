# Outputs

## Engagement strategy

- Concise statement of the engagement purpose and desired outcome.
- Summary of the initiative, decision, or change requiring engagement.
- Recommended engagement approach and guiding principles.
- Definition of what successful engagement would look like.
- Assumptions, constraints, and information gaps affecting the plan.

## Stakeholder prioritization

- Prioritized stakeholder list based on authority, influence, interest, impact, and urgency.
- Stakeholder classification, such as:
  - Manage closely.
  - Keep satisfied.
  - Keep informed.
  - Monitor.
- Current and desired support level for each stakeholder.
- Identification of decision-makers, approvers, influencers, implementers, affected groups, and potential blockers.
- Explanation of why each high-priority stakeholder requires attention.

## Stakeholder engagement plan

For each material stakeholder or stakeholder group:

- Engagement objective.
- Key messages and information needs.
- Requested decision, action, participation, or support.
- Recommended engagement method and communication channel.
- Recommended sender or relationship owner.
- Timing, sequencing, and frequency.
- Required preparation or supporting materials.
- Known concerns and recommended response.
- Follow-up action, owner, and due date.
- Evidence or source supporting the recommendation.

## Engagement schedule

- Sequence of meetings, briefings, consultations, and communications.
- Critical engagements required before major decisions or milestones.
- Dependencies between stakeholders or engagement activities.
- Decision dates, communication deadlines, and follow-up checkpoints.
- Recommended opportunities for executive participation.

## Risks and mitigation

- Stakeholders who may delay, oppose, redirect, or misunderstand the work.
- Conflicting stakeholder expectations or priorities.
- Communication sequencing or confidentiality risks.
- Missing representation or insufficient consultation.
- Risk of engagement fatigue or unnecessary meetings.
- Recommended prevention, mitigation, and escalation actions.

## Leadership attention

- Stakeholders requiring direct executive engagement.
- Matters requiring leadership alignment before outreach.
- Decisions or messages requiring executive approval.
- Unresolved concerns that could affect the initiative.
- Recommended next actions for the executive sponsor.

## Measurement and follow-up

- Engagement success measures.
- Indicators of support, understanding, participation, and commitment.
- Commitments and follow-up actions resulting from engagement.
- Changes in stakeholder position or influence.
- Recommended cadence for reviewing and updating the plan.
