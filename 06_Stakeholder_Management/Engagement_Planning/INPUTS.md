# Inputs

## Required inputs

- Engagement objective and the outcome leadership wants to achieve.
- Stakeholder list, including names, roles, organizations, and relationship owners.
- Stakeholder responsibilities, decision authority, influence, and level of interest.
- Initiative, program, decision, or organizational change requiring engagement.
- Current stage of the work and the decisions or milestones ahead.
- Known stakeholder positions, concerns, expectations, and information needs.
- Previous meetings, communications, agreements, decisions, and commitments.
- Required stakeholder actions, approvals, participation, or support.
- Target dates, deadlines, dependencies, and decision points.
- Executive sponsor, engagement owner, and supporting team members.

## Stakeholder assessment inputs

- Stakeholder influence: low, medium, or high.
- Stakeholder interest: low, medium, or high.
- Current level of support: opposed, concerned, neutral, supportive, or champion.
- Desired level of support or participation.
- Authority to approve, block, advise, influence, implement, or receive information.
- Relationship strength and history of engagement.
- Preferred communication channel, frequency, and level of detail.
- Known sensitivities, competing interests, or areas of disagreement.
- Connections and dependencies among stakeholders.

## Planning constraints

- Confidentiality, privacy, legal, labor, regulatory, or governance restrictions.
- Information that may or may not be shared with each stakeholder.
- Required sequencing of communications or approvals.
- Available time, staffing, budget, and executive availability.
- Accessibility, language, geographic, cultural, or scheduling considerations.
- Matters that require formal communication approval.

## Optional supporting inputs

- Stakeholder maps, organization charts, and governance structures.
- Communication plans and previous engagement strategies.
- Meeting agendas, minutes, decision logs, and action trackers.
- Initiative charters, roadmaps, risk registers, and status reports.
- Surveys, feedback, interview findings, or sentiment information.
- Relevant external events or organizational developments.
