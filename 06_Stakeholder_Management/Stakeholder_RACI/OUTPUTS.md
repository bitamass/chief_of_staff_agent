# Outputs

## RACI matrix

A responsibility matrix showing each activity, deliverable, milestone, or decision against relevant roles:

- **R — Responsible:** performs or coordinates the work.
- **A — Accountable:** owns the result and has final authority for its completion or approval.
- **C — Consulted:** provides required expertise or input before action or decision.
- **I — Informed:** receives relevant information after or during progress.

Each row should include:

- Activity, deliverable, or decision.
- Expected outcome.
- Responsible role or roles.
- One accountable role.
- Consulted roles.
- Informed roles.
- Due date or milestone.
- Source supporting the assignment.
- Confidence level or validation status.

## Role summaries

For each material role:

- Responsibilities assigned.
- Outcomes for which the role is accountable.
- Decisions the role can approve.
- Activities requiring consultation.
- Information the role must receive.
- Dependencies on other roles.
- Estimated responsibility load.
- Delegation or backup arrangements.
- Assignments requiring confirmation.

## Responsibility gaps

- Activities with no responsible role.
- Activities with no accountable owner.
- Decisions without a documented decision-maker.
- Deliverables lacking acceptance ownership.
- Required functions or perspectives not represented.
- Assignments based on incomplete or conflicting evidence.
- Responsibilities assigned to roles without appropriate authority or expertise.

## Responsibility conflicts

- Rows with more than one accountable role.
- One person or team assigned both Responsible and Accountable where independent oversight may be needed.
- Excessive numbers of Consulted parties that may delay decisions.
- Stakeholders marked Informed when consultation or approval appears necessary.
- Multiple teams performing overlapping work.
- Responsibilities inconsistent with formal governance or delegation of authority.
- Accountable roles lacking control over the required resources.

## Workload and concentration analysis

- Roles with excessive Responsible assignments.
- Roles with excessive Accountable assignments.
- Critical work concentrated in one individual or team.
- Missing backups for essential responsibilities.
- Potential capacity or scheduling conflicts.
- Recommendations to rebalance assignments.

## Governance and escalation recommendations

- Recommended owner for unresolved responsibilities.
- Responsibility assignments requiring executive confirmation.
- Conflicts requiring governance review.
- Proposed escalation path.
- Proposed cadence for reviewing and updating the RACI.
- Trigger events that should prompt reassessment.

## Executive summary

- Major ownership gaps.
- Material overlaps or conflicts.
- Decisions requiring clearer accountability.
- Overloaded or underrepresented roles.
- Most important validation questions.
- Recommended actions before work proceeds.
