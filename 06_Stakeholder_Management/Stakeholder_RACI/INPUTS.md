# Inputs

## Required scope inputs

- Initiative, program, project, operational process, decision, or organizational change.
- Purpose and intended use of the RACI.
- Scope boundaries and items explicitly out of scope.
- Initiative charter, objectives, deliverables, and expected outcomes.
- Current phase, timeline, milestones, and decision points.
- Existing governance, reporting, and approval structures.
- Executive sponsor and accountable business or operational owner.

## Work and deliverable inputs

- List of deliverables, decisions, activities, milestones, and recurring responsibilities.
- Description and acceptance criteria for each deliverable.
- Work breakdown structure, roadmap, or implementation plan.
- Required reviews, approvals, consultations, and handoffs.
- Dependencies between activities and deliverables.
- Required dates, deadlines, and reporting cadence.
- Escalation and exception-management requirements.
- Activities that involve shared responsibility across teams.

## Stakeholder inputs

For each person, role, team, committee, or organization:

- Name or role.
- Title and organizational unit.
- Formal responsibilities.
- Decision and approval authority.
- Subject-matter expertise.
- Operational responsibilities.
- Required contribution to each deliverable or decision.
- Availability, capacity, and known constraints.
- Delegation or backup authority.
- Governance, legal, privacy, security, clinical, finance, procurement, labor, or compliance responsibilities.

## Existing responsibility evidence

- Initiative or program charter.
- Job descriptions and organizational charts.
- Governance documents and committee charters.
- Statements of work, contracts, or service agreements.
- Meeting notes, decisions, and previous responsibility assignments.
- Existing RACI, RASCI, DACI, RAPID, or responsibility matrices.
- Action-item trackers and ownership records.
- Policies, procedures, and regulatory requirements.
- Approved delegation-of-authority documentation.

## Validation inputs

- Known gaps, overlaps, disputes, or unclear ownership.
- Deliverables without an identified owner.
- Decisions without a documented approval authority.
- Activities assigned to multiple accountable owners.
- Stakeholders who may be overallocated.
- Required review or approval participants who may be missing.
- Escalation path for unresolved responsibility conflicts.
