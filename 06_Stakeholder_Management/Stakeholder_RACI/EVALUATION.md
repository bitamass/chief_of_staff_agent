# Evaluation — Stakeholder RACI

The result passes when:

- It answers the specific stakeholder raci question.
- Every material claim is traceable to dated evidence.
- The decision and delivery RACI has the required fields and accountable owners.
- Unknowns, conflicts, and assumptions are explicit.
- Recommendations are practical and clearly separated from facts.
- Avoid speculative personal judgments; label assumptions and use role-based evidence.
