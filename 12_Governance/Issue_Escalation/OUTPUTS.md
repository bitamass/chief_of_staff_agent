## Issue classification

Classify the issue as:

- Under validation.
- Confirmed.
- Contained.
- Resolution in progress.
- Monitoring.
- Escalated.
- Specialized response required.
- Resolved.
- Closed.
- Reopened.
- Reclassified as risk, blocker, dependency, or incident.
- Insufficient evidence.

Explain the basis for the classification.

## Escalation-level recommendation

Potential escalation levels:

- Level 0 — Manage within the owning team.
- Level 1 — Program or operational lead attention.
- Level 2 — Sponsor or cross-functional leadership attention.
- Level 3 — Executive or governance intervention.
- Level 4 — Specialized incident, crisis, regulatory, legal, clinical, security, privacy, safety, or continuity response.

For the recommended level:

- Rationale.
- Trigger met.
- Authority required.
- Recipient.
- Response deadline.
- Requested action.
- Next escalation trigger.
- Confidence.

Use approved institutional definitions when available.
