# Inputs

## Issue context

- Organization, portfolio, program, initiative, project, service, system, or operational area.
- Issue ID and title.
- Issue description.
- Date identified.
- Person or role identifying the issue.
- Issue owner.
- Executive sponsor.
- Current status.
- Reporting cutoff date.
- Source and last update date.
- Confidentiality classification.

## Issue classification

- Issue category:
  - Strategic.
  - Operational.
  - Financial.
  - Schedule or delivery.
  - Clinical or patient-related.
  - Research.
  - Technology or architecture.
  - Cybersecurity.
  - Privacy or data governance.
  - Legal, regulatory, policy, or compliance.
  - Workforce or labor.
  - Procurement, vendor, or contract.
  - Stakeholder or communication.
  - Quality or accessibility.
  - Safety, continuity, or service availability.
- Severity.
- Urgency.
- Scope.
- Number or type of stakeholders affected.
- Whether the issue is isolated or systemic.
- Whether a specialized incident or response process applies.

- What has occurred.
- Current impact.
- Date and time of occurrence.
- Duration.
- Systems, services, operations, or outcomes affected.
- Evidence.
- Source owner.
- Facts confirmed.
- Facts unconfirmed.
- Conflicting information.
- Assumptions.
- Confidence level.
- Current data cutoff time.

## Impact assessment

- Strategic impact.
- Operational impact.
- Financial impact.

- Continuity impact.
- Current consequence.
- Potential future consequence.

## Containment and immediate response

- Containment action taken.
- Date and time.
- Response owner.
- Current effectiveness.
- Temporary workaround.
- Limitations.
- New risks introduced.

## Root-cause and contributing-factor information

- Immediate cause.
- Confirmed root cause.
- Suspected root cause.
- Contributing factors.
- Control or process failure.
- Dependency failure.
- Human, technical, organizational, vendor, or environmental factors.
- Evidence.
- Root-cause-analysis owner.
- Target completion date.
- Confidence level.

Avoid unnecessary personal attribution or unsupported blame.

## Data-quality inputs

- Missing owner.
- Missing severity or urgency.
- Unsupported impact claims.
- Stale status.
- Conflicting reports.
- Issue incorrectly recorded as a risk or blocker.
- Escalation without documented rationale.
- Closure without evidence.
- Sensitive information included unnecessarily.
- Confidence level.
- Items requiring specialized or human validation.
