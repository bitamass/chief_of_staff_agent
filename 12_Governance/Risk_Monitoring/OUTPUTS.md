# Outputs

## Executive risk summary

- Overall risk posture for the selected initiative, program, or operating area.
- Material changes since the previous review.
- Risks requiring immediate attention, decision, or escalation.
- Concise explanation of why each highlighted risk matters now.

## Risk monitoring dashboard

For each monitored risk:

- Risk identifier and title.
- Risk category and accountable owner.
- Current inherent and residual risk ratings.
- Previous rating and direction of change: increasing, stable, decreasing, or undetermined.
- Key risk indicators and threshold status.
- Control and mitigation status.
- Next review date.
- Current confidence level and source references.

## Threshold and exception report

- Indicators that have crossed a warning or escalation threshold.
- Control failures, overdue mitigations, or missing evidence.
- Emerging conditions that could materially change exposure.
- Risks without an owner, response plan, review date, or approved acceptance.
- Conflicting assessments or incomplete information requiring validation.

## Trend and dependency analysis

- Patterns across risk indicators and reporting periods.
- Risks affected by shared dependencies, milestones, resources, vendors, or decisions.
- Potential concentration or cascading effects across initiatives.
- Explanation of changes attributable to new evidence, completed mitigation, or revised assumptions.

## Recommended management actions

- Specific mitigation, investigation, validation, or contingency actions.
- Recommended owner and completion date for each action.
- Required approvals, resources, or cross-functional participation.
- Suggested monitoring frequency based on current exposure.
- Conditions that should trigger escalation or reassessment.

## Executive decisions required

- Decision to accept, reduce, avoid, transfer, or further investigate a risk.
- Approval of mitigation funding, resources, schedule changes, or contingency activation.
- Confirmation of risk ownership or escalation authority.
- Decision deadline and consequence of delay.
- Supporting evidence and unresolved uncertainty.

## Source and quality report

- Sources used for each material conclusion.
- Date and freshness of the evidence.
- Missing, inconsistent, or unverified information.
- Confidence level for each significant assessment.
- Items requiring human confirmation before distribution or action.
