# Inputs

## Risk context and scope

- Initiative, program, service, operational area, or decision being monitored.
- Business objective, expected outcome, and relevant success measures.
- Monitoring period, review frequency, and reporting audience.
- Applicable risk categories, thresholds, policies, and governance requirements.

## Risk register information

For each risk:

- Unique risk identifier and descriptive title.
- Clear risk statement using the structure: cause, uncertain event, and potential impact.
- Risk category, such as strategic, operational, financial, technology, security, privacy, compliance, workforce, clinical, reputational, or vendor.
- Date identified, source, and current status.
- Inherent likelihood and impact before controls.
- Residual likelihood and impact after existing controls.
- Overall risk rating and scoring method.
- Risk owner and accountable executive.
- Control owner, mitigation owner, and supporting participants.

## Indicators and monitoring evidence

- Key risk indicators and their definitions.
- Current indicator values, prior values, trends, and measurement dates.
- Warning and escalation thresholds.
- Control-performance results and exceptions.
- Incidents, issues, audit findings, complaints, delays, or service disruptions.
- Milestone, budget, resource, dependency, and schedule information.
- Relevant decisions, assumptions, actions, and unresolved blockers.
- Supporting reports, meeting notes, dashboards, and source documents.

## Mitigation and response information

- Current response strategy: avoid, reduce, transfer, accept, or monitor.
- Approved mitigation actions, owners, due dates, and status.
- Expected reduction in likelihood or impact.
- Contingency actions and activation conditions.
- Dependencies, resource requirements, and approval needs.
- Evidence that completed actions have reduced exposure.
- Any accepted residual risk and the approving authority.

## Governance and escalation information

- Risk appetite and tolerance statements.
- Required review or reporting cadence.
- Decision rights and escalation pathways.
- Conditions requiring executive, governance, security, privacy, clinical, legal, compliance, or operational review.
- Previous escalations, decisions, and accepted-risk determinations.
- Confidentiality, data classification, and distribution restrictions.

## Data-quality requirements

- Source and date for every material risk claim.
- Clear distinction between confirmed facts, estimates, assumptions, and interpretations.
- Identification of missing, conflicting, outdated, or unverified information.
- Confidence level for risk scores and trend assessments.
- Named person responsible for validating material changes.
