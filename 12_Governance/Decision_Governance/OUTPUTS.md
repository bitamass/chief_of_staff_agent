# Outputs

## Decision-governance summary

- Decision requested.
- Decision category and level.
- Authorized decision-maker or body.
- Required governance pathway.
- Options considered.
- Recommended option.
- Material risks and trade-offs.
- Required consultations and reviews.
- Decision deadline.
- Current readiness.
- Missing evidence.
- Confidence and limitations.

## Decision-rights matrix

For the decision:

- Requester.
- Decision owner.
- Responsible preparer.
- Required consultees.
- Subject-matter reviewers.
- Recommending authority.
- Approving authority.
- Implementation authority.
- Risk-acceptance authority.
- Informed stakeholders.
- Escalation body.
- Source of authority.

## Governance pathway

Show:

Decision request  
→ Classification  
→ Evidence preparation  
→ Required consultations  
→ Specialized reviews  
→ Recommendation  
→ Authorized deliberation  
→ Decision  
→ Conditions and implementation  
→ Monitoring and outcome review  
→ Renewal, modification, or closure

For every stage:

- Purpose.
- Owner.
- Reviewer or decision body.
- Required inputs.
- Entry criteria.
- Exit criteria.
- Target date.
- Dependencies.
- Current status.
- Escalation route.

## Decision-readiness assessment

Assess whether the package contains:

- Clear decision statement.
- Authorized decision-maker.
- Defined scope.
- Strategic rationale.
- Credible options.
- Evaluation criteria.
- Cost and resource analysis.
- Risk assessment.
- Required specialized reviews.
- Stakeholder analysis.
- Implementation plan.
- Success measures.
- Monitoring plan.
- Source-linked evidence.
- Identified assumptions and gaps.

Classify readiness as:

- Not ready.
- Early development.
- Partially ready.
- Ready with gaps.
- Ready for decision.
- Requires specialized review.
- Requires escalation.

## Governance gaps and conflicts

- Unclear authority.
- Missing quorum or voting record.
- Required review omitted.
- Conflicting committee mandates.
- Decision made outside the approved pathway.
- Recommendation represented as approval.
- Conditional approval represented as unrestricted.
- Implementation before authorization.
- Decision without ownership.
- Missing monitoring or review.
- Material change without re-review.

## Executive attention

- Authority conflict.
- Material risk requiring acceptance.
- Decision package not ready.
- Time-sensitive decision.
- Cross-organizational disagreement.
- Missing specialized review.
- Required escalation.
- Decision-maker and deadline.

- Assumptions.
- Low-confidence conclusions.
- Items requiring human validation.
