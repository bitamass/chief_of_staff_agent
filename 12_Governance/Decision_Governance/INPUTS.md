# Inputs

## Governance scope

- Organization, portfolio, program, initiative, policy, investment, technology, data use, or operational area.
- Governance purpose.
- Decision category.
- Intended decision outcome.
- Executive sponsor.
- Accountable decision owner.
- Required decision date.
- Reporting cutoff date.
- Applicable governance framework.
- Items explicitly out of scope.
- Authoritative source documents.

## Decision request

- Decision ID and title.
- Complete decision statement.
- Problem or opportunity.
- Decision requested.
- Requesting person, team, or organizational unit.
- Decision owner.
- Proposed implementation owner.
- Scope.
- Expected outcome.
- Urgency.
- Consequence of delay.
- Source and submission date.

## Decision classification

- Decision type:
  - Strategic.
  - Investment or funding.
  - Portfolio or priority.
  - Policy.
  - Operational.
  - Clinical or research.
  - Technology or architecture.
  - Data or information governance.
  - Security or privacy.
  - Legal or regulatory.
  - Procurement, vendor, or contract.
  - Workforce or organizational.
  - Risk acceptance or exception.
  - Communication or public position.
- Decision level.
- Reversibility.
- Time horizon.
- Materiality.
- Impacted organizational units.
- Whether precedent may be created.
- Whether an existing decision or approval applies.

## Decision authority

- Authorized decision-maker.
- Source of authority.
- Delegation of authority.
- Approval threshold.
- Quorum or voting requirement.
- Required chair or sponsor.
- Recommending authority.
- Reviewing authority.
- Implementation authority.
- Risk-acceptance authority.
- Escalation body.
- Effective and expiration dates.
- Conflicts or uncertainty regarding authority.

## Governance bodies

For each relevant board, committee, council, steering group, or review body:

- Name.
- Charter.
- Sponsoring authority.
- Chair.
- Membership.
- Decision, approval, recommendation, advisory, or oversight role.
- Scope.
- Meeting cadence.
- Submission deadline.
- Quorum and voting rules.
- Escalation authority.
- Records requirements.
- Effective date and charter version.

## Options and alternatives

For each option:

- Option title.
- Description.
- Expected benefits.
- Costs.
- Resource requirements.
- Implementation time.
- Dependencies.
- Risks.
- Reversibility.
- Stakeholder impact.
- Compliance and governance implications.
- Opportunity cost.
- Assumptions.
- Supporting evidence.
- Confidence level.

Include maintaining the current state when it is a credible option.

## Evaluation criteria

- Strategic alignment.
- Expected outcome or value.
- Financial impact.
- Operational feasibility.
- Technical feasibility.
- Clinical or research impact.
- Security and privacy.
- Legal, regulatory, policy, and compliance.
- Workforce and stakeholder impact.
- Accessibility and equity.
- Risk and resilience.
- Implementation readiness.
- Time to value.
- Reversibility.
- Evidence quality.
- Approved criteria weights, when applicable.

## Business and financial information

- Business case.
- Strategic objective supported.
- Expected outcomes.
- Success measures.
- Funding source.
- Initial cost.
- Ongoing cost.
- Forecast value.
- Benefits owner.
- Resource requirements.
- Opportunity cost.
- Financial assumptions.
- Required finance or budget approval.

## Risk and assurance information

- Risks and issues.
- Inherent and residual ratings.
- Risk owner.
- Existing controls.
- Mitigation.
- Contingency.
- Required security, privacy, legal, clinical, research, accessibility, regulatory, finance, procurement, architecture, policy, records-management, or ethics review.
- Specialized findings.
- Exceptions.
- Residual risk requiring acceptance.
- Monitoring and incident obligations.

## Stakeholder information

- Stakeholders affected.
- Decision participants.
- Required consultees.
- Subject-matter experts.
- Current positions or concerns.
- Expected effect of each option.
- Engagement completed.
- Engagement still required.
- Conflicts of interest.
- Dissenting perspectives.
- Communication requirements.

## Evidence and source information

- Decision brief.
- Charter.
- Analysis.
- Financial model.
- Technical assessment.
- Risk assessment.
- Legal or policy review.
- Stakeholder evidence.
- Research or benchmark information.
- Prior decisions.
- Source owner.
- Date.
- Version.
- Approval status.
- Confidence and limitations.

## Meeting and deliberation information

- Governance meeting.
- Date.
- Agenda item.
- Materials submitted.
- Participants.
- Quorum.
- Conflicts disclosed.
- Questions raised.
- Options discussed.
- Additional evidence requested.
- Recommendation.
- Motion or resolution.
- Vote or consensus record.
- Deferral or escalation.
- Approved minutes.

## Decision conditions

- Conditions required before implementation.
- Scope restrictions.
- Safeguards.
- Funding conditions.
- Required approvals.
- Required monitoring.
- Reporting obligations.
- Review date.
- Expiration date.
- Material-change triggers.
- Conditions requiring suspension or withdrawal.
- Owner for each condition.

## Implementation and accountability information

- Implementation owner.
- Responsible teams.
- Actions.
- Milestones.
- Due dates.
- Resources.
- Communications.
- Operational owner.
- Monitoring owner.
- Success measures.
- Outcome-review date.
- Escalation path.
- Evidence required for closure.

## Decision history

- Related prior decisions.
- Superseded decisions.
- Previous approvals.
- Conditions.
- Implementation results.
- Outcome evidence.
- Lessons learned.
- Changes in strategy, policy, technology, data, or risk.
- Current applicability.

## Data-quality inputs

- Missing decision authority.
- Missing or incomplete options.
- Unsupported recommendation.
- Missing risk or stakeholder review.
- Draft discussion represented as a decision.
- Conditional approval represented as unrestricted.
- Conflicting governance records.
- Missing source or decision record.
- Stale evidence.
- Confidence level.
- Items requiring governance, legal, policy, or specialized validation.
