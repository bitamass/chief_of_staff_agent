Background Memo — Technology Investment Review

The proposed two-phase initiative would consolidate approved operational data into an executive analytics experience. Phase one would focus on a limited set of service-line measures and would avoid clinical decision-making functionality.

Key risks include incomplete integration estimates, unclear benefit ownership, workforce adoption, vendor dependency, data-access permissions, cybersecurity review, and the possibility that reporting improvements do not translate into operational action.

Suggested investment measures include executive preparation time saved, reduction in manual reconciliation, decision turnaround time, user-validated data accuracy, total operating cost, and benefit realization at 90 and 180 days.
