AI Governance Steering Discussion — Sample Agenda

Objective: Review the proposed AI-agent governance approach and determine whether to sponsor a limited executive meeting-preparation pilot.

Agenda:
1. Confirm the business problem and intended users.
2. Review minimum safeguards for agent registration, permissions, monitoring, and source traceability.
3. Decide whether to approve a time-boxed pilot using sample data.
4. Identify an executive sponsor, operational owner, success measures, and next review date.

Decision requested: Approve or decline a limited pilot and identify the accountable pilot owner.
