AI Agent Governance Discussion — Sample Agenda

Objective: Explore how agent governance could apply to an executive meeting-preparation use case and identify the questions that would require further consideration.

Agenda:
1. Confirm the business problem and intended users.
2. Review minimum safeguards for agent registration, permissions, monitoring, and source traceability.
3. Discuss a limited meeting-preparation demonstration as one possible use case.
4. Identify the roles, safeguards, and evaluation questions that further exploration would require.

Discussion outcome: Identify which questions merit further exploration; no approval is requested.
