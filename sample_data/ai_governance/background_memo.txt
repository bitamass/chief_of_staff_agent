Background Memo — Executive Meeting Intelligence Demonstration

This exploratory prototype prepares a concise executive brief containing meeting context, prior commitments, unresolved issues, decisions needed, recommended questions, next actions, and source references.

This demonstration uses curated sample documents and does not connect to production clinical or university systems. Any future institutional integration would require authentication, role-based permissions, audit logging, privacy and security review, records-management review, governance approval, and a clearly accountable service owner.

Possible evaluation measures include preparation time saved, factual accuracy, percentage of important claims linked to a source, executive usefulness, and the number of corrections required during human review.
