Background Memo — Executive Meeting Intelligence Pilot

The proposed prototype prepares a concise executive brief containing meeting context, prior commitments, unresolved issues, decisions needed, recommended questions, next actions, and source references.

The first demonstration must use sample documents supplied directly by the user and must not connect to production clinical or university systems. Any future institutional integration would require authentication, role-based permissions, audit logging, privacy and security review, records-management review, governance approval, and a clearly accountable service owner.

Suggested pilot measures include preparation time saved, factual accuracy, percentage of important claims linked to a source, executive usefulness, and the number of corrections required during human review.
