UW Medicine Digital Operations Risk Review — Sample Agenda

Objective: Assess readiness for a limited clinical-support technology pilot and decide whether operational, security, and continuity risks are sufficiently controlled.

Agenda:
1. Review pilot scope, intended users, and prohibited uses.
2. Confirm access controls, monitoring, downtime procedures, and escalation paths.
3. Determine go/no-go thresholds and rollback authority.
4. Identify the service owner, clinical safety owner, and next readiness review.

Decision requested: Approve, conditionally approve, or pause the pilot pending additional risk controls.
