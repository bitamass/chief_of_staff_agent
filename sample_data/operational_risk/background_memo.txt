Background Memo — Digital Operations Risk Review

The proposed pilot would provide non-diagnostic workflow support in one operational unit. It would use approved test data during validation and would not autonomously take clinical action.

Required controls include role-based permissions, audit logging, cybersecurity testing, human review, downtime procedures, monitoring for incorrect output, incident escalation, and a documented rollback plan. The service owner must coordinate with clinical safety, privacy, security, and operations.

Suggested readiness measures include response accuracy, correction frequency, service availability, incident response time, workflow impact, user-reported safety concerns, and successful completion of a pre-launch tabletop exercise.
