Prior Discussion Notes

Clinical and technology leaders supported a limited pilot only if it remains advisory and does not replace professional judgment. The group agreed that human review, clear escalation paths, and rapid rollback capability are required.

Key concerns included incorrect or delayed output, inappropriate access, workflow disruption, unclear incident authority, and service continuity during downtime. Patient safety requested explicit communication and monitoring measures before launch.

No final decision was made about launch readiness, pilot duration, incident thresholds, or who has authority to pause the service.
