# Evaluation — Align Deliverables With Strategic Goals

The result passes when:

- It answers the specific align deliverables with strategic goals question.
- Every material claim is traceable to dated evidence.
- The strategic alignment traceability matrix has the required fields and accountable owners.
- Unknowns, conflicts, and assumptions are explicit.
- Recommendations are practical and clearly separated from facts.
- Do not invent priorities or silently override accountable owners.
