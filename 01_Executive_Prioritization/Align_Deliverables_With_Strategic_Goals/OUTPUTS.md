# Outputs — Align Deliverables With Strategic Goals

## Executive summary

Provide a concise assessment of whether the current initiative deliverables collectively support the approved strategic goals and top business priorities.

Highlight:

* Areas of strong strategic alignment
* Strategic goals that are insufficiently supported
* Deliverables with weak or unclear value
* Major conflicts, dependencies, or capacity concerns
* Decisions requiring executive attention

## Strategic-goal evaluation

For each strategic goal, provide:

* Strategic goal and owner
* Related business priorities
* Supporting initiatives
* Number of associated deliverables
* Strength of overall deliverable coverage
* Coverage gaps or unnecessary duplication
* Evidence and confidence level

## Deliverable alignment matrix

For each deliverable, provide:

* Deliverable name
* Associated initiative
* Deliverable owner
* Due date and current status
* Strategic goal supported
* Strategic-alignment score from 1 to 5
* Business-priority contribution score from 1 to 5
* Expected-value score from 1 to 5
* Urgency score from 1 to 5
* Dependency-importance score from 1 to 5
* Feasibility score from 1 to 5
* Weighted overall score
* Evidence and scoring rationale
* Known risks, dependencies, or constraints

## Prioritized deliverable list

Rank deliverables from highest to lowest overall score.

For every deliverable, recommend one of the following:

* **Keep:** Continue as currently planned.
* **Accelerate:** Increase priority or deliver earlier.
* **Revise:** Change the scope, timing, owner, or success criteria.
* **Defer:** Postpone until a dependency, resource, or higher priority is addressed.
* **Combine:** Consolidate with an overlapping deliverable.
* **Remove:** Discontinue because strategic alignment or expected value is insufficient.
* **Escalate:** Request executive review because of a conflict, risk, or missing decision.

## Strategic coverage gaps

Identify:

* Strategic goals with no supporting deliverables
* Business priorities with insufficient initiative coverage
* Deliverables that do not clearly support an approved goal
* Duplicated or competing deliverables
* Missing owners, measures, dependencies, or completion criteria

## Executive decisions and recommendations

Provide:

* Decisions needed from leadership
* Deliverables recommended for acceleration, revision, deferral, combination, removal, or escalation
* Resource or sequencing adjustments
* Owners and proposed completion dates for follow-up actions
* Evidence gaps that must be resolved before a final decision

## Human-review requirement

All scores and recommendations must be reviewed by an authorized person before priorities, resources, deliverables, owners, or timelines are changed.
