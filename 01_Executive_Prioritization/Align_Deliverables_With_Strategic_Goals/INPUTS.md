
# Inputs — Align Deliverables With Strategic Goals

## Required inputs

* Approved strategic goals, including each goal’s description, owner, success measures, and time horizon.
* Current top business priorities, including priority level and responsible executive.
* List of active initiatives, including each initiative’s objective, owner, status, expected outcome, and associated strategic goal, if already identified.
* List of deliverables for each initiative, including deliverable name, owner, due date, status, expected value, and completion criteria.
* Known dependencies, constraints, risks, resource requirements, and mandatory commitments affecting each deliverable.
* Approved strategic-alignment scoring criteria and weights.

## Recommended scoring criteria

Each deliverable should be evaluated from 1 to 5 against:

* Strategic-goal alignment
* Contribution to top business priorities
* Expected organizational value
* Urgency or consequence of delay
* Dependency or prerequisite importance
* Feasibility within current capacity

## Optional supporting inputs

* Initiative charters and business cases
* Portfolio or program status reports
* Prior prioritization or funding decisions
* Relevant executive meeting notes
* Benefit-realization measures
* Budget and resource-capacity information
* Regulatory, compliance, or operational requirements

## Input validation

Before evaluating deliverables:

* Confirm that strategic goals and priorities are current and approved.
* Confirm the owner, source, effective date, and status of each input.
* Identify deliverables that are missing an initiative, owner, due date, expected value, or completion criteria.
* Flag conflicting, outdated, incomplete, or unverified information.
* Do not infer strategic alignment when supporting evidence is unavailable.
* Use only synthetic or explicitly approved information in the demonstration.
