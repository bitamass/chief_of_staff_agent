# Inputs — Key Decision Identification

## Required inputs

* Current strategic goals and executive priorities.
* Active initiatives, programs, projects, and major commitments.
* Upcoming meeting agendas and meeting objectives.
* Recent meeting notes, discussion summaries, and executive correspondence.
* Prior decisions and decision logs.
* Open action items, commitments, and unresolved follow-ups.
* Initiative milestones, deliverables, dependencies, and deadlines.
* Known risks, issues, constraints, and escalation items.
* Pending proposals, recommendations, requests, and approvals.
* Identified decision owners, approvers, advisors, and affected stakeholders.

## Decision context

For each possible decision, collect:

* Decision statement or question.
* Reason the decision is needed.
* Initiative, goal, deliverable, or issue affected.
* Decision owner and approval authority.
* Stakeholders who must be consulted or informed.
* Available options.
* Benefits, costs, risks, and trade-offs for each option.
* Dependencies and prerequisite decisions.
* Supporting evidence and source.
* Decision deadline.
* Consequence of delay or no decision.
* Current status: emerging, ready, blocked, deferred, escalated, or decided.

## Decision signals to review

Look for:

* Requests for approval, direction, authorization, or funding.
* Multiple options without a selected direction.
* Work blocked by unresolved leadership guidance.
* Upcoming milestones dependent on a decision.
* Repeatedly deferred questions.
* Conflicting stakeholder recommendations.
* Unresolved ownership, scope, funding, staffing, or timing.
* Risks that require acceptance, mitigation, avoidance, or escalation.
* Changes that exceed an owner’s delegated authority.
* Decisions discussed in meetings but not formally documented.
* Actions that imply a decision was made without a recorded approval.
* Previously made decisions that may need reconsideration because conditions changed.

## Optional supporting inputs

* Business cases and initiative charters.
* Financial and resource analyses.
* Option assessments and trade-off analyses.
* Risk, security, privacy, compliance, clinical, or architecture reviews.
* Governance and approval requirements.
* Delegation-of-authority documentation.
* Stakeholder feedback.
* External deadlines, mandates, or policy changes.
* Historical decisions involving similar circumstances.

## Input validation

Before identifying key decisions:

* Confirm whether the matter requires a decision, information, discussion, or action.
* Verify the authorized decision owner and approval path.
* Determine whether the decision has already been made.
* Confirm that prior decisions remain current and applicable.
* Identify missing options, evidence, stakeholders, dependencies, or deadlines.
* Separate documented decisions from assumptions or implied direction.
* Flag conflicting, incomplete, outdated, or unverified information.
* Do not interpret general discussion or stakeholder preference as an approved decision.
