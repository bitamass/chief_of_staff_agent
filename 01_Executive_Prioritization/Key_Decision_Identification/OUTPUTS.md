# Outputs — Key Decision Identification

## Executive decision summary

Provide a concise overview of:

* Decisions requiring immediate leadership attention.
* Decisions approaching a deadline.
* Decisions blocking initiatives, deliverables, or commitments.
* Decisions that are not ready because evidence or consultation is missing.
* Previously made decisions that may require confirmation or reconsideration.

## Key decision register

For each identified decision, provide:

* Decision title.
* Clear decision statement or question.
* Reason the decision is needed.
* Affected strategic goal, initiative, deliverable, risk, or issue.
* Decision owner and approval authority.
* Required contributors or stakeholders.
* Decision deadline.
* Consequence of delay or no decision.
* Current status:

  * Emerging.
  * In development.
  * Ready for decision.
  * Blocked.
  * Deferred.
  * Escalated.
  * Decided.
  * Requires reconsideration.
* Supporting evidence and source.
* Confidence level: high, medium, or low.

## Decision-readiness assessment

For each decision, assess whether the following are available:

* Clearly defined decision question.
* Confirmed decision owner.
* Required options.
* Benefits and expected outcomes.
* Costs and resource implications.
* Risks and trade-offs.
* Stakeholder input.
* Dependencies and prerequisite decisions.
* Supporting evidence.
* Decision deadline.

Classify decision readiness as:

* **Ready:** Required information and authority are available.
* **Partially ready:** A decision could be made, but material information is incomplete.
* **Not ready:** Essential evidence, options, authority, or consultation is missing.

## Options and trade-offs

When supported by the inputs, present for each decision:

* Available options.
* Benefits of each option.
* Costs and resource implications.
* Risks and potential consequences.
* Dependencies.
* Reversibility.
* Consequence of taking no action.

Do not invent options or recommend a preferred option without supporting evidence.

## Prioritized decision agenda

Organize decisions into:

* **Decide now:** Immediate decision required.
* **Prepare for decision:** Additional analysis or consultation is needed.
* **Monitor:** A decision may be required if defined conditions occur.
* **Confirm or document:** Direction appears to exist but has not been formally recorded.
* **Reconsider:** A prior decision may no longer fit current conditions.

Explain the reason for each placement.

## Recommended preparation actions

For decisions that are not ready, identify:

* Missing information or evidence.
* Required analysis.
* Stakeholders to consult.
* Dependencies to resolve.
* Responsible owner.
* Target completion date.
* Recommended decision forum or upcoming meeting.

## Decision conflicts and dependencies

Identify:

* Decisions dependent on other unresolved decisions.
* Conflicting decisions or leadership direction.
* Multiple decisions competing for the same resources.
* Decisions affecting more than one initiative.
* Actions proceeding without documented authorization.
* Prior decisions that conflict with current priorities or evidence.

## Information gaps and confidence

Identify:

* Missing decision owners or approval authority.
* Incomplete or unverified options.
* Missing financial, resource, risk, or stakeholder analysis.
* Unclear deadlines or consequences.
* Discussion interpreted as direction without formal confirmation.
* Confidence level for each major finding.

## Human-review requirement

Authorized leadership must verify the decision question, decision owner, options, evidence, and readiness status before the output is used to approve a direction or communicate a decision.

This skill identifies and prepares decisions; it does not make, approve, or record decisions on behalf of leadership.
