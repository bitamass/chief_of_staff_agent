# Priority Conflict Detection

## Purpose
Perform **priority conflict detection** as a distinct executive workflow—not as a generic summary.

## Skill logic
1. Define and weight value, urgency, effort, risk, and dependency criteria.
2. Score each candidate from 1 to 5 and explain the evidence.
3. Test the ranking against capacity, conflicts, and mandatory constraints.
4. Recommend what to advance, sequence, defer, delegate, or stop.

## Operating rules
- Use INPUTS.md as the evidence contract.
- Produce every item in OUTPUTS.md.
- Cite material findings and explain every score.
- Separate facts, assumptions, analysis, and recommendations.
- Require human review before distribution, approval, reassignment, or action.
