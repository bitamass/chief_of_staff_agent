# Inputs

## Dashboard definition

- Dashboard title, purpose, and reporting period.
- Initiative, program, portfolio, operational area, or organizational scope.
- Intended audience and decisions the dashboard should support.
- Reporting cadence and data cutoff date.
- Approved risk taxonomy, scoring model, thresholds, and status definitions.
- Risk appetite, tolerance, and escalation criteria.
- Previous dashboard or reporting-period snapshot for comparison.
- Dashboard owner and responsible data providers.

## Risk records

For each risk:

- Risk ID and title.
- Risk statement using cause–event–impact structure.
- Initiative, workstream, objective, deliverable, or operation affected.
- Risk category.
- Date identified.
- Risk owner.
- Probability.
- Impact.
- Inherent risk rating before controls or mitigation.
- Existing controls.
- Control effectiveness.
- Mitigation actions.
- Target mitigation date.
- Residual risk rating after controls and mitigation.
- Current trend: improving, stable, or worsening.
- Trigger or early-warning indicator.
- Escalation status.
- Source and last review date.

## Issue records

For each issue:

- Issue ID and title.
- Description of the condition that has occurred.
- Date identified.
- Initiative, workstream, objective, deliverable, or operation affected.
- Issue category.
- Issue owner.
- Severity and urgency.
- Current impact.
- Root cause, if established.
- Containment action.
- Resolution plan.
- Target resolution date.
- Current status.
- Dependencies.
- Escalation status.
- Source and last review date.

## Impact data

Where relevant:

- Strategic impact.
- Operational impact.
- Financial impact.
- Schedule or delivery impact.
- Clinical or patient-related impact.
- Technology or integration impact.
- Data, cybersecurity, privacy, or records-management impact.
- Legal, regulatory, policy, or compliance impact.
- Workforce or labor impact.
- Stakeholder or reputational impact.
- Continuity, safety, or service-availability impact.
- Best-case, expected, and worst-case consequences, when supported.

## Control and mitigation data

- Existing preventive, detective, and corrective controls.
- Control owner.
- Evidence that the control exists and operates.
- Control gaps or failures.
- Planned mitigation or corrective actions.
- Action owner and due date.
- Required funding, resources, or decisions.
- Dependency on another team, vendor, system, or approval.
- Expected change in probability or impact.
- Risk-acceptance or exception documentation.

## Governance and escalation data

- Required review forum or governance body.
- Escalation thresholds.
- Risk-acceptance authority.
- Issue-resolution authority.
- Decisions already made.
- Decisions still required.
- Open actions and commitments.
- Regulatory, legal, clinical, security, privacy, financial, or audit obligations.
- Required reporting or notification deadlines.
- Confidentiality and distribution restrictions.

## Data-quality inputs

- Source systems, documents, or owners.
- Date of latest update.
- Stale-record threshold.
- Missing owners, dates, ratings, or mitigations.
- Conflicting risk assessments.
- Duplicate or overlapping records.
- Risks incorrectly recorded as issues, or issues recorded as risks.
- Confidence level and items requiring validation.
