# Outputs

## Executive dashboard summary

- Total active risks.
- Total open issues.
- Risks above tolerance.
- Critical and high-severity issues.
- New risks and issues during the reporting period.
- Closed risks and resolved issues.
- Risks and issues with worsening trends.
- Overdue mitigation and resolution actions.
- Items requiring executive decision, acceptance, or escalation.
- Overall change from the previous reporting period.

## Risk dashboard

For each material risk:

- Risk ID and title.
- Cause–event–impact statement.
- Category.
- Owner.
- Inherent probability and impact.
- Inherent rating.
- Existing controls and control effectiveness.
- Residual probability and impact.
- Residual rating.
- Trend.
- Mitigation status.
- Target date.
- Tolerance status.
- Requested decision or assistance.
- Source and data-confidence indicator.

## Issue dashboard

For each material issue:

- Issue ID and title.
- Current condition and impact.
- Category.
- Owner.
- Severity and urgency.
- Date identified and age.
- Containment status.
- Root-cause status.
- Resolution plan.
- Target resolution date.
- Current trend.
- Dependencies.
- Escalation status.
- Requested decision or assistance.
- Source and data-confidence indicator.

## Risk matrix and distribution

- Risks by probability and impact.
- Risks by category.
- Risks by initiative, owner, and organizational area.
- Risks above appetite or tolerance.
- Inherent versus residual risk.
- Risk concentration and correlated-risk themes.
- Changes in risk distribution from the prior reporting period.

## Issue distribution and aging

- Issues by severity and urgency.
- Issues by category and initiative.
- Issues by owner.
- Issues by age.
- Issues past their target resolution date.
- Repeated or reopened issues.
- Issues affecting critical milestones, services, or obligations.

## Mitigation and resolution performance

- Open mitigation and corrective actions.
- Actions due soon.
- Overdue actions.
- Actions without owners or due dates.
- Mitigations completed but not validated.
- Risks whose ratings have not changed despite mitigation.
- Issues without credible resolution plans.
- Decisions or resources blocking mitigation or resolution.

## Trends and emerging themes

- New risks and issues.
- Increasing or decreasing exposure.
- Recurring causes.
- Cross-initiative dependencies.
- Concentrations in specific categories, owners, vendors, or systems.
- Risks becoming issues.
- Issues generating new risks.
- Control weaknesses appearing across multiple records.
- Early-warning indicators requiring attention.

## Executive attention

- Risks requiring acceptance.
- Issues requiring immediate intervention.
- Risks and issues above tolerance.
- Unresolved ownership.
- Material resource or priority conflicts.
- Required governance, legal, regulatory, clinical, privacy, security, or financial review.
- Recommended action, decision-maker, and required date.

## Data-quality summary

- Stale records.
- Missing owners, ratings, dates, or action plans.
- Conflicting assessments.
- Duplicate records.
- Unverified closures.
- Assumptions and limitations.
- Items requiring owner validation before distribution.
