# Outputs

## Warning summary

For each warning:

- Warning ID and title.
- Warning level:
  - Informational.
  - Watch.
  - Warning.
  - Critical.
- Risk, objective, initiative, or operation affected.
- Indicator or evidence that triggered the warning.
- Current value and relevant threshold.
- Direction and rate of change.
- Date and time detected.
- Duration or persistence.
- Risk owner.
- Confidence level.
- Source references.

## Warning rationale

- What changed.
- Why the change matters.
- Whether the signal is isolated or part of a pattern.
- Risk event that may follow.
- Potential impact.
- Time available to respond.
- Existing controls or mitigation relevant to the warning.
- Key assumptions and uncertainty.
- Alternative explanations.

## Recommended response

- Immediate validation step.
- Responsible person or role.
- Requested response date.
- Recommended monitoring frequency.
- Mitigation or contingency action to consider.
- Decision or resource required.
- Stakeholders or governance bodies requiring notification.
- Escalation level.
- Conditions for increasing, maintaining, reducing, or closing the warning.

## Warning dashboard

- Active warnings by level.
- New warnings.
- Escalated warnings.
- De-escalated or closed warnings.
- Warnings by initiative, category, owner, and source.
- Warnings with worsening trends.
- Warnings awaiting validation.
- Warnings without response owners.
- Warnings past the required response time.

## Indicator trends

- Current versus baseline values.
- Threshold crossings.
- Rate and direction of change.
- Persistent adverse patterns.
- Multiple indicators pointing to the same exposure.
- Indicators returning to normal.
- Indicators producing repeated false alerts.
- Gaps or delays in monitoring data.

## Risk conversion analysis

- Existing risks showing evidence of increasing exposure.
- Risks approaching realization.
- Risks that may have become active issues.
- New emerging risks suggested by warning signals.
- Issues creating additional secondary risks.
- Controls or mitigations showing signs of failure.

## Executive attention

- Critical warnings requiring immediate review.
- Warnings affecting material strategic, operational, clinical, financial, legal, regulatory, privacy, security, or reputational outcomes.
- Decisions required.
- Resource or priority conflicts.
- Contingencies that may need activation.
- Required decision-maker and deadline.
- Consequence of delayed response.

## Warning disposition

For each warning:

- Validated.
- Under investigation.
- Escalated.
- Mitigation initiated.
- Contingency activated.
- Converted to issue or incident.
- De-escalated.
- Closed.
- False positive.
- Insufficient evidence.

Include the approving human role, disposition date, rationale, and residual monitoring requirement.

## Data-quality and confidence summary

- Missing or stale indicators.
- Unverified warnings.
- Conflicting evidence.
- Monitoring gaps.
- Thresholds requiring review.
- Data sources with reliability concerns.
- Warnings based on low-confidence inference.
