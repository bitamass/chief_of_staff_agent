# Outputs

## Risk summary

- Risk ID and title.
- Normalized cause–event–impact statement.
- Objective or operation affected.
- Inherent risk rating.
- Existing controls and their effectiveness.
- Residual risk rating.
- Current trend and time horizon.
- Risk-tolerance status.
- Key evidence, assumptions, and uncertainties.

## Mitigation options

For each viable option:

- Option name and description.
- Response strategy:
  - Avoid.
  - Reduce probability.
  - Reduce impact.
  - Transfer or share.
  - Accept with monitoring.
  - Contingency or recovery preparation.
- Actions required.
- Owner.
- Expected implementation time.
- Estimated resources or cost.
- Dependencies and approvals.
- Expected effect on probability.
- Expected effect on impact.
- Expected residual risk.
- Benefits.
- Limitations and trade-offs.
- Secondary risks or unintended consequences.
- Evidence supporting the estimate.
- Confidence level.

## Comparative analysis

A comparison of options across:

- Expected risk reduction.
- Implementation time.
- Cost and resource demand.
- Technical and operational feasibility.
- Compliance and governance fit.
- Effect on strategic objectives.
- Reversibility.
- Stakeholder impact.
- Dependency complexity.
- Secondary risk.
- Confidence in expected effectiveness.

## Recommended mitigation

- Recommended option or combination of options.
- Reason for the recommendation.
- Expected reduction in exposure.
- Proposed owner.
- Required actions and sequencing.
- Target dates.
- Required budget or resources.
- Required decisions and approvals.
- Success measures.
- Validation method.
- Residual risk expected after implementation.
- Residual risk requiring formal acceptance.

## Contingency plan

When appropriate:

- Risk trigger.
- Immediate containment action.
- Decision authority.
- Communication and escalation path.
- Required resources.
- Recovery action.
- Continuity or fallback approach.
- Conditions for activating and ending the contingency.
- Link to specialized incident-response procedures when applicable.

## Implementation roadmap

- Immediate actions.
- Near-term mitigation.
- Longer-term structural controls.
- Dependencies.
- Milestones.
- Owners.
- Due dates.
- Review checkpoints.
- Evidence required to confirm effectiveness.

## Monitoring plan

- Key risk indicators.
- Trigger thresholds.
- Control-performance measures.
- Mitigation-progress measures.
- Residual-risk review date.
- Reporting cadence.
- Responsible monitoring owner.
- Conditions requiring escalation or reassessment.

## Executive decision request

- Decision required.
- Recommended option.
- Alternatives considered.
- Consequence of delaying the decision.
- Required decision-maker.
- Decision deadline.
- Budget or resource approval required.
- Residual risk requiring acceptance.
- Material assumptions or uncertainties.

## Data-quality and validation summary

- Missing or stale information.
- Unverified control effectiveness.
- Unsupported cost or impact estimates.
- Conflicting assessments.
- Assumptions requiring confirmation.
- Specialized reviews required before approval.
