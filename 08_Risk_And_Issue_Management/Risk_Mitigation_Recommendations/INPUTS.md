# Inputs

## Risk definition

For each risk:

- Risk ID and title.
- Cause–event–impact statement.
- Initiative, program, objective, deliverable, system, or operation affected.
- Risk category.
- Date identified.
- Risk owner.
- Source and last review date.
- Current status and trend.
- Time horizon in which the risk could materialize.
- Trigger events or early-warning indicators.
- Related risks, issues, assumptions, and dependencies.

## Risk assessment

- Inherent probability and impact.
- Inherent risk rating.
- Relevant impact dimensions:
  - Strategic.
  - Operational.
  - Financial.
  - Schedule or delivery.
  - Clinical or safety.
  - Technology or integration.
  - Cybersecurity, privacy, data, or records management.
  - Legal, regulatory, policy, or compliance.
  - Workforce or labor.
  - Stakeholder or reputational.
  - Continuity or service availability.
- Velocity: how quickly the impact could occur.
- Detectability: likelihood the risk would be recognized before impact.
- Duration and reversibility of consequences.
- Confidence level in the assessment.
- Approved risk appetite and tolerance.

## Existing controls

For each existing control:

- Control description.
- Control type: preventive, detective, corrective, or compensating.
- Control owner.
- Implementation status.
- Evidence that the control operates.
- Frequency.
- Scope and coverage.
- Known limitations or gaps.
- Control-effectiveness assessment.
- Date last tested or reviewed.
- Assurance, audit, or validation findings.

## Existing mitigation actions

- Mitigation description.
- Intended effect on probability or impact.
- Action owner.
- Planned start and completion dates.
- Current status.
- Resources and funding.
- Dependencies.
- Evidence of implementation.
- Expected and actual effectiveness.
- Overdue or blocked actions.
- Previous changes to the mitigation plan.

## Mitigation constraints

- Available budget and staffing.
- Technical feasibility.
- Operational feasibility.
- Implementation timeline.
- Clinical, security, privacy, legal, regulatory, labor, procurement, or governance requirements.
- Dependencies on vendors, partners, systems, or approvals.
- Change-management and adoption needs.
- Potential secondary risks or unintended consequences.
- Actions that are prohibited or out of scope.
- Authority required to approve mitigation.

## Decision context

- Decision or milestone affected by the risk.
- Available response options.
- Cost of mitigation.
- Expected loss or consequence of no action.
- Opportunity cost.
- Risk-transfer options.
- Risk-acceptance authority.
- Escalation requirements.
- Deadline for selecting or implementing a response.
- Stakeholders who must be consulted or informed.

## Supporting evidence

- Risk register.
- Issue register.
- Control assessments.
- Incident, audit, or assurance findings.
- Initiative charter and roadmap.
- Architecture, security, privacy, legal, clinical, financial, or operational reviews.
- Vendor documentation.
- Prior decisions and lessons learned.
- Comparable risks or historical outcomes.
- Approved policies and standards.
