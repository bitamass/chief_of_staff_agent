# Outputs

## Risk-scoring record

For each risk:

- Risk ID and title.
- Normalized cause–event–impact statement.
- Affected objective.
- Risk category.
- Assessment date.
- Assessment owner.
- Scoring methodology used.
- Time horizon.
- Evidence cutoff date.
- Data-confidence rating.

## Inherent risk assessment

- Inherent probability score.
- Probability rationale and evidence.
- Inherent impact score.
- Impact assessment by applicable dimension.
- Inherent risk rating.
- Velocity.
- Detectability.
- Proximity and persistence.
- Key assumptions.
- Confidence level.
- Reasonable worst-case scenario.

## Control assessment

- Existing controls.
- Control owners.
- Implementation status.
- Control-effectiveness assessment.
- Evidence of operation.
- Control gaps.
- Controls requiring testing or validation.
- Overall confidence in the control assessment.

## Residual risk assessment

- Residual probability score.
- Residual impact score.
- Residual risk rating.
- Explanation of the reduction from inherent risk.
- Evidence supporting the reduction.
- Residual exposure.
- Risk-appetite and tolerance comparison.
- Acceptance or escalation authority required.

## Planned treatment view

- Planned mitigation actions.
- Expected future probability.
- Expected future impact.
- Target risk rating.
- Target date.
- Assumptions underlying the target rating.
- Required validation after implementation.

The target rating must be clearly distinguished from the current residual rating.

## Trend analysis

- Previous versus current scores.
- Direction of change.
- Reason for the change.
- Changes in conditions, controls, or evidence.
- New uncertainty.
- Rating changes requiring approval.
- Whether the change reflects real exposure or only better information.

## Risk-priority view

- Risks above tolerance.
- Highest residual risks.
- Fastest-moving risks.
- Risks with low detectability.
- Risks with weak controls.
- Risks with low-confidence assessments.
- Risks requiring immediate reassessment.
- Risks requiring executive or governance review.

## Scoring-quality report

- Missing evidence.
- Stale assessments.
- Unsupported scores.
- Scores inconsistent with the approved framework.
- Inherent and residual ratings that are not distinguished.
- Planned controls incorrectly treated as existing.
- Risk ratings reduced without evidence.
- Conflicting assessments.
- Assessments requiring specialized review.
