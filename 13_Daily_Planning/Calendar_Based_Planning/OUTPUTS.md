# Outputs

## Executive daily plan

- Date and concise statement of the day’s primary purpose.
- Three to five highest-priority outcomes.
- Recommended sequence of meetings, preparation, focus work, decisions, and follow-up.
- Protected time for high-value or time-sensitive work.
- Unallocated capacity available for emerging needs.

## Prioritized calendar

For each calendar event:

- Start and end time.
- Meeting title and objective.
- Executive’s expected role.
- Priority classification: essential, important, optional, delegate, or reschedule.
- Preparation required and recommended preparation period.
- Decision, contribution, or outcome expected.
- Relevant initiative, commitment, or strategic priority.
- Recommended follow-up time.

## Daily time blocks

- Meeting blocks.
- Preparation blocks.
- Decision and approval blocks.
- Focus-work blocks.
- Communication and response blocks.
- Follow-up and action-capture blocks.
- Break, travel, and transition time.
- Contingency capacity for urgent matters.

## Calendar conflict report

- Overlapping or double-booked events.
- Meetings competing with higher-priority work.
- Insufficient preparation or travel time.
- Events with unclear objectives or missing materials.
- Meetings that may be delegated, shortened, combined, moved, or declined.
- Recommended calendar adjustment and the reason for it.
- Person responsible for confirming the change.

## Meeting preparation queue

For each meeting requiring preparation:

- Meeting time and title.
- Required materials.
- Relevant prior decisions, actions, and risks.
- Questions or talking points to prepare.
- Decision or alignment expected.
- Preparation owner and completion deadline.
- Missing information requiring follow-up.

## Decisions and commitments due today

- Decision or commitment description.
- Related initiative or meeting.
- Due time and consequence of delay.
- Required information and participants.
- Current readiness status.
- Recommended next step.

## Delegation and rescheduling recommendations

- Work or meetings that may be delegated.
- Recommended delegate and reason.
- Information or authority the delegate will need.
- Events recommended for rescheduling or cancellation.
- Proposed alternative timing.
- Items requiring executive approval before a calendar change is made.

## End-of-day continuity checklist

- Decisions to record.
- Actions and commitments to assign.
- Follow-ups to send.
- Unfinished priorities to carry forward.
- Calendar changes needed for subsequent days.
- Risks, issues, or blockers requiring escalation.
- Source and confidence notes for all material recommendations.
