# Outputs

## Pending decision and approval summary

- Total number of pending items.
- Items ready for decision.
- Items awaiting information or specialist review.
- Items approaching or exceeding their response deadline.
- Items without a confirmed decision-maker or approval pathway.
- Highest-consequence decisions requiring executive attention.
- Material changes since the previous review.

## Prioritized decision queue

For each item:

- Priority rank and title.
- Decision or approval required.
- Decision-maker or approving body.
- Requestor and accountable owner.
- Due date and days remaining.
- Current status.
- Readiness classification.
- Related initiative, milestone, risk, or commitment.
- Consequence of delay.
- Recommended next action.
- Source and confidence level.

## Decision-readiness brief

For each material item:

### Decision at a glance

- Clear decision statement.
- Why the decision is needed.
- Deadline.
- Decision owner and authority.
- Desired outcome.
- Scope and affected stakeholders.

### Options and trade-offs

- Options under consideration.
- Benefits and disadvantages of each option.
- Cost, timing, risk, and implementation implications.
- Reversibility.
- Assumptions and unresolved uncertainties.
- Documented recommendation and its source.

### Evidence and analysis

- Key facts and measures.
- Supporting documents and specialist reviews.
- Relevant prior decisions.
- Confidence in the evidence.
- Missing, outdated, or conflicting information.

### Risks and dependencies

- Risks of each option.
- Risks of delay or inaction.
- Required mitigations or conditions.
- Dependencies and downstream impacts.
- Escalation thresholds.

### Readiness determination

- Ready, partially ready, not ready, or awaiting information.
- Basis for the classification.
- Remaining gaps.
- Owner and deadline for closing each gap.
- Earliest reasonable decision date.

## Approvals due by date

- Decision or approval title.
- Required response date.
- Approving authority.
- Readiness status.
- Scheduled meeting or review point.
- Preparation required.
- Consequence if the deadline is missed.
- Recommended reminder or escalation date.

## Authority and governance exceptions

- Item without a confirmed decision-maker.
- Request exceeding the identified authority level.
- Missing required reviewer or co-approver.
- Potential conflict of interest or separation-of-duty concern.
- Missing quorum or formal governance requirement.
- Proposed action inconsistent with a prior decision or policy.
- Recommended correction or escalation pathway.

## Missing information and clarification report

- Decision affected.
- Missing evidence, document, reviewer, option, owner, or approval.
- Why the information is material.
- Person responsible for providing it.
- Required date.
- Effect on decision readiness.
- Focused clarification question.

## Recommended executive actions

- Decide or approve now.
- Request additional evidence.
- Seek specialist review.
- Clarify authority.
- Return the request for revision.
- Defer to a stated date or condition.
- Escalate to the appropriate authority.
- Decline or withdraw the request.
- Record the rationale and any conditions.

## Decision and approval record template

- Decision identifier.
- Final decision or approval status.
- Decision-maker.
- Decision date.
- Approved option or authorized action.
- Conditions, limitations, or expiration.
- Rationale.
- Evidence reviewed.
- Dissenting or alternative views.
- Follow-up actions, owners, and dates.
- Communication and distribution requirements.
- Next review date, if applicable.

## Source and quality report

- Sources reviewed for each material item.
- Date and freshness of each source.
- Confirmed facts, assumptions, and interpretations.
- Conflicting or missing information.
- Confidence level.
- Items requiring human confirmation before action or distribution.
