# Outputs

## Daily priority summary

- Date and statement of the day’s primary leadership objective.
- Three to five highest-priority outcomes.
- Explanation of why each outcome requires attention today.
- Executive role required: decide, approve, prepare, communicate, sponsor, review, or unblock.
- Key constraint, dependency, or consequence associated with each priority.

## Ranked daily priorities

For each ranked priority:

- Rank and priority title.
- Required outcome.
- Related strategic goal, initiative, or commitment.
- Priority classification: critical, high, standard, or defer.
- Due time or deadline.
- Executive action required.
- Estimated executive time.
- Owner and supporting participants.
- Dependencies and readiness.
- Consequence of delay or non-completion.
- Recommended next action.
- Source and confidence level.

## Priority rationale

- Strategic alignment.
- Urgency and deadline.
- Material value or expected benefit.
- Risk or issue addressed.
- Stakeholder consequence.
- Executive-specific need.
- Downstream activities that will be unblocked.
- Reason the item ranks above or below competing work.

## Decisions and approvals due today

- Decision or approval statement.
- Related priority and deadline.
- Available options and material trade-offs.
- Current recommendation, if documented.
- Required evidence or participants.
- Decision readiness: ready, partially ready, not ready, or awaiting information.
- Consequence of delay.
- Recommended preparation action.

## Priority schedule

- Recommended time block for each priority.
- Meetings in which a priority can be advanced.
- Preparation, focus work, response, and follow-up periods.
- Protected capacity for urgent or unexpected work.
- Total planned effort compared with available time.
- Any workload that does not fit within the day.

## Delegate, defer, or decline recommendations

For each lower-priority item:

- Item and current requestor or owner.
- Recommendation: delegate, defer, decline, combine, or complete asynchronously.
- Reason for the recommendation.
- Proposed delegate or revised date.
- Context, authority, or resources required.
- Potential consequence or stakeholder communication need.
- Executive approval required before the change.

## Blockers and escalation needs

- Priority affected by the blocker.
- Description and source.
- Owner and current resolution activity.
- Authority, resource, information, or decision needed.
- Response deadline.
- Consequence if unresolved.
- Recommended escalation pathway, when applicable.

## Daily success measures

- Observable condition indicating each priority is complete or sufficiently advanced.
- Decision recorded.
- Approval issued.
- Material reviewed.
- Stakeholder response completed.
- Blocker removed.
- Owner and deadline confirmed.
- Follow-up scheduled or delegated.

## End-of-day continuity record

- Completed or advanced priorities.
- Decisions and approvals made.
- New actions and commitments.
- Priorities carried forward and reason.
- Items delegated or deferred.
- New risks, issues, or blockers.
- Changes required to subsequent daily or weekly plans.
- Sources and assumptions requiring continued validation.
