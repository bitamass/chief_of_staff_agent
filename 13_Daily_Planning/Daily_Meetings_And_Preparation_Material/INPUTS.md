# Inputs

## Daily meeting context

- Planning date, time zone, and executive working hours.
- Executive’s role, current priorities, and decision authority.
- Complete list of meetings scheduled for the day.
- Calendar source and last synchronization time.
- Known travel, transition, accessibility, or location constraints.
- Protected focus periods and other non-meeting commitments.

## Meeting information

For each meeting:

- Meeting identifier, title, date, start time, end time, and location or virtual platform.
- Organizer, attendees, and participant roles.
- Meeting objective and desired outcome.
- Executive’s role: decision-maker, sponsor, presenter, contributor, reviewer, observer, or optional attendee.
- Agenda and allocated time for each topic.
- Related initiative, program, operational area, or strategic objective.
- Meeting sensitivity, data classification, and distribution restrictions.
- Whether the meeting is recurring, newly scheduled, tentative, or confirmed.

## Preparation materials

- Agenda and meeting invitation.
- Pre-read documents, briefing notes, presentations, reports, or dashboards.
- Relevant prior meeting summaries.
- Previous decisions and decision rationale.
- Open action items, commitments, and owners.
- Initiative charter, objectives, milestones, deliverables, and status.
- Financial, operational, technology, security, privacy, compliance, workforce, or clinical information.
- Prior executive communications and stakeholder correspondence.
- External developments approved for consideration.

## Decision requirements

For each anticipated decision:

- Decision statement and deadline.
- Decision owner and approving authority.
- Options under consideration.
- Evaluation criteria and material trade-offs.
- Current recommendation and its source.
- Required evidence or specialist review.
- Consequence of deciding, delaying, or taking no action.
- Dependencies, assumptions, risks, and unresolved questions.

## Participant and stakeholder context

- Names, roles, and organizational relationships of attendees.
- Meeting sponsor and accountable leader.
- Subject-matter experts and required reviewers.
- Known stakeholder interests, concerns, commitments, or prior positions.
- Participants required for quorum, approval, or informed discussion.
- People affected by the meeting who are not attending.
- Any conflicts of interest or authority limitations that must be considered.

## Actions, commitments, and continuity

- Open actions from earlier meetings.
- Action owner, due date, status, and supporting evidence.
- Commitments made by or to the executive.
- Overdue or blocked actions.
- Upcoming milestones or meetings affected by today’s discussion.
- Items that require confirmation, escalation, or reassignment.
- Expected follow-up and communication after the meeting.

## Risks and issues

- Current risks and issues relevant to the meeting.
- Risk or issue owner.
- Current rating, status, and trend.
- Mitigation, containment, or resolution actions.
- Warning or escalation thresholds.
- Decisions required to address the exposure.
- Evidence supporting material risk or issue claims.

## Preparation constraints and quality

- Time available before each meeting.
- Required preparation depth.
- Missing, outdated, conflicting, or unverified materials.
- Source and date for each material claim.
- Assumptions used when information is incomplete.
- Items requiring human, specialist, or executive validation.
