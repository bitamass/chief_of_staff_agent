# Outputs

## Executive overview of the week ahead

- Week dates and concise statement of the week’s leadership focus.
- Three to seven highest-priority weekly outcomes.
- Major decisions, milestones, and stakeholder engagements.
- Most significant risks, blockers, and capacity concerns.
- Key changes from the prior plan.
- Areas requiring early executive attention.

## Weekly priority plan

For each priority:

- Priority rank and title.
- Required outcome.
- Related strategic objective, initiative, or commitment.
- Executive role required.
- Target completion date.
- Estimated effort.
- Owner and supporting participants.
- Current readiness.
- Dependencies and risks.
- Consequence of delay.
- Recommended next action.
- Source and confidence level.

## Day-by-day operating plan

For each working day:

- Primary outcome for the day.
- Critical meetings and expected results.
- Required preparation and follow-up.
- Decisions or approvals due.
- Protected focus-work blocks.
- Stakeholder communications.
- Deliverables or actions due.
- Available contingency capacity.
- Items that may carry into the next day.

## Key meetings and preparation schedule

For each material meeting:

- Date, time, title, and objective.
- Executive role.
- Decision, alignment, or contribution expected.
- Key participants.
- Preparation materials required.
- Preparation owner and deadline.
- Related prior decisions, actions, risks, and commitments.
- Readiness status.
- Recommended follow-up period.

## Decisions and approvals calendar

- Decision or approval statement.
- Related meeting, initiative, or milestone.
- Decision owner and authority.
- Deadline.
- Required evidence and participants.
- Current recommendation, when documented.
- Readiness status.
- Consequence of delay.
- Required preparation action.

## Deliverables and milestone outlook

- Deliverable or milestone.
- Owner and related initiative.
- Due date.
- Current status.
- Executive involvement required.
- Dependencies and blockers.
- Risk to completion.
- Upcoming downstream effect.
- Evidence needed to confirm completion.

## Risks, issues, and blockers requiring attention

- Risk, issue, or blocker.
- Affected priority, meeting, or deliverable.
- Owner and current status.
- Trend or urgency.
- Response currently underway.
- Executive action or decision needed.
- Response deadline.
- Consequence if unresolved.
- Escalation condition and pathway.

## Stakeholder engagement plan

- Stakeholder or group.
- Purpose of engagement.
- Desired outcome.
- Recommended timing and communication method.
- Executive role.
- Supporting materials or talking points needed.
- Existing commitment or sensitivity.
- Follow-up owner and deadline.

## Capacity and conflict report

- Total planned workload compared with available time.
- Days with excessive meeting or preparation demand.
- Overlapping commitments.
- Insufficient focus, travel, transition, or follow-up time.
- Competing deadlines or resource demands.
- Meetings or work recommended for delegation, deferral, shortening, combination, or asynchronous completion.
- Human approval needed for proposed changes.

## Delegation and support recommendations

- Work proposed for delegation or shared ownership.
- Recommended delegate or contributor.
- Expected outcome and deadline.
- Authority, access, and context required.
- Executive responsibilities that remain.
- Review and escalation points.
- Confirmation still required.

## Weekly success measures

- Observable result for each major weekly priority.
- Decisions recorded.
- Deliverables accepted.
- Risks reduced or escalated.
- Stakeholder commitments completed.
- Blockers removed.
- Owners and deadlines confirmed.
- Preparation completed for upcoming milestones.

## Continuity into the following week

- Work expected to carry forward.
- Decisions and actions created during the week.
- Meetings or milestones requiring advance preparation.
- Dependencies requiring continued monitoring.
- Risks or issues likely to remain open.
- Items requiring inclusion in the next weekly plan.
- Sources, assumptions, and uncertainties requiring validation.
