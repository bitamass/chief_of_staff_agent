# Outputs

## Delegation recommendation

- Work item and required outcome.
- Recommendation: retain, delegate, share, reassign, or defer.
- Recommended delegate, co-owner, or contributing participants.
- Reason for the recommendation.
- Expected benefit and material risks.
- Required authorization before the assignment takes effect.
- Confidence level and unresolved questions.

## Delegation brief

For each delegated or shared work item:

### Purpose and outcome

- Why the work matters.
- Required deliverable or outcome.
- Connection to the relevant priority, initiative, or commitment.
- Completion criteria and due date.

### Roles and responsibilities

- Accountable owner.
- Responsible delegate or work lead.
- Contributors, reviewers, and approvers.
- Stakeholders to consult or inform.
- Responsibilities retained by the executive or original owner.

### Scope and boundaries

- Included work.
- Out-of-scope activities.
- Decisions the delegate may make.
- Decisions requiring consultation or approval.
- Prohibited actions or authority limitations.
- Conditions requiring escalation.

### Required context

- Relevant background and prior decisions.
- Source materials and templates.
- Known assumptions, dependencies, risks, and issues.
- Stakeholder expectations.
- Confidentiality and information-handling requirements.

### Deliverables and milestones

- Required deliverables.
- Interim milestones and deadlines.
- Expected quality and format.
- Evidence required for completion.
- Review and acceptance process.

### Communication and review plan

- Kickoff or handoff meeting.
- Status-update cadence and format.
- Required review points.
- Escalation channel and response time.
- Final approval or acceptance authority.
- Closeout and knowledge-transfer requirements.

## Delegation suitability assessment

For each potential delegate:

- Relevant expertise and experience.
- Available capacity.
- Existing authority and required additional authorization.
- Required system, data, or stakeholder access.
- Potential conflicts or constraints.
- Support or development needed.
- Suitability assessment and rationale.
- Confirmation still required.

## Responsibility assignment

- Accountable person.
- Responsible person.
- Contributors.
- Required reviewers.
- Approvers.
- People to consult.
- People to inform.
- Explicit identification of any unresolved role ambiguity.

## Capacity and workload impact

- Estimated time transferred from the executive or original owner.
- Time required for handoff, support, and review.
- Effect on the delegate’s existing priorities.
- Workload or scheduling conflicts.
- Recommended workload adjustments.
- Backup or continuity arrangement.

## Risk and exception report

- Delegated work without a confirmed owner.
- Delegate lacking required authority, access, expertise, or capacity.
- Unclear completion criteria.
- Missing context or source documents.
- Separation-of-duty or conflict-of-interest concerns.
- Sensitive work requiring specialist or executive review.
- Overdue checkpoints or unresolved escalations.
- Recommended corrective action.

## Handoff checklist

- Outcome and deadline confirmed.
- Scope and boundaries understood.
- Roles and authority confirmed.
- Source materials provided.
- Systems and access verified.
- Milestones and review cadence agreed.
- Risks and escalation pathway understood.
- Acceptance criteria agreed.
- Assignment acknowledged by all required parties.

## Completion and reintegration record

- Deliverables completed.
- Evidence reviewed.
- Outstanding items.
- Decisions and actions created.
- Final acceptance status.
- Lessons or context to preserve.
- Responsibilities returning to the original owner.
- Follow-up or monitoring requirements.
