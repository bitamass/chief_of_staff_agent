# Inputs

## Task identification

- Unique task identifier.
- Current task title and description.
- Official task repository or tracking system.
- Related strategic goal, initiative, program, meeting, decision, commitment, risk, or issue.
- Original source and creation date.
- Current responsible owner and accountable person.
- Current status, priority, start date, and due date.
- Last update date and person who made the update.

## Requested update

- Description of the proposed change.
- Reason the update is needed.
- Requestor and request date.
- Type of change:
  - Status.
  - Owner or contributor.
  - Scope.
  - Required outcome.
  - Priority.
  - Start date or due date.
  - Milestone or dependency.
  - Completion criteria.
  - Authority or approval requirement.
  - Risk, issue, or blocker.
  - Supporting context.
  - Cancellation, closure, deferral, or reopening.
- Whether the change is confirmed, requested, recommended, or inferred from another record.
- Date the change should take effect.

## Progress and status evidence

- Work completed since the previous update.
- Current deliverables or work products.
- Evidence of progress.
- Percentage complete, when supported by a defined method.
- Milestones reached or missed.
- Reviews, approvals, or acceptance received.
- Time or effort used compared with the estimate.
- Remaining work and estimated effort.
- Source and date of the progress information.

## Outcome and scope

- Current required outcome.
- Proposed revised outcome, if applicable.
- Current included and excluded work.
- Proposed scope additions, removals, or clarifications.
- Reason for the scope change.
- Effect on quality, benefit, cost, timing, resources, or risk.
- Whether the proposed update creates a materially different task.
- Required acceptance criteria and evidence of completion.

## Ownership and authority

- Current responsible owner.
- Proposed new owner or contributors.
- Accountable person.
- Required reviewers and approvers.
- Authority needed to approve the proposed change.
- Current owner and proposed owner acknowledgment.
- Manager or cross-functional approval required.
- Required system, data, tool, or stakeholder access.
- Any separation-of-duty or conflict-of-interest considerations.

## Dates, priority, and capacity

- Original start date and due date.
- Current approved dates.
- Proposed revised dates.
- Reason for any schedule change.
- Consequence of delay.
- Current and proposed priority.
- Existing owner capacity and competing commitments.
- Required preparation, review, or approval time.
- Downstream meetings, decisions, deliverables, or milestones affected.
- Whether the proposed date is confirmed or estimated.

## Dependencies, blockers, risks, and issues

- Current dependencies and prerequisite work.
- New or resolved dependencies.
- Current blockers and resolution activities.
- Risks or issues affecting the task.
- Risk or issue owner.
- Required decision, authority, resource, or information.
- Escalation threshold and deadline.
- Downstream work affected by the update.

## Related records and duplicate detection

- Linked decisions, approvals, commitments, milestones, deliverables, risks, and issues.
- Tasks with overlapping scope or outcome.
- Predecessor and successor tasks.
- Related task updates that must remain synchronized.
- Prior versions or superseded task records.
- Potential duplicate, conflicting, or obsolete work.

## Closure or cancellation evidence

When closure or cancellation is proposed:

- Completion evidence.
- Acceptance or approval record.
- Person authorized to close or cancel the task.
- Remaining actions or exceptions.
- Reason for cancellation or withdrawal.
- Downstream impact.
- Knowledge, documents, or decisions that must be retained.
- Required stakeholder communication.

## Source and data quality

- Source for each proposed change.
- Date and freshness of the source.
- Distinction between confirmed changes, recommendations, assumptions, and interpretations.
- Missing, outdated, conflicting, or unverified information.
- Confidence in reported status, effort, and completion.
- Items requiring owner, manager, approver, or specialist confirmation.
