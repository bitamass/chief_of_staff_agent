# Outputs

## Proposed task update

- Task identifier and current title.
- Summary of the proposed update.
- Change type.
- Reason for the change.
- Requestor and source.
- Effective date.
- Required approving authority.
- Recommendation: approve, approve with revisions, hold for clarification, separate into a new task, merge with another task, or reject the update.

## Updated task record

- Updated task title.
- Revised required outcome.
- Current status.
- Responsible owner and accountable person.
- Contributors, reviewers, and approvers.
- Updated priority.
- Start date and due date.
- Scope and boundaries.
- Completion and acceptance criteria.
- Dependencies and prerequisites.
- Risks, issues, and blockers.
- Required next action.
- Next review date.
- Source references.

## Progress update

- Work completed since the previous review.
- Evidence supporting the reported progress.
- Milestones completed.
- Remaining work.
- Current estimate to completion.
- Variance from the prior plan.
- Explanation for the variance.
- Confidence in the status.
- Required corrective action.

## Impact assessment

- Effect on related priorities and initiatives.
- Effect on deadlines and milestones.
- Effect on other tasks and owners.
- Resource or capacity impact.
- Financial, operational, technical, governance, stakeholder, or quality impact.
- New or changed risks.
- Downstream decisions or meetings affected.
- Communication or approval required.

## Readiness and validation report

- Is the proposed change clearly described?
- Is the source documented?
- Is the requested authority appropriate?
- Has the current owner been consulted?
- Is the revised deadline realistic?
- Are dependencies updated?
- Are completion criteria still valid?
- Does the change require a new task rather than an update?
- Are linked records synchronized?
- Is sensitive or specialist review required?

## Exceptions and clarification questions

- Unsupported status or completion claim.
- Unconfirmed ownership change.
- Deadline change without rationale.
- Scope expansion without capacity or approval.
- Conflicting task records.
- Missing acceptance evidence.
- Inconsistent priority or dependency information.
- Proposed closure with unresolved work.
- Focused question and person responsible for clarification.

## Closure, cancellation, or reopening recommendation

- Recommended status.
- Basis for closure, cancellation, or reopening.
- Completion or acceptance evidence.
- Remaining exceptions.
- Required authorization.
- Related records that must be updated.
- Stakeholders requiring notification.
- Information that must remain in the task history.

## Change history entry

- Change date.
- Changed by.
- Approved by.
- Fields changed.
- Prior values.
- New values.
- Reason for the change.
- Source reference.
- Conditions or limitations.
- Follow-up actions.

## Source and confidence record

- Sources reviewed.
- Confirmed facts, requested changes, assumptions, and interpretations.
- Date and freshness of each source.
- Missing or conflicting information.
- Confidence in the proposed update.
- Items requiring human validation before the official record changes.
