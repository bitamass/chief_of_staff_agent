# Outputs

## Draft task record

For each proposed task:

- Draft task identifier.
- Concise, action-oriented task title.
- Required outcome.
- Description and relevant context.
- Related strategic goal, initiative, meeting, decision, or commitment.
- Proposed owner and accountable person.
- Contributors, reviewers, and approvers.
- Proposed start date and due date.
- Estimated effort.
- Priority and rationale.
- Current draft status.
- Source reference and date.

## Completion criteria

- Required deliverable or result.
- Observable conditions demonstrating completion.
- Required evidence or supporting documentation.
- Quality and format expectations.
- Required review and approval.
- Person authorized to accept or close the task.
- Conditions that would make the task incomplete.

## Scope and authority statement

- Included work.
- Out-of-scope work.
- Decisions the owner may make.
- Decisions requiring consultation or approval.
- Information-handling restrictions.
- Specialist-review requirements.
- Conditions requiring escalation.

## Dependencies and readiness assessment

- Required predecessor work.
- Required people, information, systems, or approvals.
- Related tasks and downstream effects.
- Known blockers and unresolved assumptions.
- Readiness classification: ready, partially ready, not ready, or awaiting information.
- Corrective actions required before activation.

## Task quality assessment

- Is the outcome specific?
- Is a proposed owner identified?
- Is accountability clear?
- Is the deadline supported by a source?
- Is the task appropriately sized?
- Are completion criteria measurable?
- Are dependencies and risks visible?
- Is the required authority available?
- Are sources and assumptions documented?

## Duplicate and overlap report

- Existing task with similar scope or outcome.
- Type of relationship: duplicate, overlapping, dependent, superseding, or unrelated.
- Recommended action: merge, link, revise, retain separately, or discard.
- Reason for the recommendation.
- Human confirmation required.

## Clarification questions

- Missing outcome or business purpose.
- Unconfirmed owner or authority.
- Unsupported or conflicting deadline.
- Unclear completion criteria.
- Missing dependency or required input.
- Unresolved priority conflict.
- Sensitive work requiring qualified review.
- Unclear distinction from an existing task.

## Activation recommendation

- Recommendation: approve as drafted, approve with revisions, hold for clarification, merge with an existing task, or do not create.
- Reason for the recommendation.
- Required human confirmation.
- Recommended first action after approval.
- Proposed review or check-in date.
- Recommended task repository or tracking location.

## Source and confidence record

- Source used for each material task attribute.
- Confirmed facts, proposals, assumptions, and interpretations.
- Date and freshness of the source.
- Missing or conflicting information.
- Confidence in the draft.
- Items requiring validation before assignment.
