# Outputs — Meeting Preparation

## Meeting readiness summary

Provide a concise overview of:

* Meeting purpose and desired outcome.
* Overall readiness status.
* Decisions or discussions requiring executive attention.
* Missing participants, materials, evidence, or logistics.
* Primary risks to a productive meeting.
* Preparation actions required before the meeting.

## Meeting profile

Provide:

* Meeting title.
* Date, time, duration, location, and format.
* Meeting owner and facilitator.
* Executive’s expected role.
* Strategic goal, initiative, project, or responsibility supported.
* Participants and roles.
* Required decision-makers.
* Confidentiality or access requirements.

## Readiness assessment

Assess each area as **Ready**, **Partially ready**, **Not ready**, or **Not applicable**:

* Purpose and desired outcome.
* Agenda and time allocation.
* Participant attendance.
* Decision authority.
* Decision materials and options.
* Supporting evidence and data.
* Pre-reading and briefing materials.
* Prior actions and commitments.
* Risks, issues, and dependencies.
* Logistics and technology.
* Access permissions and confidentiality.
* Note-taking and decision-recording plan.

For every item that is not ready, identify what is missing, the responsible owner, and the required completion date.

## Key decisions and discussion outcomes

For each expected decision or material discussion, provide:

* Decision or discussion question.
* Desired outcome.
* Decision owner and approval authority.
* Available options.
* Supporting evidence.
* Risks and trade-offs.
* Missing information.
* Decision-readiness status.
* Consequence of delay.

## Participant preparation

For each key participant, identify:

* Name and role.
* Reason participation is required.
* Agenda item or contribution.
* Materials to review or prepare.
* Decision authority or subject-matter responsibility.
* Attendance status.
* Preparation deadline.

## Required materials checklist

For each required item, provide:

* Material or document.
* Purpose.
* Owner.
* Status.
* Distribution deadline.
* Intended recipients.
* Access or confidentiality requirements.
* Source and last-updated date.

## Prior decisions, actions, and commitments

Identify:

* Prior decisions relevant to the meeting.
* Open actions requiring review.
* Overdue, blocked, or at-risk commitments.
* Items affecting an expected decision.
* Owners expected to provide updates.
* Matters requiring confirmation or closure.

## Meeting risks and contingencies

Identify:

* Missing decision-makers or essential participants.
* Incomplete or conflicting evidence.
* Unresolved stakeholder disagreement.
* Agenda overload.
* Insufficient preparation time.
* Confidentiality or access concerns.
* Technology or logistical risks.
* Dependencies that could prevent the desired outcome.

For each material risk, recommend a contingency or mitigation.

## Preparation action plan

List all remaining preparation actions with:

* Action.
* Owner.
* Due date.
* Dependency.
* Completion evidence required.
* Consequence if incomplete.
* Escalation recommendation, when appropriate.

## Overall readiness status

Classify the meeting as:

* **Ready:** Essential participants, materials, decisions, and logistics are confirmed.
* **Ready with minor gaps:** The meeting can proceed, but limited preparation remains.
* **At risk:** Material gaps could prevent the desired outcome.
* **Not ready:** Essential evidence, authority, participants, materials, or logistics are missing.

State the conditions required to improve the readiness status.

## Human-review requirement

The meeting owner, executive, or authorized preparation coordinator must verify the readiness assessment and approve any changes to participants, materials, decisions, agenda, logistics, or distribution before action is taken.
