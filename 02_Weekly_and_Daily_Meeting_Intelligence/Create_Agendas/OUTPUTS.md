# Outputs — Create Agendas

## Meeting overview

Provide:

* Meeting title.
* Date, time, duration, location, and format.
* Meeting owner and facilitator.
* Meeting purpose.
* Desired meeting outcome.
* Participants and their roles.
* Required decision-makers.
* Related strategic goal, initiative, project, or operational responsibility.

## Recommended agenda

For each agenda item, provide:

* Sequence number.
* Agenda-item title.
* Purpose: inform, discuss, decide, review, plan, or escalate.
* Desired outcome.
* Item owner or presenter.
* Required participants.
* Supporting materials.
* Allocated time.
* Decision or action expected.
* Related initiative, milestone, risk, issue, or commitment.

Include time for:

* Opening and confirmation of objectives.
* Review of prior decisions and outstanding actions, when relevant.
* Transitions between agenda items.
* Summary of decisions, actions, owners, and due dates.
* Closing and confirmation of next steps.

Ensure the total allocated time does not exceed the scheduled meeting duration.

## Decisions expected

For each decision item, provide:

* Decision question.
* Decision owner and approval authority.
* Options to be considered.
* Supporting evidence required.
* Recommended preparation.
* Decision-readiness status: ready, partially ready, or not ready.
* Consequence if the decision is deferred.

## Prior commitments and actions to review

Identify:

* Prior decisions requiring confirmation or follow-through.
* Open action items.
* Overdue, blocked, or at-risk commitments.
* Actions affecting the current meeting’s decisions.
* Owners expected to provide status updates.

## Required preparation

List:

* Pre-reading materials.
* Data or analysis required.
* Questions participants should consider.
* Materials to be prepared.
* Preparation owner.
* Distribution deadline.
* Participants who must review each item before the meeting.

## Parking lot and deferred topics

List proposed items that should not be included in the current agenda because they:

* Do not support the meeting purpose.
* Are not ready for discussion or decision.
* Lack essential participants or evidence.
* Can be handled asynchronously.
* Duplicate another agenda item or meeting.
* Cannot fit within the available time.

For each item, recommend an alternative forum, owner, or review date when appropriate.

## Agenda risks and gaps

Identify:

* Missing decision-makers.
* Unclear meeting objectives.
* Decision items without sufficient evidence.
* Missing materials or item owners.
* Excessive agenda content.
* Insufficient discussion or decision time.
* Confidential topics requiring restricted attendance.
* Dependencies that may prevent the desired outcome.

## Agenda readiness status

Classify the agenda as:

* **Ready:** Purpose, participants, materials, timing, and decision items are confirmed.
* **Partially ready:** The agenda can proceed, but identified preparation remains incomplete.
* **Not ready:** Essential objectives, participants, materials, or decision requirements are missing.

State what must be completed before distribution.

## Human-review requirement

The meeting owner or authorized agenda manager must review and approve the agenda, participants, timing, materials, and decision items before the agenda is distributed or added to a calendar invitation.
