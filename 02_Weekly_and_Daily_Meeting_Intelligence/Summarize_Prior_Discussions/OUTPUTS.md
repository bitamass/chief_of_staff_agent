# Outputs — Summarize Prior Discussions

## Executive history summary

Provide a concise overview of:

* Why the topic was discussed.
* How the discussion developed over time.
* Major areas of agreement and disagreement.
* Decisions and commitments already established.
* Open questions and unresolved matters.
* What has changed since the earliest discussion.
* Why this history matters now.

## Scope and sources

State:

* Topic, initiative, decision, issue, or question summarized.
* Time period covered.
* Intended audience and purpose.
* Meetings, documents, and communications reviewed.
* Known gaps in the record.
* Confidentiality or source limitations.

## Chronological discussion history

For each relevant meeting or communication, provide:

* Date.
* Meeting or communication title.
* Key participants and roles.
* Purpose or context.
* Main points discussed.
* Options or proposals considered.
* Areas of agreement.
* Areas of disagreement.
* Decisions made.
* Matters deferred.
* Open questions.
* Actions or commitments created.
* Source reference.

## Evolution of the discussion

Explain how the topic changed over time, including:

* Changes in objectives or scope.
* New evidence or assumptions.
* Changes in stakeholder positions.
* Risks or issues that emerged.
* Options added, revised, or removed.
* Decisions that were reconsidered, superseded, or reversed.
* Dependencies or constraints that changed.
* Recurring concerns that remain unresolved.

## Prior decisions

For each documented decision, provide:

* Decision statement.
* Date and source.
* Decision owner or authority.
* Rationale, when documented.
* Affected initiative, deliverable, or stakeholder.
* Current status:

  * Active.
  * Implemented.
  * Partially implemented.
  * Superseded.
  * Reversed.
  * Requires confirmation.
* Related actions or commitments.

Do not classify discussion, preference, or recommendation as a decision.

## Commitments and follow-through

For each relevant commitment, provide:

* Commitment statement.
* Owner.
* Date committed.
* Due date.
* Current status.
* Completion evidence.
* Dependencies or blockers.
* Source.
* Relevance to the current discussion.

## Areas of agreement and disagreement

Clearly separate:

* Confirmed agreement.
* Partial agreement.
* Unresolved disagreement.
* Differing stakeholder positions.
* Matters where the record does not establish consensus.

Attribute positions only when supported by a source.

## Open questions and unresolved matters

For each open item, provide:

* Question or issue.
* Why it remains unresolved.
* Information or decision required.
* Responsible owner, if assigned.
* Relevant deadline or upcoming meeting.
* Consequence of continued delay.

## Current implications

Explain what the prior discussions mean for the current situation, including:

* Existing decisions that should guide the next discussion.
* Commitments that should be honored or reviewed.
* Questions that should not be reopened without new evidence.
* Matters requiring confirmation or reconsideration.
* Risks created by inconsistency with prior direction.
* Upcoming decisions, meetings, or milestones affected.

## Source traceability and confidence

For each material finding, identify:

* Source title.
* Source date.
* Source type.
* Relevant section or context.
* Attribution, when applicable.
* Confidence level: high, medium, or low.

Clearly distinguish:

* Documented facts.
* Participant positions.
* Proposals.
* Assumptions.
* Recommendations.
* Formal decisions.

## Human-review requirement

An authorized human must review the summary for factual accuracy, attribution, completeness, confidentiality, and interpretation before it is used to represent institutional history or prior stakeholder positions.
