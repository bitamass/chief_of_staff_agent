# Inputs — Action Item Management

## Required inputs

* Meeting agendas, notes, transcripts, and summaries.
* Existing action-item and commitment trackers.
* Decisions that created or changed follow-up work.
* Active initiatives, deliverables, milestones, and deadlines.
* Participant and stakeholder names, roles, and areas of responsibility.
* Prior meeting follow-ups and unresolved commitments.
* Current status updates from action owners.
* Relevant dependencies, risks, issues, and constraints.
* Upcoming meetings or milestones where actions will be reviewed.

## Required action-item fields

For each action item, collect:

* Unique action-item identifier.
* Clear action statement beginning with a verb.
* Source meeting or decision.
* Initiative, deliverable, or strategic goal supported.
* Accountable owner.
* Supporting contributors, if applicable.
* Date assigned.
* Due date.
* Priority.
* Current status.
* Completion criteria or expected evidence.
* Dependencies.
* Risks or blockers.
* Next review date or meeting.
* Date completed, when applicable.
* Source and last-updated date.

## Status definitions

Use consistent status values:

* **Proposed:** Identified but not yet confirmed.
* **Open:** Confirmed and ready to begin.
* **In progress:** Work has started.
* **Blocked:** Progress cannot continue because of a dependency or issue.
* **At risk:** Completion by the due date is uncertain.
* **Overdue:** Due date has passed without documented completion.
* **Complete:** Completion criteria have been met and evidence is available.
* **Deferred:** Authorized decision has moved the action to a later date.
* **Cancelled:** Authorized decision has ended the action.

## Action-item signals to review

Look for:

* Explicit commitments such as “will,” “must,” “prepare,” “send,” or “follow up.”
* Requests assigned to a named person or team.
* Decisions that require implementation or communication.
* Unresolved questions requiring additional analysis.
* Deliverables required before an upcoming meeting or milestone.
* Actions carried forward from earlier meetings.
* Completed work lacking confirmation or evidence.
* Actions without an owner, due date, or completion criteria.
* Duplicate or conflicting action items.
* Actions whose scope, owner, or deadline changed.

## Optional supporting inputs

* Initiative charters and project plans.
* Work-management system exports.
* Email or collaboration-platform follow-ups.
* Dependency maps.
* Risk and issue registers.
* Resource and capacity information.
* Executive priorities.
* Governance or approval requirements.
* Prior escalation decisions.
* Evidence of completion, such as documents, approvals, or delivered outputs.

## Input validation

Before updating an action item:

* Confirm that it represents work to be completed rather than a decision or discussion topic.
* Verify that the owner accepted or was assigned the action through an authorized process.
* Confirm the due date, priority, and completion criteria.
* Verify status changes using current evidence.
* Do not mark an item complete solely because an owner reports progress.
* Identify actions missing an owner, due date, dependency, or completion criterion.
* Identify duplicate, conflicting, outdated, or superseded actions.
* Preserve the source meeting or decision for traceability.
* Do not infer agreement, ownership, completion, cancellation, or deadline changes without evidence.
