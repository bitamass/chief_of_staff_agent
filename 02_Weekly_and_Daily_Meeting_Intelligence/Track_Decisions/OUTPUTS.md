# Outputs — Track Decisions

## Executive decision summary

Provide a concise overview of:

* New decisions recorded during the review period.
* Active decisions currently governing work.
* Decisions awaiting confirmation or documentation.
* Decisions with delayed or at-risk implementation.
* Decisions that were superseded, reversed, expired, or closed.
* Actions or initiatives operating inconsistently with approved direction.
* Decisions requiring leadership review.

## Decision register

For each decision, provide:

* Decision identifier.
* Clear decision statement.
* Decision date.
* Decision owner or authorized authority.
* Source meeting, document, or communication.
* Related strategic goal, initiative, deliverable, risk, or issue.
* Selected option or approved direction.
* Documented rationale.
* Conditions, limitations, or assumptions.
* Stakeholders affected or informed.
* Effective date.
* Review or expiration date.
* Current decision status.
* Implementation status.
* Related or superseding decisions.
* Source reference.
* Confidence level: high, medium, or low.

## Decision status categories

Organize decisions into:

* **Pending confirmation:** Decision wording, authority, or source requires verification.
* **Approved:** Authorized direction has been formally established.
* **In effect:** Decision currently governs work.
* **Implementation in progress:** Required follow-through is underway.
* **Partially implemented:** Some required actions remain incomplete.
* **Implemented:** Approved direction and required actions have been completed.
* **Deferred:** Decision was formally postponed.
* **Superseded:** A later decision replaced it.
* **Reversed:** Authorized leadership formally changed the direction.
* **Expired:** Defined review or end date was reached.
* **Closed:** Implementation and follow-through are complete.

## Implementation tracking

For each approved decision requiring follow-through, provide:

* Required action.
* Accountable owner.
* Due date.
* Current status.
* Dependencies.
* Risks or blockers.
* Evidence of progress or completion.
* Related meeting or review checkpoint.
* Consequence of delay.
* Variance from the approved direction.

## Decision relationships and conflicts

Identify:

* Decisions that depend on other decisions.
* Later decisions that modify or replace earlier decisions.
* Conflicting decision records.
* Actions or initiatives inconsistent with an active decision.
* Decisions affecting multiple initiatives.
* Decisions lacking corresponding implementation actions.
* Work proceeding without a traceable authorization.
* Decisions approaching a review or expiration date.

## Decisions requiring attention

Identify decisions that need:

* Confirmation of wording or authority.
* Formal documentation.
* Implementation follow-up.
* Resolution of a blocker.
* Review of changed assumptions or evidence.
* Confirmation of continued applicability.
* Leadership reconsideration.
* Communication to affected stakeholders.

Explain why attention is required and identify the responsible role and target date.

## Decision timeline

Present a chronological view of:

* Original decision.
* Subsequent confirmations or modifications.
* Related implementation milestones.
* Review dates.
* Superseding or reversing decisions.
* Final implementation or closure.

Preserve the original decision and complete change history.

## Information gaps and confidence

Identify:

* Missing decision authority.
* Unclear decision wording.
* Missing rationale or conditions.
* Unverified source records.
* Missing implementation owners or deadlines.
* Conflicting versions.
* Unverified completion claims.
* Unknown review or expiration requirements.
* Confidence level for each material decision record.

## Human-review requirement

An authorized human must verify the decision wording, authority, source, status, implementation, and any superseding or reversing direction before the decision register is treated as authoritative or communicated to stakeholders.
