# Evaluation — Five Day Work List Of Deliverables

The result passes when:

- It answers the specific five day work list of deliverables question.
- Every material claim is traceable to dated evidence.
- The five-day executive work plan has the required fields and accountable owners.
- Unknowns, conflicts, and assumptions are explicit.
- Recommendations are practical and clearly separated from facts.
- Keep the plan achievable and distinguish commitments from recommendations.
