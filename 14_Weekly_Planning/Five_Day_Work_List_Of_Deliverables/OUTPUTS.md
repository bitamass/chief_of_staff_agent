# Outputs

## Five-day delivery overview

- Week dates and primary delivery objective.
- Most important deliverables for the week.
- Deliverables expected to be completed, reviewed, approved, or meaningfully advanced.
- Major decisions, dependencies, and risks.
- Capacity or scheduling constraints.
- Material changes from the prior delivery plan.

## Five-day work list

For each deliverable:

- Priority rank.
- Deliverable name.
- Required result.
- Related weekly priority, initiative, or commitment.
- Responsible owner and accountable person.
- Planned workdays.
- Due date.
- Current status.
- Weekly target status.
- Estimated effort.
- Dependencies and blockers.
- Completion and acceptance criteria.
- Recommended next action.
- Source and confidence level.

## Day-by-day delivery schedule

### Day 1: Establish and prepare

- Deliverables to begin or continue.
- Required source materials and prerequisites.
- Decisions or approvals to secure.
- Work blocks and responsible owners.
- Interim outputs expected by the end of the day.
- Risks or blockers to address.

### Day 2: Develop and validate

- Deliverable work to complete.
- Required collaboration or specialist input.
- Preliminary review or validation.
- Dependencies to confirm.
- Interim outputs expected by the end of the day.
- Required corrective action.

### Day 3: Integrate and review

- Work products to consolidate.
- Cross-team dependencies to reconcile.
- Drafts or prototypes ready for review.
- Reviewer and review deadline.
- Feedback expected.
- Schedule or quality risks requiring attention.

### Day 4: Revise and prepare for acceptance

- Review comments to resolve.
- Quality checks and evidence to assemble.
- Required approvals or endorsements.
- Final dependencies to close.
- Deliverables expected to become ready for acceptance.
- Recovery action for delayed work.

### Day 5: Deliver, accept, and carry forward

- Final deliverables due.
- Acceptance or approval required.
- Evidence and documentation to preserve.
- Stakeholder communication required.
- Work that must carry into the next week.
- Reasons for incomplete work and revised next steps.

## Deliverable readiness dashboard

For each deliverable:

- Readiness classification: ready, partially ready, not ready, or awaiting information.
- Required source materials.
- Owner and contributor readiness.
- Decision and approval readiness.
- Review and acceptance readiness.
- Current blocker.
- Action required to improve readiness.
- Person responsible and deadline.

## Milestone and dependency schedule

- Deliverable.
- Required milestone.
- Planned day.
- Predecessor.
- Successor.
- Shared resource or participant.
- Risk if the sequence slips.
- Recommended coordination action.

## Decisions and approvals needed

- Decision or approval statement.
- Related deliverable.
- Required authority.
- Deadline.
- Current readiness.
- Required evidence or reviewers.
- Consequence of delay.
- Recommended preparation and escalation action.

## Capacity and workload report

- Available hours by person and day.
- Planned delivery effort.
- Meeting and operational workload.
- Overallocated people or days.
- Deliverables that do not fit within capacity.
- Recommended delegation, sequence change, scope adjustment, or additional support.
- Contingency capacity preserved.

## Risk and blocker report

- Affected deliverable.
- Risk, issue, or blocker.
- Owner and current status.
- Schedule, quality, scope, or acceptance impact.
- Current response.
- Decision, information, resource, or authority required.
- Response deadline.
- Escalation condition and pathway.

## Delivery trade-off recommendations

For each deliverable that may not fit:

- Deliverable name.
- Recommendation: retain, delegate, reduce scope, defer, separate, or stop.
- Reason.
- Consequence and risk.
- Proposed owner or revised date.
- Effect on downstream work.
- Human authorization required.

## Completion and acceptance checklist

For each deliverable:

- Required output completed.
- Completion criteria satisfied.
- Quality review completed.
- Required evidence attached.
- Reviewer comments resolved.
- Required approval or acceptance recorded.
- Related tasks and records updated.
- Stakeholders notified.
- Remaining exceptions documented.
- Follow-up owner and date confirmed.

## End-of-week delivery record

- Deliverables completed and accepted.
- Deliverables ready for review.
- Deliverables partially completed.
- Deliverables blocked, deferred, or canceled.
- Decisions and actions created.
- Variance from the weekly plan.
- Work carried into the next week.
- Source, rationale, owner, and revised date for each carryover item.
