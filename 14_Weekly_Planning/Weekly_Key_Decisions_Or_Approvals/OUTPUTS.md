# Outputs

## Weekly decisions and approvals overview

- Week dates and review scope.
- Total number of decision and approval items.
- Items ready for action.
- Items partially ready or awaiting information.
- Items approaching or exceeding deadlines.
- Items carried forward from the previous week.
- Highest-consequence items requiring executive attention.
- Material authority, evidence, or capacity constraints.

## Prioritized weekly decision queue

For each item:

- Priority rank and title.
- Decision or approval required.
- Decision-maker or approving authority.
- Requestor and accountable owner.
- Scheduled review date.
- Final response deadline.
- Current status.
- Readiness classification.
- Related weekly priority, initiative, milestone, or deliverable.
- Consequence of delay.
- Recommended next action.
- Source and confidence level.

## Day-by-day decision schedule

For each working day:

- Decisions or approvals scheduled.
- Required preparatory work.
- Pre-reads or evidence due.
- Stakeholders or specialists to consult.
- Meeting or approval forum.
- Expected decision outcome.
- Follow-up actions and communications.
- Decisions affected by an earlier item in the week.

## Decision-readiness brief

For each material decision:

### Decision at a glance

- Clear decision statement.
- Why the decision is required this week.
- Decision-maker and authority.
- Deadline and scheduled forum.
- Related objective, initiative, milestone, or commitment.
- Consequence of delay or no action.

### Options and trade-offs

- Available options.
- Benefits and disadvantages of each option.
- Cost, timing, resource, risk, and implementation implications.
- Reversibility.
- Assumptions and uncertainties.
- Documented recommendation and source.
- Alternative or dissenting views.

### Evidence and context

- Key supporting facts and measures.
- Relevant prior decisions and commitments.
- Stakeholder and specialist input.
- Evidence changes since the previous review.
- Source dates and confidence.
- Missing or conflicting information.

### Risks and dependencies

- Risks associated with each option.
- Risks of delay or inaction.
- Required mitigation or conditions.
- Dependencies on other decisions, people, resources, systems, or vendors.
- Downstream deliverables and meetings affected.
- Escalation conditions.

### Readiness determination

- Ready, partially ready, not ready, or awaiting information.
- Basis for the classification.
- Material readiness gaps.
- Owner and deadline for closing each gap.
- Earliest reasonable decision point.
- Whether conditional approval is feasible.

## Decision dependency map

- Decision or approval.
- Required predecessor decision, evidence, or review.
- Decisions or deliverables dependent on it.
- Shared authority, participant, or resource.
- Required sequence.
- Risk if the sequence slips.
- Recommended coordination action.

## Authority and governance exceptions

- Item without a confirmed decision-maker.
- Request exceeding the identified authority.
- Missing reviewer, approver, endorsement, or quorum.
- Potential conflict-of-interest or separation-of-duty concern.
- Missing specialist or governance review.
- Conflict with policy, prior decisions, or delegated authority.
- Recommended correction or escalation pathway.

## Readiness-gap action list

For each gap:

- Affected decision.
- Missing information, review, participant, or authority.
- Why the gap is material.
- Responsible owner.
- Completion deadline.
- Effect on the decision schedule.
- Recommended corrective action.
- Escalation date if unresolved.

## Weekly disposition recommendations

For each item:

- Decide or approve this week.
- Approve with explicit conditions.
- Request targeted additional evidence.
- Seek specialist review.
- Return for revision.
- Separate into multiple decisions.
- Defer to a stated date or triggering condition.
- Escalate to the appropriate authority.
- Decline or withdraw.

Include the rationale, required human authority, and next action.

## Capacity and meeting-load assessment

- Number and complexity of decisions by day.
- Estimated preparation and review time.
- Decision-maker and specialist availability.
- Competing meetings or deadlines.
- Items unlikely to receive adequate review.
- Recommended sequencing, delegation of preparation, or meeting adjustment.
- Contingency time for emerging decisions.

## Decision record template

- Decision identifier.
- Final decision or approval status.
- Decision-maker or approving body.
- Decision date.
- Approved option or authorized action.
- Conditions, limitations, or expiration.
- Rationale.
- Evidence reviewed.
- Dissenting or alternative views.
- Follow-up actions, owners, and deadlines.
- Communication and distribution requirements.
- Next review date, if applicable.

## End-of-week continuity report

- Decisions and approvals completed.
- Conditional approvals and outstanding conditions.
- Items deferred, rejected, withdrawn, or escalated.
- Decisions carried into the following week.
- Reason for each carryover.
- Remaining readiness gaps.
- Revised owner, deadline, and next action.
- Downstream plans or records requiring updates.
