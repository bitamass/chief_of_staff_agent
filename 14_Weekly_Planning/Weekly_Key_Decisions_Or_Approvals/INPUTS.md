# Inputs

## Weekly decision-review context

- Week start and end dates, time zone, and working-day assumptions.
- Executive, leadership team, committee, or governance body responsible for review.
- Organizational unit, initiative, program, or portfolio in scope.
- Current strategic and operational priorities.
- Applicable decision rights, approval thresholds, policies, and delegated authorities.
- Scheduled leadership, governance, or decision meetings during the week.
- Intended audience and review cadence.

## Candidate decisions and approvals

For each item:

- Unique identifier and concise title.
- Clear decision or approval statement.
- Request type: decision, approval, endorsement, authorization, acceptance, exception, or risk acceptance.
- Date submitted and source.
- Requestor, sponsor, preparer, and accountable owner.
- Proposed decision-maker or approving authority.
- Current status.
- Required response date.
- Related initiative, milestone, deliverable, contract, policy, risk, issue, or commitment.
- Scheduled meeting or review forum.

## Business and operational context

- Problem, opportunity, obligation, or condition requiring action.
- Desired outcome.
- Strategic or operational alignment.
- Scope and affected organizational areas.
- Expected value, benefit, or required compliance outcome.
- Relevant prior decisions and commitments.
- Current state and reason action is required this week.
- Consequence of approval, rejection, delay, or no action.

## Options and recommendations

- Options available to the decision-maker.
- Description and implementation requirements for each option.
- Documented recommendation and recommending party.
- Evaluation criteria.
- Expected benefits and disadvantages.
- Financial, operational, technical, workforce, security, privacy, legal, clinical, compliance, procurement, vendor, and reputational implications.
- Reversibility and recovery options.
- Assumptions supporting each option.
- Alternatives considered and excluded, with rationale.

## Decision evidence

- Decision memo, business case, proposal, charter, contract, budget, analysis, or supporting report.
- Relevant measures, forecasts, costs, benefits, and performance data.
- Source and date of each material fact.
- Specialist assessments or reviews.
- Stakeholder feedback and documented positions.
- Prior meeting notes, decisions, and unresolved questions.
- Confidence level and limitations of the available evidence.
- Evidence that has materially changed since the previous review.

## Authority and governance

- Authorized decision-maker or approving body.
- Authority level and applicable threshold.
- Required co-approvers, reviewers, endorsers, or advisors.
- Quorum, voting, or formal-meeting requirements.
- Delegated authority and limitations.
- Conflict-of-interest or separation-of-duty requirements.
- Required finance, legal, procurement, clinical, security, privacy, HR, compliance, records, or governance review.
- Escalation pathway if authority or consensus is unavailable.

## Readiness requirements

- Required documents and analyses.
- Required stakeholder consultation.
- Required specialist reviews.
- Required financial and resource validation.
- Required implementation or change-management plan.
- Required risk assessment and mitigation plan.
- Named implementation owner.
- Unresolved questions or readiness gaps.
- Owner and deadline for resolving each gap.

## Weekly timing and sequencing

- Decision or approval deadline.
- Meeting or review forum where action is expected.
- Preparation deadline.
- Required pre-read distribution date.
- Sequence among related decisions.
- Decisions that must occur before a deliverable, milestone, contract, communication, or task can proceed.
- Time-sensitive opportunity or nonrecoverable deadline.
- Effect on subsequent meetings or decisions during the week.

## Risks and dependencies

- Risks associated with each option.
- Risks of delay or no action.
- Required conditions, mitigations, or contingencies.
- Dependencies on resources, systems, vendors, approvals, participants, or other decisions.
- Downstream work affected.
- Escalation thresholds.
- Residual risks requiring formal acceptance.

## Stakeholder context

- Stakeholders affected by the decision.
- Stakeholder roles, interests, concerns, and documented positions.
- Required consultation, endorsement, or notification.
- Areas of agreement and disagreement.
- Commitments already made.
- Sensitive communication requirements.
- Stakeholders missing from the current process.

## Prior-week continuity

- Decisions carried forward from the previous week.
- Reason the decision was not completed.
- Evidence, review, or authority still outstanding.
- Changes in options, recommendation, urgency, risk, or consequence.
- Prior deferral date or condition.
- New downstream impacts created by continued delay.

## Source and data quality

- Source and date for the decision statement, deadline, authority, options, and recommendation.
- Distinction between confirmed facts, estimates, assumptions, interpretations, and proposals.
- Missing, outdated, inconsistent, or unverified information.
- Conflicting recommendations or specialist assessments.
- Confidence in cost, benefit, timing, and risk estimates.
- Items requiring human or qualified specialist validation.
