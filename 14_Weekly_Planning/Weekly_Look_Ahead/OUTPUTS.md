# Outputs

## Weekly look-ahead summary

- Week dates and concise statement of the week’s leadership focus.
- Three to seven most important outcomes.
- Key decisions and approvals.
- Major deliverables and milestones.
- Critical stakeholder engagements.
- Material risks, blockers, and emerging opportunities.
- Primary capacity or scheduling constraints.
- Significant changes from the prior weekly outlook.

## What requires executive attention

For each attention item:

- Issue, decision, opportunity, or commitment.
- Why it matters this week.
- Executive action required.
- Deadline or decision point.
- Owner and supporting participants.
- Consequence of delay.
- Required preparation.
- Source and confidence level.

## Day-by-day outlook

For each working day:

- Primary outcome.
- Key meetings and expected results.
- Decisions or approvals due.
- Deliverables and milestones.
- Preparation and follow-up requirements.
- Stakeholder communications.
- Risks, issues, or blockers to monitor.
- Protected focus time.
- Available contingency capacity.
- Dependencies affecting the following day.

## Priority and outcome dashboard

For each priority:

- Rank and priority title.
- Required weekly outcome.
- Related strategic objective or initiative.
- Accountable owner and responsible lead.
- Executive role.
- Target date.
- Current status and readiness.
- Dependencies and risks.
- Weekly success measure.
- Recommended next action.

## Meeting and preparation outlook

For each material meeting:

- Date, time, title, and objective.
- Executive’s role.
- Key participants.
- Decision, alignment, or contribution expected.
- Required pre-reads and preparation.
- Prior decisions, actions, risks, or commitments.
- Readiness status.
- Missing information.
- Recommended follow-up.

## Decisions and approvals schedule

- Decision or approval statement.
- Related meeting, priority, or milestone.
- Decision-maker or approving authority.
- Deadline.
- Readiness classification.
- Options and documented recommendation.
- Required evidence and reviewers.
- Consequence of delay.
- Preparation owner and next action.

## Deliverables and milestone outlook

- Deliverable or milestone.
- Related initiative or priority.
- Owner and due date.
- Current status.
- Weekly target.
- Executive involvement required.
- Dependencies and blockers.
- Acceptance criteria and authority.
- Risk to completion.
- Downstream impact.

## Risk, issue, and opportunity outlook

For each item:

- Description.
- Type: risk, issue, blocker, or opportunity.
- Owner and current status.
- Trend or time sensitivity.
- Affected objective, initiative, meeting, or deliverable.
- Current response or action.
- Executive decision, resource, or communication needed.
- Response deadline.
- Escalation or opportunity-expiration condition.

## Stakeholder engagement plan

- Stakeholder or group.
- Purpose and desired outcome.
- Recommended timing.
- Executive role.
- Known interests, concerns, or commitments.
- Required materials or talking points.
- Sensitivity or communication constraints.
- Follow-up owner and deadline.

## Dependency and sequencing report

- Priority, meeting, decision, or deliverable.
- Required predecessor.
- Downstream item affected.
- Shared resource, person, approval, or system.
- Required sequence.
- Risk if the dependency slips.
- Recommended coordination action.
- Owner and response deadline.

## Capacity and conflict report

- Available capacity by day.
- Planned meeting, preparation, and focus workload.
- Overallocated people or days.
- Scheduling conflicts.
- Insufficient preparation, transition, or follow-up time.
- Priorities or deliverables that do not fit.
- Recommended delegation, deferral, scope adjustment, meeting change, or added support.
- Human authorization required.

## Preparation for the following week

- Decisions or deliverables due early in the next week.
- Meetings requiring advance preparation.
- Stakeholders requiring early engagement.
- Dependencies that must be resolved now.
- Risks or opportunities likely to continue.
- Materials or reviews that should begin during the current week.
- Owner and target date.

## Source and confidence report

- Sources supporting material conclusions.
- Date and freshness of each source.
- Confirmed facts, assumptions, forecasts, and interpretations.
- Missing or conflicting information.
- Confidence in each significant outlook item.
- Items requiring human validation before use or distribution.
