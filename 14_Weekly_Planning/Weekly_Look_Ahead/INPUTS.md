# Inputs

## Planning horizon

- Week start and end dates, time zone, and working-day assumptions.
- Executive, leadership team, initiative, program, or portfolio in scope.
- Current strategic objectives, quarterly priorities, or OKRs.
- Major organizational, operational, regulatory, market, or external conditions expected during the week.
- Planned leave, travel, holidays, reduced availability, and protected focus periods.
- Intended audience and person authorized to confirm the weekly outlook.

## Prior-week continuity

- Priorities and deliverables carried forward from the previous week.
- Decisions, approvals, and commitments completed during the previous week.
- Incomplete, deferred, blocked, canceled, or superseded work.
- Reasons unfinished work remains open.
- New actions, risks, issues, or dependencies created.
- Changes in scope, timing, ownership, assumptions, or urgency.
- Lessons that should affect the upcoming week.

## Weekly priorities and expected outcomes

For each proposed weekly priority:

- Required outcome.
- Related strategic goal, initiative, program, or operational responsibility.
- Sponsor, accountable owner, responsible lead, and contributors.
- Current status and readiness.
- Required executive role: decide, approve, review, sponsor, communicate, prepare, or unblock.
- Target date or milestone.
- Expected value or benefit.
- Consequence of delay.
- Estimated executive and team effort.
- Source and source date.

## Calendar and scheduled events

For each event:

- Meeting or event title.
- Date, start time, end time, and location or virtual platform.
- Organizer, attendees, and participant roles.
- Objective and desired outcome.
- Executive’s expected role.
- Related priority, initiative, milestone, decision, or stakeholder.
- Required preparation and follow-up.
- Fixed, flexible, tentative, recurring, delegable, or optional status.
- Travel, transition, or setup requirements.
- Confidentiality and distribution restrictions.

## Decisions and approvals

- Decision or approval statement.
- Decision-maker or approving authority.
- Requestor, sponsor, and accountable owner.
- Deadline and scheduled review forum.
- Options and documented recommendation.
- Required evidence, reviewers, and specialist assessments.
- Current readiness.
- Risks and trade-offs.
- Consequence of delay or no action.
- Downstream work dependent on the decision.

## Deliverables and milestones

- Deliverable or milestone name.
- Required outcome and completion criteria.
- Related priority or initiative.
- Owner, contributors, reviewers, and approvers.
- Current status and due date.
- Required executive participation.
- Dependencies and required resources.
- Evidence supporting the status.
- Consequence of delay.
- Upcoming work affected by completion.

## Actions and commitments

- Open actions and commitments.
- Source meeting, decision, request, or communication.
- Responsible owner and accountable person.
- Due date and current status.
- Overdue or blocked items.
- Required executive action.
- Commitments to significant internal or external stakeholders.
- Items that may be delegated, deferred, combined, or completed asynchronously.

## Risks, issues, blockers, and opportunities

- Material risks and issues expected to affect the week.
- Emerging opportunities requiring timely attention.
- Owner, status, rating, trend, and response.
- Warning or escalation thresholds.
- Required mitigation, containment, decision, or resource.
- Blockers and dependencies.
- Opportunity window and consequence of delay.
- Affected priorities, meetings, deliverables, and stakeholders.

## Stakeholder engagement

- Stakeholders requiring a meeting, response, update, decision, or follow-up.
- Stakeholder role, interest, influence, and documented concerns.
- Existing commitments and communication deadlines.
- Desired engagement outcome.
- Required preparation or talking points.
- Sensitive relationships or messages requiring executive participation.
- Engagement that may be delegated.
- Follow-up owner and timing.

## Dependencies and sequencing

- Predecessor and successor work.
- Decisions required before execution.
- Shared resources, systems, vendors, specialists, or participants.
- Cross-initiative dependencies.
- Work that must occur early in the week.
- Work requiring preparation for a later meeting or milestone.
- Dependencies extending into the following week.
- Cascading effects if a dependency slips.

## Capacity and workload

- Executive and team availability by day.
- Meeting, preparation, travel, transition, and follow-up time.
- Focus-work requirements.
- Routine operational workload.
- Resource or expertise constraints.
- Days or people with excessive demand.
- Work that may be delegated, deferred, shortened, combined, or stopped.
- Contingency capacity for unexpected developments.

## External developments

- Approved external intelligence relevant to the week.
- Regulatory, policy, market, vendor, technology, clinical, financial, or reputational developments.
- Source, publication date, and relevance.
- Potential impact on current decisions, risks, priorities, or stakeholders.
- Required verification or specialist interpretation.
- Whether the information is confirmed, emerging, or speculative.

## Source and data quality

- Source and date for every material priority, event, deadline, commitment, risk, and forecast.
- Distinction between confirmed commitments, tentative plans, estimates, assumptions, and interpretations.
- Missing, outdated, conflicting, or unverified information.
- Confidence in readiness, timing, effort, and impact assessments.
- Items requiring executive, owner, stakeholder, or specialist confirmation.
