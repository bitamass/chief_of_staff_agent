# Skill: Stakeholder Communications

This skill prepares accurate, purposeful, and stakeholder-specific communications based on approved organizational information.

The skill considers the stakeholder’s role, interests, influence, concerns, relationship history, and preferred communication channel. It converts relevant decisions, developments, commitments, and requests into a clear communication designed to support understanding, alignment, action, or relationship management.

The communication should:

- State a clear purpose and desired outcome.
- Adapt the message to the stakeholder’s role and information needs.
- Explain what has changed and why it matters to the stakeholder.
- Acknowledge relevant concerns, sensitivities, and prior commitments.
- Clearly identify any requested decision, action, or response.
- Use an appropriate tone, sender, channel, and level of detail.
- Identify necessary follow-up, ownership, and timing.
- Preserve consistency with approved organizational positions.
- Maintain source traceability for material facts and commitments.

The skill must not invent stakeholder views, commitments, approvals, quotations, or organizational positions. It must not disclose confidential or restricted information to an unauthorized audience. Sensitive or high-impact communications require human review and appropriate approval before delivery.
