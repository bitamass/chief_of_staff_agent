# Inputs

- Communication purpose, desired outcome, and required response.
- Intended stakeholder or stakeholder group.
- Stakeholder role, interests, influence, concerns, and communication preferences.
- Relevant relationship history and previous communications.
- Initiative, decision, issue, or organizational context.
- Approved facts, status updates, milestones, and supporting evidence.
- Commitments previously made to or by the stakeholder.
- Known concerns, sensitivities, disagreements, and potential objections.
- Decisions, actions, or support requested from the stakeholder.
- Executive position, approved talking points, and matters that remain negotiable.
- Communication channel, sender, timing, and required follow-up.
- Confidentiality, legal, privacy, accessibility, and approval requirements.
