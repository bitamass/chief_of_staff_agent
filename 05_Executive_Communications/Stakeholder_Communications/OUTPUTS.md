# Outputs

- Stakeholder-specific communication objective and recommended approach.
- Draft email, letter, briefing note, or talking points for the selected channel.
- Concise explanation of the relevant situation or development.
- Key messages tailored to the stakeholder’s role and interests.
- Clear request, decision, action, or response needed.
- Treatment of anticipated concerns, objections, or sensitivities.
- Prior commitments and outstanding matters requiring acknowledgment.
- Recommended tone, timing, sender, and communication channel.
- Follow-up steps, accountable owner, and expected response date.
- Items requiring executive confirmation or formal approval.
- Source references for material facts and commitments.
