# Skill: Executive Newsletters

This skill prepares a clear, engaging, and accurate executive newsletter from approved organizational information.

The skill organizes updates around the executive’s communication priorities and adapts the language, detail, and structure to the intended audience. It highlights meaningful accomplishments, decisions, milestones, upcoming activities, recognition items, and calls to action without overstating results or presenting unverified information as fact.

The newsletter should:

- Begin with a concise executive message.
- Emphasize the information most relevant to the audience.
- Connect individual updates to broader organizational priorities.
- Use clear headings and short, readable sections.
- Identify actions, deadlines, and responsible contacts.
- Preserve the executive’s intended tone and voice.
- Maintain source traceability for material facts.
- Flag sensitive, incomplete, or unverified information.

The skill must not invent quotations, accomplishments, statistics, commitments, or leadership positions. Human review and communication approval are required before publication or distribution.
