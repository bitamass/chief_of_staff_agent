# Skill: Board Meeting Summaries

This skill transforms approved board meeting records and supporting materials into a concise, source-linked executive summary.

The skill identifies the meeting’s principal discussions, decisions, motions, voting outcomes, commitments, unresolved matters, and implications for institutional priorities. It distinguishes formal board action from discussion, recommendations, and items deferred for future consideration.

For each material conclusion, preserve traceability to the agenda, minutes, or supporting document. Do not infer agreement, approval, ownership, or voting outcomes when they are not explicitly documented. Clearly label missing, conflicting, confidential, or unverified information.

The final summary should help an executive quickly understand:

- What the board discussed.
- What the board formally decided or approved.
- What remains unresolved.
- Who is responsible for follow-up.
- What requires leadership attention before the next meeting.

Human review is required before the summary is distributed or treated as an official board record.
