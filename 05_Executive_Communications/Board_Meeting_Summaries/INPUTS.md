# Inputs

- Board meeting agenda, including topics, presenters, and planned decisions.
- Approved meeting minutes, transcripts, recordings, or detailed notes.
- Board materials, presentations, reports, and supporting documents.
- Motions, resolutions, votes, approvals, and deferred decisions.
- Questions and concerns raised by board members.
- Commitments, action items, owners, and expected completion dates.
- Relevant governance requirements and confidentiality classifications.
- Intended audience and desired summary length.
