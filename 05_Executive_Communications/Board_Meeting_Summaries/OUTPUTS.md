# Outputs

- Executive summary of the meeting’s most significant discussions and outcomes.
- Decisions, motions, resolutions, approvals, and voting results.
- Key questions, concerns, disagreements, and unresolved matters.
- Action items with accountable owners and due dates.
- Matters deferred or requiring additional information.
- Implications for institutional priorities, initiatives, risk, or governance.
- Topics requiring executive attention or follow-up.
- Source references connecting each material conclusion to the meeting record.
