# Inputs

- Announcement purpose, subject, and desired outcome.
- Approved organizational decision or change being announced.
- Intended audience, affected groups, and distribution channels.
- Effective date, implementation schedule, and important deadlines.
- Rationale and relevant organizational context.
- Expected effects on employees, teams, operations, or stakeholders.
- Required actions and responsible contacts.
- Leadership sponsor and authorized sender.
- Approved quotations, facts, statistics, and supporting information.
- Frequently asked questions and anticipated audience concerns.
- Legal, human resources, privacy, labor, accessibility, or communication guidance.
- Confidentiality, sequencing, approval, and publication requirements.
