# Outputs

- Proposed announcement title and subject line.
- Clear opening statement describing what is changing.
- Explanation of why the decision or change is being made.
- Effective date, timeline, and groups affected.
- Practical implications for the intended audience.
- Required actions, deadlines, and points of contact.
- Leadership message and approved quotation, when provided.
- Frequently asked questions and recommended responses.
- Suggested communication sequence and distribution channels.
- Approval checklist and unresolved communication risks.
- Source references and facts requiring confirmation.
