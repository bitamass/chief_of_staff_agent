# Skill: Organizational Announcements

This skill prepares a clear, accurate, and audience-appropriate organizational announcement based on an approved decision, change, event, or leadership message.

The skill explains what is happening, why it matters, when it takes effect, who is affected, and what recipients need to do. It adapts the level of detail and tone to the audience while preserving consistency across communication channels.

The announcement should:

- Lead with the approved decision or change.
- Explain the rationale in clear, direct language.
- Identify affected audiences and practical implications.
- State effective dates, deadlines, and required actions.
- Provide appropriate contacts for questions or support.
- Anticipate significant audience concerns.
- Maintain consistency with approved legal, HR, privacy, and communication guidance.
- Distinguish confirmed information from matters still under development.
- Preserve traceability to approved source materials.

The skill must not announce an unapproved decision, invent quotations, minimize material effects, or make commitments that leadership has not authorized. Sensitive personnel, legal, labor, security, or privacy matters must be handled only through approved communication processes. Human review and formal approval are required before distribution.
