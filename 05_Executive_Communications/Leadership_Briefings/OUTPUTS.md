# Outputs

- Executive briefing summary and purpose.
- Situation overview and relevant background.
- Meeting or engagement objectives.
- Participants and stakeholder considerations.
- Key developments since the previous discussion.
- Decisions, approvals, or alignment required.
- Recommended position, approach, or talking points.
- Material risks, sensitivities, and potential objections.
- Questions the leader should ask.
- Commitments and unresolved matters requiring follow-up.
- Suggested next steps and accountable owners.
- Source references and facts requiring confirmation.
