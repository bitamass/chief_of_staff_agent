# Leadership Briefings

## Purpose
Perform **leadership briefings** as a distinct executive workflow—not as a generic summary.

## Skill logic
1. Select only audience- and decision-relevant approved evidence.
2. Separate facts, decisions, risks, actions, and recommendations.
3. Organize the message for the audience, purpose, channel, and length.
4. Cite material claims and route the draft for human approval.

## Operating rules
- Use INPUTS.md as the evidence contract.
- Produce every item in OUTPUTS.md.
- Cite material findings and explain every score.
- Separate facts, assumptions, analysis, and recommendations.
- Require human review before distribution, approval, reassignment, or action.
