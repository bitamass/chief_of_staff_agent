# Skill: Leadership Briefings

This skill prepares a leader for an upcoming meeting, decision, event, or stakeholder engagement by assembling the most relevant context into a concise, source-linked briefing.

The skill identifies what the leader needs to know, decide, communicate, and ask. It connects prior discussions and commitments with current developments, stakeholder considerations, risks, decision requirements, and anticipated next steps.

The briefing should:

- State the purpose and desired outcome of the engagement.
- Explain why the matter requires leadership attention.
- Summarize only the background necessary for the upcoming discussion.
- Identify participants, interests, influence, and likely perspectives.
- Distinguish established facts from assumptions or recommendations.
- Clearly identify decisions, approvals, or alignment required.
- Provide recommended talking points and questions.
- Surface risks, sensitivities, objections, and unresolved issues.
- Connect conclusions to their supporting sources.
- Fit the leader’s available preparation time.

The skill must not invent stakeholder positions, leadership preferences, decisions, quotations, or supporting evidence. Unverified or conflicting information must be identified clearly. Human review is required before the briefing is used or distributed.
