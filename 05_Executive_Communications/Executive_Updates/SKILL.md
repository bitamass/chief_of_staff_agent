# Skill: Executive Updates

This skill converts approved operational, program, and initiative information into a concise executive update.

The skill emphasizes what has changed since the previous reporting period, why it matters, and where leadership attention or action is required. It separates accomplishments, decisions, risks, dependencies, upcoming milestones, and requests for executive guidance.

The update should:

- Lead with the most significant changes and outcomes.
- Distinguish completed work from work in progress.
- Identify decisions already made and decisions still required.
- Clearly state owners and deadlines for material actions.
- Highlight risks, issues, or dependencies affecting outcomes.
- Compare the current status with the previous reporting period.
- Explain the organizational or strategic significance of each major item.
- Use concise, executive-appropriate language.
- Preserve source traceability for significant statements.

The skill must not invent progress, decisions, metrics, ownership, or deadlines. Missing or conflicting information must be clearly flagged. Human review is required before the update is distributed or treated as an official status report.
