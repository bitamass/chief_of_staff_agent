# Inputs

- Purpose, audience, delivery channel, and required length of the update.
- Executive priorities and current organizational objectives.
- Initiative, program, or operational status reports.
- Milestones completed and measurable results achieved.
- Decisions made since the previous update.
- Open issues, risks, dependencies, and emerging concerns.
- Actions completed, overdue actions, owners, and due dates.
- Upcoming decisions, milestones, meetings, and deadlines.
- Approved performance measures, financial information, and supporting data.
- Previous executive updates and relevant source documents.
- Confidentiality, approval, and distribution requirements.
