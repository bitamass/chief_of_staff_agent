# Outputs

## Executive health summary

- Initiative name and objective.
- Sponsor and owner.
- Current phase.
- Overall health.
- Trend from the previous assessment.
- Most significant accomplishment.
- Most material concern.
- Next milestone.
- Highest-priority decision or intervention.
- Confidence in the assessment.
- Data cutoff date.

## Health scorecard

For each health dimension:

- Current status.
- Previous status.
- Trend.
- Supporting evidence.
- Material variance.
- Owner.
- Required corrective action.
- Target date.
- Confidence level.

Suggested dimensions include:

- Strategic alignment.
- Scope.
- Schedule.
- Financial.
- Resources and capacity.
- Deliverables and quality.
- Outcomes and benefits.
- Risks and issues.
- Dependencies.
- Decisions and actions.
- Stakeholders and adoption.
- Governance and assurance readiness.

## Overall health determination

- Overall rating:
  - On track.
  - Watch.
  - At risk.
  - Off track.
- Rating rationale.
- Health dimensions driving the result.
- Exceptions that override an otherwise favorable aggregate.
- Difference between owner-reported and analytical health, when applicable.
- Required validation.

## Scope health

- Approved versus current scope.
- Scope changes.
- Unresolved requirements.
- Acceptance gaps.
- Change-control status.
- Effect on schedule, cost, outcomes, or risk.
- Leadership decision required.

## Schedule health

- Baseline completion date.
- Current forecast.
- Schedule variance.
- Milestone performance.
- Critical-path status.
- Upcoming milestones.
- Recovery plan.
- Likelihood of achieving the target date.
- Required intervention.

## Financial health

When relevant:

- Budget.
- Actual and committed spending.
- Forecast.
- Variance.
- Benefits forecast and realized.
- Financial risks.
- Funding decisions required.
- Confidence in the financial information.

## Resource health

- Planned versus available capacity.
- Critical skill gaps.
- Competing priorities.
- Key-person dependencies.
- Vendor or partner capacity.
- Expected effect on delivery.
- Recommended resource action.

## Deliverable and quality health

- Deliverables completed.
- Deliverables due next.
- Deliverables at risk.
- Review and approval status.
- Defects or rework.
- Acceptance and implementation status.
- Quality trends.
- Required corrective action.

## Outcome and benefit health

- Intended outcomes.
- Baseline and target.
- Current evidence.
- Forecast.
- Adoption.
- Benefits realized.
- Outcome gaps.
- Attribution and measurement limitations.
- Required leadership attention.

## Risk, issue, and dependency health

- Highest residual risks.
- Critical issues.
- Worsening trends.
- Overdue mitigation or resolution.
- Critical dependencies.
- Single points of failure.
- Decisions or escalation required.

## Decision and accountability health

- Pending decisions.
- Overdue actions.
- Unresolved commitments.
- Ownership gaps.
- Repeated missed deadlines.
- Escalations.
- Specific leadership action requested.

## Stakeholder and governance health

- Sponsor and stakeholder engagement.
- Adoption and change readiness.
- Material concerns or resistance.
- Governance reviews and approvals.
- Assurance gaps.
- Communications required.
- Decisions required before proceeding.

## Recovery and intervention plan

For each health concern:

- Problem.
- Cause.
- Impact.
- Corrective action.
- Owner.
- Due date.
- Resource or decision needed.
- Expected effect.
- Evidence required.
- Escalation trigger.

## Forward view

- Upcoming milestones.
- Decisions.
- Governance reviews.
- Deliverables.
- Dependencies.
- Risks approaching thresholds.
- Expected status change before the next review.
- Executive participation required.

## Data-quality summary

- Stale information.
- Missing evidence.
- Conflicting owner reports.
- Unverified completion claims.
- Low-confidence forecasts.
- Status dimensions requiring specialized review.
