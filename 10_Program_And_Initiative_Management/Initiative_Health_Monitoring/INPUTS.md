# Inputs

## Initiative definition

- Initiative ID and name.
- Initiative description.
- Strategic objective supported.
- Approved charter and scope.
- Expected outcomes and benefits.
- Executive sponsor.
- Accountable owner.
- Program or project manager.
- Supporting teams.
- Current phase.
- Planned start and completion dates.
- Review cadence.
- Source and last update date.

## Health framework

- Approved health dimensions.
- Status definitions.
- Scoring or rating methodology.
- Thresholds for on track, watch, at risk, and off track.
- Escalation criteria.
- Risk appetite and tolerance.
- Reporting cutoff date.
- Previous health assessment.
- Assessment owner.
- Required reviewers and approvers.

## Scope information

- Approved scope.
- Items explicitly out of scope.
- Current scope.
- Scope changes requested or approved.
- Change-control records.
- Requirements.
- Deliverables.
- Acceptance criteria.
- Unresolved scope questions.
- Evidence of scope completion.
- Effect of scope changes on cost, schedule, resources, benefits, and risk.

## Schedule and milestone information

- Baseline schedule.
- Current forecast.
- Critical milestones.
- Planned and actual milestone dates.
- Critical path.
- Schedule variance.
- Float or contingency.
- Upcoming reviews and decision points.
- Delayed activities.
- Recovery plan.
- Dependencies.
- Approved rebaseline information.

## Financial information

When relevant:

- Approved budget.
- Actual spending.
- Committed spending.
- Forecast at completion.
- Variance.
- Funding source.
- Benefits forecast.
- Benefits realized.
- Cost avoidance.
- Financial assumptions.
- Procurement and vendor commitments.
- Financial risks.
- Confidence in estimates.

## Resource and capacity information

- Planned staffing.
- Current staffing.
- Required skills and expertise.
- Capacity and allocation.
- Key-person dependencies.
- Open roles or staffing gaps.
- Vendor and partner resources.
- Competing priorities.
- Resource constraints.
- Resource actions and decisions required.
- Continuity and backup arrangements.

## Deliverable and quality information

For each material deliverable:

- Owner.
- Planned and forecast dates.
- Current status.
- Acceptance criteria.
- Review and approval status.
- Defects, rework, or quality concerns.
- Testing and validation.
- Operational-readiness status.
- Implementation or adoption status.
- Evidence of completion.
- Residual actions.

## Outcome and benefit information

- Intended outcome.
- Baseline.
- Target.
- Current value.
- Forecast.
- Measurement method.
- Data source.
- Adoption and utilization.
- Stakeholder or user outcome.
- Evidence of benefit realization.
- Attribution limitations.
- Timing of expected value.
- Benefits owner.

## Risk and issue information

- Active risks.
- Open issues.
- Ratings, severity, urgency, and trends.
- Owners.
- Mitigation and resolution status.
- Target dates.
- Risks above tolerance.
- Critical issues.
- New and emerging concerns.
- Escalations.
- Contingency readiness.

## Dependency information

- Upstream and downstream dependencies.
- Provider and consumer owners.
- Required and forecast dates.
- Acceptance status.
- Shared-resource dependencies.
- Technology, data, vendor, procurement, approval, governance, or operational dependencies.
- Critical-path effect.
- Workarounds.
- Escalation status.

## Decision, action, and commitment information

- Decisions made.
- Decisions pending.
- Decision-maker and required date.
- Open actions.
- Overdue actions.
- Commitments.
- Owners and due dates.
- Escalations.
- Consequences of delay.
- Follow-up history.

## Stakeholder and governance information

- Stakeholder support and concerns.
- Sponsor engagement.
- Decision-maker participation.
- Governance reviews.
- Required approvals.
- Security, privacy, legal, clinical, regulatory, financial, procurement, policy, accessibility, or records-management readiness.
- Communications and change-management status.
- Audit or assurance findings.

## Data-quality information

- Source systems and owners.
- Data freshness.
- Completeness.
- Accuracy.
- Reconciliation status.
- Conflicting status assessments.
- Missing evidence.
- Stale owner updates.
- Manual adjustments.
- Confidence level.
- Items requiring validation.
