# Outputs

## Milestone register

For each milestone:

- Milestone ID and title.
- Initiative or workstream.
- Owner and approver.
- Baseline date.
- Current forecast date.
- Actual date.
- Status.
- Critical-path designation.
- Entry and exit criteria.
- Dependencies.
- Risks and issues.
- Required decision or approval.
- Next action.
- Source and confidence level.

## Milestone status classification

Each milestone should be classified as:

- Not started.
- In preparation.
- On track.
- Watch.
- At risk.
- Off track.
- Blocked.
- Ready for review.
- Pending decision.
- Pending approval.
- Achieved.
- Achieved with conditions.
- Missed.
- Deferred.
- Cancelled.
- Superseded.

The output must explain the evidence behind Watch, At risk, Off track, Blocked, or Missed status.

## Timeline view

- Milestones achieved.
- Milestones due soon.
- Baseline versus forecast dates.
- Schedule variance.
- Critical-path milestones.
- Decision and approval windows.
- Available float.
- Recovery dates.
- Downstream milestones affected.
- Expected initiative completion effect.

## Milestone readiness assessment

For each near-term milestone:

- Required criteria.
- Criteria satisfied.
- Criteria remaining.
- Evidence available.
- Required reviewer or approver.
- Dependency readiness.
- Resource readiness.
- Governance readiness.
- Likelihood of timely achievement.
- Confidence and gaps.

## Missed and at-risk milestones

For each material concern:

- Milestone.
- Original date.
- Current forecast.
- Days early or late.
- Cause.
- Current impact.
- Downstream effect.
- Recovery plan.
- Owner.
- Required decision, resource, or intervention.
- Escalation level.
- Target recovery date.

## Decision and approval milestones

- Decision required.
- Decision-maker.
- Required date.
- Materials needed.
- Current preparation status.
- Consequence of delay.
- Questions requiring resolution.
- Recommended escalation or preparation action.

## Critical-path analysis

- Critical-path milestones.
- Dependencies with no available float.
- Single points of failure.
- Milestones likely to affect the final completion date.
- Alternative sequencing or recovery options.
- Required leadership action.

## Trend analysis

- Forecast changes since the previous report.
- Repeatedly moved milestones.
- Milestone reliability.
- Common causes of delay.
- Approval or review bottlenecks.
- Dependency patterns.
- Resource constraints.
- Difference between planned and actual milestone performance.

## Portfolio milestone summary

- Total active milestones.
- Milestones achieved during the reporting period.
- Milestones due next.
- Milestones at risk, off track, or blocked.
- Milestones pending decision or approval.
- Milestones by initiative, owner, and status.
- Cross-initiative collisions or shared dependencies.
- Material dates requiring executive awareness.

## Executive attention

- Milestones threatening strategic outcomes.
- Decisions or approvals required.
- Resource or priority conflicts.
- Critical dependencies.
- Required intervention.
- Authorized decision-maker.
- Required decision date.
- Consequence of delayed action.

## Data-quality summary

- Milestones without criteria.
- Missing owners or approvers.
- Stale forecasts.
- Conflicting dates.
- Unsupported completion claims.
- Date changes without approval.
- Low-confidence status.
- Items requiring validation.
