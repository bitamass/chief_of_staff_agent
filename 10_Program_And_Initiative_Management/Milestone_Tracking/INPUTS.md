# Inputs

## Initiative and schedule context

- Initiative, program, project, or workstream name.
- Approved objective and expected outcome.
- Executive sponsor.
- Accountable owner.
- Program or project manager.
- Current phase.
- Baseline start and completion dates.
- Current forecast completion date.
- Reporting period and data cutoff date.
- Approved schedule and roadmap.
- Previous milestone report.
- Scheduling methodology and calendar assumptions.

## Milestone definition

For each milestone:

- Milestone ID and title.
- Description of the event, outcome, decision, approval, or completion point.
- Initiative or workstream.
- Milestone owner.
- Accountable approver.
- Baseline date.
- Current forecast date.
- Actual completion date, when achieved.
- Milestone type:
  - Decision.
  - Deliverable completion.
  - Review.
  - Approval.
  - Funding.
  - Procurement.
  - Technical.
  - Data.
  - Integration.
  - Testing.
  - Security, privacy, legal, clinical, or regulatory.
  - Operational readiness.
  - Launch or release.
  - Adoption.
  - Benefit realization.
- Priority and criticality.
- Critical-path designation.
- Source and last update date.

## Entry and exit criteria

For each milestone:

- Preconditions.
- Required inputs.
- Required deliverables.
- Completion or exit criteria.
- Acceptance criteria.
- Required evidence.
- Required reviewers.
- Required approver.
- Testing or validation requirements.
- Required governance or assurance review.
- Handoff or operational-readiness requirements.
- Conditions that would prevent milestone completion.

## Schedule and progress information

- Planned predecessor activities.
- Planned successor activities.
- Current progress.
- Work remaining.
- Forecast confidence.
- Schedule variance.
- Available float or contingency.
- Review and approval duration.
- Expected decision time.
- Recovery date.
- Approved date changes.
- Percentage complete of supporting work, when evidence supports it.
- Evidence that milestone criteria are satisfied.

## Dependency information

- Upstream dependencies.
- Downstream dependencies.
- Dependency owners.
- Required-by dates.
- Current forecasts.
- Acceptance status.
- Shared-resource dependencies.
- Vendor, technology, data, procurement, governance, or approval dependencies.
- Workarounds or contingencies.
- Effect of dependency delay.

## Decision and approval information

- Decision or approval required.
- Authorized decision-maker.
- Required decision date.
- Decision materials required.
- Current preparation status.
- Outstanding questions.
- Prior decisions.
- Escalation path.
- Consequence of delayed decision.

## Risk and issue information

- Risks threatening the milestone.
- Issues currently affecting it.
- Probability, impact, severity, and urgency.
- Risk or issue owner.
- Mitigation or resolution action.
- Target date.
- Current trend.
- Trigger or early-warning indicator.
- Escalation status.
- Contingency plan.

## Resource and capacity information

- Required roles and expertise.
- Assigned staffing.
- Current availability.
- Key-person dependencies.
- Vendor or partner capacity.
- Funding status.
- Technology or environment availability.
- Competing priorities.
- Resource gap.
- Required resource decision.

## Governance and readiness information

- Required security, privacy, legal, clinical, regulatory, architecture, procurement, accessibility, finance, records-management, or policy review.
- Review owner.
- Submission date.
- Review status.
- Findings or conditions.
- Required remediation.
- Approval or exception status.
- Operational, support, communication, training, or change-readiness requirements.

## Follow-up history

- Previous status updates.
- Forecast changes.
- Missed target dates.
- Recovery commitments.
- Escalations.
- Decisions made.
- Actions assigned.
- Reasons for changes.
- Approvals of reforecast or rebaseline.

## Data-quality inputs

- Source system or document.
- Data owner.
- Last validation date.
- Missing dates, owners, criteria, or evidence.
- Conflicting forecasts.
- Milestones recorded under multiple names.
- Status reported without supporting evidence.
- Confidence level.
- Items requiring human confirmation.
