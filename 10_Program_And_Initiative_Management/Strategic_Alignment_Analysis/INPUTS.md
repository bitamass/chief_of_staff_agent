# Inputs

## Strategic framework

- Organizational mission and vision.
- Approved strategic plan.
- Strategic priorities and objectives.
- Annual operating priorities.
- Executive and board commitments.
- Approved OKRs or performance measures.
- Relevant policy, regulatory, clinical, operational, financial, research, workforce, technology, or sustainability priorities.
- Strategic planning period.
- Source, owner, approval date, and current version.
- Material changes in strategy since initiative approval.

## Initiative definition

- Initiative ID and name.
- Initiative description.
- Charter.
- Business need or problem addressed.
- Executive sponsor.
- Accountable owner.
- Current phase and status.
- Approved start and completion dates.
- Current forecast.
- Scope and exclusions.
- Intended stakeholders or beneficiaries.
- Source and last update.

## Initiative objectives

For each initiative objective:

- Objective statement.
- Strategic objective or priority supported.
- Expected outcome.
- Rationale.
- Owner.
- Baseline.
- Target.
- Timeframe.
- Success measure.
- Evidence supporting the proposed connection.
- Confidence level.

## Scope and deliverables

For each deliverable:

- Deliverable ID and description.
- Initiative objective supported.
- Strategic objective supported.
- Owner.
- Due date.
- Current status.
- Expected contribution.
- Acceptance criteria.
- Evidence of contribution.
- Cost and resource requirement.
- Dependencies.
- Relevant risks and issues.

## Outcome and benefit information

- Intended outcomes.
- Benefits.
- Baseline.
- Target.
- Current value.
- Forecast.
- Benefits owner.
- Measurement method.
- Data source.
- Time to value.
- Duration of value.
- Evidence of realized value.
- Attribution limitations.
- Difference between activity, output, outcome, and benefit.

## Financial and resource information

- Approved budget.
- Actual and forecast cost.
- Staffing and expertise.
- Technology and vendor requirements.
- Operational and support cost.
- Opportunity cost.
- Competing resource demand.
- Investment already made.
- Remaining investment.
- Resource and funding constraints.

## Performance information

- Milestone performance.
- Deliverable status.
- Quality and acceptance.
- Outcome performance.
- Adoption.
- Benefits realized.
- Risks and issues.
- Dependencies.
- Decisions pending.
- Actions overdue.
- Current initiative-health assessment.
- Changes from the prior reporting period.

## Stakeholder information

- Executive sponsor engagement.
- Key stakeholder groups.
- Stakeholder needs and expected outcomes.
- Current support or concerns.
- Affected populations.
- Operational ownership.
- Adoption and change readiness.
- Stakeholder feedback.
- Evidence that current execution still addresses stakeholder needs.

## Governance and obligation information

- Required laws, regulations, policies, standards, contracts, or commitments.
- Security, privacy, clinical, legal, financial, procurement, architecture, records-management, labor, accessibility, or sustainability requirements.
- Governance bodies and decision rights.
- Required approvals.
- Mandatory versus discretionary components.
- Exceptions and unresolved conditions.

## Portfolio context

- Related initiatives.
- Duplicate or overlapping work.
- Shared capabilities.
- Competing initiatives.
- Cross-initiative dependencies.
- Portfolio priorities.
- Funding and capacity constraints.
- Opportunities to consolidate, coordinate, or stop work.
- Relative value and urgency.

## Strategic change information

- New leadership priorities.
- New board or governance decisions.
- Budget changes.
- Regulatory or policy changes.
- Technology changes.
- Stakeholder or market changes.
- Organizational restructuring.
- New risks or constraints.
- Changes that may alter the initiative’s relevance.

## Data-quality inputs

- Source documents and owners.
- Reporting cutoff date.
- Missing objectives or metrics.
- Unclear strategy mappings.
- Conflicting strategic interpretations.
- Unsupported benefit claims.
- Stale strategy or initiative information.
- Unverified alignment assertions.
- Confidence level.
- Items requiring human validation.
