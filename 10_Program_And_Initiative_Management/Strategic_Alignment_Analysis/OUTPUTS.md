# Outputs

## Executive alignment summary

* Initiative name and purpose.
* Strategic objectives supported.
* Overall alignment assessment:

  * Strongly aligned.
  * Aligned.
  * Partially aligned.
  * Weakly aligned.
  * Misaligned.
  * Insufficient evidence.
* Most important alignment strength.
* Most material alignment gap.
* Relevant change in organizational strategy or priorities.
* Recommended leadership action.
* Confidence level and source summary.

## Strategy-to-execution traceability

Show the complete alignment chain:

Strategic priority
→ Initiative objective
→ Key result or success measure
→ Deliverable
→ Resource investment
→ Observed outcome
→ Organizational benefit

For each connection, provide:

* Description of the relationship.
* Supporting evidence.
* Accountable owner.
* Current status.
* Strength of alignment.
* Confidence level.
* Missing evidence or validation needed.

## Alignment-dimension assessment

Assess the initiative across:

* Mission alignment.
* Strategic-priority alignment.
* Objective alignment.
* Scope alignment.
* Deliverable alignment.
* Outcome and benefit alignment.
* Resource alignment.
* Stakeholder alignment.
* Governance and obligation alignment.
* Portfolio alignment.
* Current-environment alignment.

For each dimension, provide:

* Rating.
* Supporting evidence.
* Identified gap.
* Effect of the gap.
* Recommended action.
* Confidence level.

## Objective and deliverable contribution analysis

For each initiative objective and deliverable:

* Strategic priority supported.
* Expected contribution.
* Evidence of current contribution.
* Importance to the initiative outcome.
* Cost and resource requirements.
* Dependencies.
* Redundancy or conflict with other work.
* Recommended disposition:

  * Retain.
  * Strengthen.
  * Clarify.
  * Rescope.
  * Consolidate.
  * Remove.
  * Replace.
* Rationale for the recommendation.

## Alignment scorecard

When an approved scoring method exists, provide:

* Criterion.
* Criterion definition.
* Weight.
* Score.
* Weighted score.
* Supporting evidence.
* Confidence level.
* Reviewer adjustment, when authorized.
* Rationale for any adjustment.

Potential criteria include:

* Strategic relevance.
* Directness of contribution.
* Expected outcome value.
* Evidence of impact.
* Stakeholder value.
* Mandatory obligation.
* Resource proportionality.
* Portfolio fit.
* Future relevance.

The scoring method must be visible and treated as decision support rather than objective certainty.

## Alignment gaps

Identify:

* Objectives not connected to an approved strategic priority.
* Deliverables not connected to an initiative objective.
* Measures that do not demonstrate strategic outcomes.
* Resources invested in low-contribution work.
* Expected benefits without supporting evidence.
* Scope that expanded beyond the approved strategic purpose.
* Mandatory requirements not adequately addressed.
* Stakeholder needs no longer reflected in execution.
* Strategic priorities without sufficient initiative support.
* Contradictions between initiative activities and organizational strategy.

## Duplication and conflict analysis

Identify:

* Overlapping initiatives.
* Duplicate deliverables.
* Competing outcomes.
* Shared-resource conflicts.
* Conflicting targets.
* Incompatible technology or operating approaches.
* Opportunities to consolidate work.
* Priority conflicts requiring executive resolution.

## Strategic-change impact

Identify:

* Strategic changes since the initiative was approved.
* Elements that remain relevant.
* Elements that have become less relevant.
* New obligations or opportunities.
* Scope or outcome changes required.
* Effect on benefits, resources, schedule, and risk.
* Required reauthorization or governance review.

## Recommended initiative disposition

Recommend one of the following:

* Continue unchanged.
* Continue with stronger measurement.
* Accelerate.
* Rescope.
* Consolidate.
* Reprioritize.
* Sequence later.
* Pause.
* Stop.
* Revalidate after obtaining additional evidence.

For the recommendation, provide:

* Rationale.
* Expected benefit.
* Cost and resource implications.
* Risks and trade-offs.
* Required decision-maker.
* Required decision date.
* Immediate next steps.
* Next review point.

## Executive decision package

* Alignment decision required.
* Recommended disposition.
* Viable alternatives.
* Benefits and trade-offs of each alternative.
* Consequence of maintaining the current course.
* Consequence of changing direction.
* Required approvals.
* Follow-up actions.
* Owners and deadlines.

## Data-quality and confidence report

Identify:

* Unsupported strategic mappings.
* Missing outcome evidence.
* Stale strategy or initiative information.
* Conflicting interpretations.
* Weak outcome attribution.
* Incomplete cost or benefit information.
* Assumptions requiring confirmation.
* Specialized reviews required before decision-making.
