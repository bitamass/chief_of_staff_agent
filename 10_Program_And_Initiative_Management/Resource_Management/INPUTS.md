# Inputs

## Planning context

- Organization, portfolio, program, initiative, project, or operational area.
- Planning period.
- Reporting cutoff date.
- Strategic priorities.
- Approved initiatives and workstreams.
- Major milestones and decision dates.
- Resource-planning assumptions.
- Budget and staffing constraints.
- Required service or operational commitments.
- Executive sponsor.
- Resource-planning owner.
- Previous resource plan and forecast.

## Resource inventory

For each available resource:

- Resource ID.
- Person, role, team, vendor, system, facility, equipment, or budget category.
- Resource type:
  - Leadership.
  - Program or project management.
  - Business.
  - Clinical.
  - Research.
  - Technical.
  - Data and analytics.
  - Security, privacy, legal, finance, procurement, or governance.
  - Change management and communications.
  - Vendor or partner.
  - Technology environment.
  - Equipment or facility.
  - Funding.
- Organizational unit.
- Manager or resource owner.
- Skills and qualifications.
- Location and time zone.
- Availability.
- Cost or rate.
- Employment, contract, or service period.
- Current assignments.
- Capacity limitations.
- Access, licensing, certification, or authorization requirements.
- Backup or substitute resource.
- Source and last update.

## Demand information

For each initiative, workstream, deliverable, or operational commitment:

- Resource type required.
- Role or skill required.
- Quantity or level of effort.
- Planned start and end dates.
- Required allocation.
- Priority.
- Criticality.
- Location or scheduling requirement.
- Named-resource requirement, when justified.
- Minimum acceptable expertise.
- Onboarding or ramp-up time.
- Funding source.
- Initiative owner.
- Demand confidence.
- Consequence if the resource is unavailable.

## Current allocation data

For each resource assignment:

- Resource.
- Initiative or operational responsibility.
- Role.
- Start and end dates.
- Planned allocation.
- Actual or current allocation.
- Remaining capacity.
- Assignment owner.
- Critical deliverables and milestones.
- Competing commitments.
- Overtime or overload indicators.
- Underutilization indicators.
- Approved changes.
- Last validation date.

## Capacity data

- Standard available hours or capacity.
- Meetings and administrative load.
- Operational or support obligations.
- Leave and unavailable periods.
- Training and onboarding.
- Maintenance or downtime.
- Productivity assumptions.
- Maximum sustainable allocation.
- Contingency capacity.
- Expected attrition or contract end.
- Capacity by week, month, or planning period.

## Skills and capability data

- Required capability.
- Available capability.
- Proficiency or experience level.
- Number of qualified resources.
- Single-person or single-vendor dependency.
- Training opportunity.
- Time to develop the capability.
- Hiring or contracting lead time.
- Succession or backup coverage.
- Skills likely to become constrained.
- Skills no longer required.

## Financial information

- Approved resource budget.
- Actual spending.
- Committed spending.
- Forecast.
- Internal labor cost.
- Contractor or vendor cost.
- Hiring cost.
- Technology, license, facility, or equipment cost.
- Budget variance.
- Funding restrictions.
- Cost-center or funding source.
- Financial assumptions.
- Required approval authority.

## Dependency and timing data

- Deliverables and milestones dependent on each resource.
- Critical-path assignments.
- Shared-resource dependencies.
- Sequential work.
- Decision and approval dependencies.
- Vendor or procurement lead times.
- Hiring and onboarding lead times.
- Technology-environment availability.
- Required-by dates.
- Contingency options.

## Performance and health data

- Assignment progress.
- Milestone reliability.
- Quality or rework indicators.
- Resource-owner concerns.
- Burnout or sustainability signals, when appropriately and lawfully collected.
- Turnover or continuity risks.
- Vendor performance.
- Skill or capacity gaps.
- Forecast confidence.
- Operational impact of resource constraints.

## Scenario and priority inputs

- Portfolio priorities.
- Mandatory obligations.
- Initiatives to accelerate.
- Initiatives that may be deferred, paused, consolidated, rescoped, or stopped.
- Budget scenarios.
- Hiring constraints.
- Vendor alternatives.
- Shared-service constraints.
- Minimum operational coverage.
- Executive decision criteria.

## Governance and constraint inputs

- Labor agreements.
- Employment policies.
- Contractor limitations.
- Procurement rules.
- Budget authority.
- Clinical, security, privacy, regulatory, certification, or access requirements.
- Segregation-of-duty requirements.
- Work-location requirements.
- Accessibility and accommodation requirements.
- Confidentiality and personnel-data restrictions.

## Data-quality information

- Source systems and owners.
- Reporting period.
- Data freshness.
- Missing allocations.
- Allocations exceeding credible capacity.
- Conflicting staffing plans.
- Unverified skill assessments.
- Stale contractor or vendor information.
- Inconsistent cost methods.
- Confidence level.
- Items requiring management or HR validation.
