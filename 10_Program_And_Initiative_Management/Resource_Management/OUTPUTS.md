# Outputs

## Resource plan

For each initiative or workstream:

- Required roles and capabilities.
- Required allocation.
- Planned start and end dates.
- Assigned resources.
- Current allocation.
- Capacity gap.
- Funding status.
- Critical milestones supported.
- Dependencies.
- Resource risks.
- Required decision or action.
- Source and confidence.

## Capacity view

For each resource, role, team, or capability:

- Total available capacity.
- Allocated capacity.
- Remaining capacity.
- Confirmed future demand.
- Forecast demand.
- Overallocated periods.
- Underutilized periods.
- Operational or support commitments.
- Maximum sustainable allocation.
- Data-confidence indicator.

## Supply-and-demand analysis

- Available supply by role and capability.
- Demand by initiative and period.
- Capacity surplus or shortage.
- Timing of gaps.
- Skills with insufficient coverage.
- Shared-resource conflicts.
- Single points of failure.
- Hiring, contracting, training, or sequencing needs.

## Allocation conflict report

For each material conflict:

- Resource or capability.
- Competing initiatives.
- Current priority.
- Requested allocation.
- Available capacity.
- Milestones affected.
- Consequence of delay.
- Allocation or sequencing options.
- Trade-offs.
- Authorized decision-maker.
- Required decision date.

## Skills-gap analysis

- Required skill.
- Current availability.
- Number of qualified resources.
- Gap.
- Time period needed.
- Development, hiring, contracting, vendor, or partnership options.
- Cost.
- Lead time.
- Risks.
- Recommended response.

## Key-person and continuity analysis

- Critical resources with no backup.
- Roles concentrated in one individual, team, or vendor.
- Contract or availability end dates.
- Succession or backup coverage.
- Knowledge-transfer needs.
- Recommended continuity actions.
- Milestones or operations at risk.

## Financial resource view

- Approved budget.
- Actual and forecast resource spending.
- Variance.
- Internal versus external resource mix.
- Cost of proposed staffing scenarios.
- Funding gaps.
- Cost implications of delay.
- Required budget approvals.

## Resource scenario analysis

Compare scenarios such as:

- Current allocation.
- Strategy-priority allocation.
- Mandatory-work-first.
- Reduced-budget.
- Accelerated delivery.
- Hiring-constrained.
- Vendor-supported.
- Cross-training and internal-redeployment.

For each scenario:

- Work supported.
- Work delayed or stopped.
- Staffing and skills required.
- Cost.
- Time to implement.
- Risks.
- Sustainability.
- Trade-offs.

## Recommended resource actions

- Maintain allocation.
- Rebalance.
- Sequence work.
- Add temporary capacity.
- Hire.
- Contract.
- Engage a vendor or partner.
- Cross-train.
- Reduce scope.
- Defer or pause work.
- Create backup coverage.
- Resolve a priority conflict.

For each recommendation:

- Resource or role.
- Action.
- Owner.
- Required approval.
- Target date.
- Expected effect.
- Cost and risk.
- Supporting evidence.

## Executive attention

- Critical capacity shortages.
- Conflicting strategic priorities.
- Resource or budget decisions.
- Single points of failure.
- Unsustainable allocation.
- Hiring or vendor decisions.
- Initiatives that cannot be staffed under current constraints.
- Required decision-maker and deadline.

## Data-quality and validation summary

- Missing assignments.
- Implausible allocations.
- Stale capacity information.
- Conflicting plans.
- Unverified skill classifications.
- Incomplete cost information.
- Assumptions requiring HR, finance, procurement, or management validation.
