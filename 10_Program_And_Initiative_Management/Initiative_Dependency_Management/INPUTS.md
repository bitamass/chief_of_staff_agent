# Inputs

## Initiative context

For each initiative:

- Initiative ID and name.
- Objective and expected outcome.
- Strategic priority supported.
- Executive sponsor.
- Accountable owner.
- Program or project manager.
- Current phase and status.
- Planned start and completion dates.
- Major deliverables and milestones.
- Critical decisions.
- Resource requirements.
- Relevant risks and issues.
- Governance and approval requirements.
- Source charter, roadmap, or plan.

## Dependency definition

For each dependency:

- Dependency ID and description.
- Requesting or dependent initiative.
- Providing initiative, team, vendor, system, or external party.
- Required input, deliverable, decision, resource, capability, or approval.
- Dependency type:
  - Deliverable.
  - Decision.
  - Resource.
  - Funding.
  - Technology.
  - Integration.
  - Data.
  - Vendor.
  - Procurement.
  - Staffing or expertise.
  - Governance or approval.
  - Security, privacy, legal, clinical, or regulatory.
  - Operational readiness.
  - Communication or stakeholder.
- Dependency owner.
- Provider owner.
- Date identified.
- Required-by date.
- Current forecast date.
- Status.
- Criticality.
- Source and last update date.

## Dependency relationship data

- Upstream initiative or provider.
- Downstream initiative or consumer.
- Predecessor activity.
- Successor activity.
- Deliverable, decision, or milestone affected.
- Sequence requirements.
- Lead and lag time.
- Whether the dependency is mandatory or optional.
- Whether a partial input is usable.
- Whether alternatives exist.
- Whether multiple initiatives depend on the same input.
- Whether the dependency creates a single point of failure.

## Readiness and acceptance data

- Definition of ready.
- Required quality and completeness.
- Acceptance criteria.
- Required reviewer or approver.
- Testing or validation requirements.
- Documentation requirements.
- Handoff process.
- Operational-readiness requirements.
- Evidence required to confirm fulfillment.
- Acceptance or rejection status.

## Schedule information

- Baseline required-by date.
- Current forecast.
- Actual completion date, when fulfilled.
- Time remaining.
- Schedule variance.
- Critical-path status.
- Contingency date.
- Downstream milestones affected.
- Review or approval duration.
- Recovery timeline.
- Approved date changes.

## Ownership and governance data

- Requesting owner.
- Providing owner.
- Accountable sponsor.
- Escalation owner.
- Decision authority.
- Relevant RACI assignments.
- Governance forum.
- Service-level or contractual expectation.
- Required follow-up cadence.
- Dispute-resolution and escalation path.

## Risk and impact data

- Probability the dependency will not be met.
- Impact if it is late, incomplete, or rejected.
- Initiatives, deliverables, decisions, or operations affected.
- Schedule impact.
- Cost or resource impact.
- Strategic or outcome impact.
- Operational, clinical, security, privacy, legal, regulatory, or reputational impact.
- Workaround or contingency.
- Residual exposure.
- Early-warning indicators and trigger thresholds.

## Follow-up history

- Previous status requests.
- Provider responses.
- Decisions made.
- Revised dates.
- Recovery commitments.
- Escalations.
- Support or resources requested.
- Issues preventing delivery.
- Changes in scope or acceptance criteria.

## Portfolio inputs

- Integrated portfolio roadmap.
- Cross-initiative milestone plan.
- Shared-resource plan.
- Technology and data roadmaps.
- Procurement and vendor schedules.
- Governance and approval calendar.
- Decision log.
- Risk and issue register.
- Action and commitment tracker.
- Previous dependency report.

## Data-quality inputs

- Source owner.
- Last validation date.
- Missing owners or dates.
- Conflicting forecasts.
- Duplicate dependencies.
- Dependencies recorded only in meeting notes.
- Dependency status without evidence.
- Confidence level.
- Items requiring human confirmation.
