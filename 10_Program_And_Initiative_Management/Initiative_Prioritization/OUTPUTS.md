## Priority tiers

Suggested tiers:

- **Tier 1 — Commit now:** strategically important, time-sensitive, sufficiently ready, and supportable.
- **Tier 2 — Prepare or sequence:** valuable but dependent on readiness, funding, decisions, or capacity.
- **Tier 3 — Monitor or defer:** lower urgency or value relative to current constraints.
- **Tier 4 — Reconsider:** weak alignment, insufficient evidence, disproportionate cost or risk, duplication, or obsolete need.
- **Mandatory:** required by law, regulation, contract, safety, continuity, or an authorized commitment and evaluated separately from discretionary work.
