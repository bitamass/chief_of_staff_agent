# Inputs

## OKR cycle and framework

- Organization, portfolio, program, initiative, or team in scope.
- OKR cycle and reporting period.
- Strategic planning period.
- Approved OKR methodology.
- Approved scoring and status definitions.
- Reporting cadence.
- Reporting cutoff date.
- Intended audience.
- Previous OKR report or check-in.
- OKR program owner.
- Required review and approval process.

## Strategic context

- Mission and strategic priorities.
- Annual and multiyear goals.
- Executive commitments.
- Board or leadership priorities.
- Relevant operating-plan objectives.
- Resource and investment priorities.
- Material changes in strategy or operating conditions.
- Mandatory commitments and constraints.

## Objective information

For each objective:

- Objective ID and title.
- Qualitative description.
- Strategic priority supported.
- Rationale.
- Executive sponsor.
- Accountable owner.
- Supporting teams.
- Start and end dates.
- Intended outcome.
- Scope and exclusions.
- Related objectives.
- Current status.
- Confidence level.
- Last update.
- Source and approval status.

## Key-result information

For each key result:

- Key-result ID and description.
- Parent objective.
- Accountable owner.
- Supporting contributors.
- Metric definition.
- Calculation formula.
- Unit of measure.
- Baseline.
- Target.
- Current value.
- Previous-period value.
- Forecast.
- Direction of favorable performance.
- Start and end dates.
- Reporting frequency.
- Data source.
- Data owner.
- Current score.
- Status.
- Confidence.
- Last updated date.
- Evidence and known limitations.

## Progress history

- Values from prior reporting periods.
- Previous scores.
- Previous status.
- Previous confidence assessment.
- Milestones completed.
- Forecast changes.
- Owner commentary.
- Changes in assumptions.
- Reasons for material change.
- Corrections or restatements.

## Supporting initiative information

For each initiative supporting an OKR:

- Initiative name.
- Owner and sponsor.
- Objective or key result supported.
- Expected contribution.
- Current status.
- Major deliverables and milestones.
- Cost and resources.
- Risks and issues.
- Dependencies.
- Decisions and actions.
- Evidence that the initiative influences the key result.
- Attribution limitations.

## Outcome and benefit evidence

- Measurable outcome achieved.
- Adoption or utilization.
- Quality or service improvement.
- Operational or financial impact.
- Clinical, research, workforce, customer, student, or stakeholder outcome.
- Baseline and target.
- Measurement method.
- Evidence source.
- Timing of expected benefits.
- Benefits owner.
- Confidence and attribution limitations.

## Risk, issue, and dependency information

- Risks affecting the objective or key result.
- Issues already affecting performance.
- Probability, impact, severity, urgency, and trend.
- Mitigation or resolution.
- Owner and target date.
- Resource constraints.
- Cross-team dependencies.
- Data or technology dependencies.
- Vendor, procurement, governance, approval, or policy dependencies.
- Escalation status.

## Decision, action, and commitment information

- Decisions made.
- Decisions pending.
- Decision-maker.
- Required decision date.
- Open actions.
- Overdue actions.
- Commitments.
- Owners and due dates.
- Consequences of delay.
- Required leadership intervention.

## Alignment information

- Objective-to-strategy mapping.
- Key-result-to-objective mapping.
- Initiative-to-key-result mapping.
- Shared key results.
- Duplicate objectives.
- Conflicting targets.
- Objectives without measurable key results.
- Key results without supporting initiatives.
- Initiatives without clear OKR alignment.
- Resource conflicts across objectives.

## Data-quality information

- System or document of record.
- Data steward.
- Data freshness.
- Completeness.
- Accuracy.
- Reconciliation status.
- Metric-definition consistency.
- Manual adjustments.
- Missing baselines or targets.
- Unsupported scores or forecasts.
- Conflicting owner reports.
- Confidence level.
- Items requiring validation.
