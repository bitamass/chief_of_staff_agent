# Outputs

## OKR reporting summary

- OKR cycle and reporting cutoff date.
- Objectives and key results in scope.
- Objectives on track, watch, at risk, or off track.
- Key results achieved.
- Most significant progress.
- Most material performance gap.
- Most important change since the previous check-in.
- Highest-priority risk or dependency.
- Leadership decisions or support required.
- Overall data-confidence statement.

## Objective status report

For each objective:

- Objective title.
- Strategic priority supported.
- Sponsor and owner.
- Overall status.
- Confidence of achievement.
- Key-result summary.
- Progress and trajectory.
- Change from previous report.
- Supporting initiatives.
- Material risks, issues, and dependencies.
- Required corrective action.
- Leadership attention.
- Sources and last update.

## Key-result status report

For each key result:

- Description.
- Owner.
- Baseline.
- Target.
- Current value.
- Previous value.
- Forecast.
- Progress.
- Score.
- Status.
- Confidence.
- Trend.
- Data source and freshness.
- Supporting initiatives.
- Risks and dependencies.
- Next checkpoint.

## Progress-over-time analysis

- Percentage of the OKR cycle elapsed.
- Expected progress.
- Actual progress.
- Current trajectory.
- Forecast likelihood of achievement.
- Front-loaded, back-loaded, or milestone-based assumptions.
- Material variance.
- Explanation of variance.
- Recovery or improvement plan.

## Changes since the previous report

- New or revised objectives.
- New or revised key results.
- Metric changes.
- Baseline or target changes.
- Score changes.
- Confidence changes.
- Forecast changes.
- New and resolved risks.
- Initiative changes.
- Resource or dependency changes.
- Decisions made.
- Corrections or restatements.
- Required approval for changes.

## Outcome versus activity analysis

- Outcomes achieved.
- Outputs delivered.
- Activities completed.
- Key results primarily measuring activity.
- Results without sufficient outcome evidence.
- Initiatives completed without demonstrated effect.
- Benefits not yet realized.
- Attribution limitations.

## Alignment and coverage report

- Objectives mapped to strategy.
- Key results mapped to objectives.
- Initiatives mapped to key results.
- Strategic priorities lacking measurable objectives.
- Objectives without sufficient initiatives.
- Initiatives without clear OKR contribution.
- Duplicate or overlapping work.
- Conflicting targets.
- Resource conflicts.

## Risk and dependency report

- Objectives and key results at risk.
- Material blockers.
- Shared dependencies.
- Resource constraints.
- Pending approvals or decisions.
- Mitigation and recovery status.
- Owner and target date.
- Escalation required.

## Confidence report

For each objective and key result:

- Confidence rating.
- Supporting evidence.
- Key assumptions.
- Conditions required for success.
- Factors that could increase or reduce confidence.
- Data-quality effect.
- Difference between current score and forecast confidence.

## Leadership attention

- Decisions required.
- Resource allocation.
- Priority conflicts.
- Objectives requiring clarification.
- Key results requiring improved measurement.
- Initiatives to accelerate, pause, consolidate, rescope, or stop.
- Target or baseline changes requiring approval.
- Required decision-maker and decision date.

## Next-cycle actions

- Follow-up action.
- Owner.
- Due date.
- Expected evidence.
- Next check-in date.
- Escalation trigger.
- Required stakeholder or governance participation.

## Data-quality and methodology report

- Missing baselines or targets.
- Stale metrics.
- Inconsistent formulas.
- Unsupported scoring.
- Manual adjustments.
- Conflicting status.
- Data-source limitations.
- Attribution uncertainty.
- Items requiring human confirmation.
