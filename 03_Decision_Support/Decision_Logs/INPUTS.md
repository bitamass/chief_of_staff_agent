# Inputs — Decision Logs

## Required decision evidence

* Approved meeting minutes.
* Meeting notes, transcripts, or summaries.
* Written approvals, resolutions, directives, or executive correspondence.
* Decision documents, recommendations, and option analyses.
* Governance or approval records.
* Source title, date, owner, version, and location.
* Confirmation that the source is authorized for use.

## Required decision information

For each decision, collect:

* Clear decision statement.
* Decision date and effective date.
* Decision owner or authorized authority.
* Source meeting, document, or communication.
* Decision type:

  * Strategic.
  * Operational.
  * Financial.
  * Technical.
  * Clinical.
  * Governance.
  * Security or privacy.
  * Personnel or workforce.
  * Policy or compliance.
* Strategic goal, initiative, project, risk, or issue affected.
* Problem or question addressed.
* Current state or context.
* Options considered.
* Selected option or approved direction.
* Documented rationale.
* Evidence used.
* Assumptions.
* Risks and trade-offs accepted.
* Conditions, limitations, or exceptions.
* Stakeholders consulted, informed, or affected.
* Required implementation actions.
* Accountable owners and due dates.
* Dependencies.
* Expected outcome and success measures.
* Review, expiration, or reconsideration date.
* Confidentiality and distribution classification.

## Decision-authority information

Collect:

* Authorized decision-maker.
* Delegation or approval authority.
* Required quorum, consultation, or concurrence.
* Governance body or approval forum.
* Participants present.
* Required approvals obtained.
* Approvals or consultations still outstanding.
* Whether the decision is final, conditional, interim, or pending confirmation.

## Supporting analysis

* Alternatives and recommendations reviewed.
* Trade-off analysis.
* Financial and resource implications.
* Operational or clinical impact.
* Technical and architecture implications.
* Security, privacy, legal, regulatory, or compliance review.
* Stakeholder impact.
* Risk assessment.
* Implementation readiness.
* Evidence limitations and confidence.

## Related records

* Prior decisions concerning the same matter.
* Decisions superseded or modified.
* Related action items and commitments.
* Related risks, issues, dependencies, and milestones.
* Related policies, charters, contracts, or plans.
* Upcoming implementation or review meetings.

## Optional supporting inputs

* Executive briefing materials.
* Business cases.
* Stakeholder correspondence.
* Voting or concurrence records.
* Historical decision logs.
* Benefits-realization plans.
* Audit or assurance requirements.
* Communication plans.

## Input validation

Before creating a decision-log entry:

* Confirm that an authorized decision was actually made.
* Verify the exact decision wording, decision-maker, date, authority, and source.
* Distinguish the decision from a proposal, recommendation, preference, discussion, or action item.
* Confirm whether the decision is final, conditional, interim, or pending confirmation.
* Verify that required approvals, quorum, consultation, or concurrence occurred.
* Identify conditions, exceptions, assumptions, risks, and limitations attached to the decision.
* Identify required actions, owners, deadlines, and review dates.
* Check for prior decisions that may be modified, superseded, or contradicted.
* Flag conflicting, incomplete, outdated, inaccessible, or unverified evidence.
* Do not infer approval from silence, attendance, informal discussion, or work already underway.
