# Outputs — Decision Logs

## Decision-log summary

Provide a concise overview of:

* New decisions recorded.
* Conditional or interim decisions.
* Decisions awaiting confirmation or remaining approvals.
* Decisions modifying or superseding earlier direction.
* Decisions with missing owners, actions, measures, or review dates.
* Records requiring leadership or governance verification.

## Decision-log entry

Create one entry for each confirmed decision.

### Decision identification

* Decision identifier.
* Decision title.
* Clear decision statement.
* Decision type.
* Decision date.
* Effective date.
* Decision owner or authorized authority.
* Governance body or approval forum.
* Source meeting, document, or communication.
* Confidentiality and distribution classification.

### Decision context

* Problem or question addressed.
* Strategic goal, initiative, project, risk, or issue affected.
* Reason the decision was required.
* Current-state context.
* Decision deadline or time sensitivity.
* Consequence of no decision.

### Alternatives considered

For each documented alternative, provide:

* Option.
* Benefits.
* Costs.
* Risks and trade-offs.
* Dependencies.
* Evidence considered.
* Reason selected or not selected, when documented.

### Approved direction

Provide:

* Selected option or authorized direction.
* Documented rationale.
* Conditions, limitations, or exceptions.
* Assumptions.
* Risks accepted.
* Matters explicitly excluded or deferred.
* Whether the decision is final, conditional, interim, or pending confirmation.

### Authority and consultation

Provide:

* Authorized decision-maker.
* Basis of authority.
* Required quorum or concurrence.
* Required approvals obtained.
* Approvals still outstanding.
* Stakeholders consulted.
* Stakeholders to be informed.
* Known dissenting or alternative positions, when documented.

### Implementation requirements

For each required action, provide:

* Action.
* Accountable owner.
* Due date.
* Dependency.
* Completion evidence.
* Related milestone or meeting.
* Consequence of delay.

### Expected outcomes and measures

Provide:

* Expected outcome.
* Success measures.
* Measurement owner.
* Review date.
* Expiration date, if applicable.
* Conditions that would trigger reconsideration.

### Related records

Identify:

* Prior related decisions.
* Decisions modified or superseded.
* Related actions and commitments.
* Related risks and issues.
* Related policies, charters, plans, or agreements.
* Related source documents.

### Evidence and traceability

For each material element, provide:

* Source title.
* Source owner.
* Source date and version.
* Relevant section or context.
* Confidence level: high, medium, or low.
* Evidence limitations.

## Record completeness status

Classify each decision-log entry as:

* **Complete:** Authority, wording, rationale, conditions, actions, and source are verified.
* **Complete with minor gaps:** Decision is confirmed, but nonmaterial details remain incomplete.
* **Incomplete:** Material implementation, rationale, measure, or review information is missing.
* **Pending confirmation:** Decision wording, authority, approval, or source has not been verified.

State what is required to complete the record.

## Human-review requirement

The authorized decision-maker, meeting owner, or governance record owner must verify the decision wording, authority, conditions, rationale, actions, and source before the entry is treated as an official decision record.
